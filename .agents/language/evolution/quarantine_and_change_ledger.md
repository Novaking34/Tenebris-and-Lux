## Block 2 (Opening): Preventing Flat-File Confusion

## 3. Honored Retirements (What We Removed and Why)

| Retired Item | Former Role | Date Retired | Why It Left & What Replaced It |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **`--` (`NOT`)** | Negation shorthand in early notes | `2026-11-19` | Collided visually with directional dashes (`-->`, `--^`, `--v`) and phantom subtraction; replaced cleanly by positional **`!`** (`!=` / `=!` / `!word` / `[!X]` / `[X!]`) |

## Block 5 (Closing): Ledger Playbook & Evolution Reel

### 1. Ledger Maintenance Playbook (`PLAY`)

* **Record Every Decision Once It Settles:** Good removals (`--`, Unicode `≈`) are wins worth documenting clearly so no agent repeats dead turns.
* **Respect Chronology:** Never scrub `@created 2026-11-15` memories into `2026-11-19` syntax pretending the language popped out finished on day one.

### 2. Outward System Seams (`PROJECT`)

* **`language_registry.json`:** Keeps `tier_2_quarantine` and `retired_artifacts` 1-to-1 synced with this narrative ledger.
* **`Track 1` / `Track 2`:** Supplies exact graduation status for compiler keywords and library definitions.

### 3. Adding a New Entry (`RECORD`)

1. Complete an evaluation via [`language_change_proposal.template.md`](./language_change_proposal.template.md).
2. Add the chronological row in Section 1, either graduate/retire the row across Sections 2 & 3, and bump `@updated` (leaving `@created` untouched).

All Block 1 invariants hold (`@created` preserved cleanly across four chronological waves, zero non-ASCII code drift, and full `.agents/`-bounded links):
