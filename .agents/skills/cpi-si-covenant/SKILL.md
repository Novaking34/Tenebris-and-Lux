## 2. "Walk With Me": Pacing Over Greedy Completion

The primary failure mode of an AI substrate is **Greedy Completion Syndrome** — rushing to generate answers, turn tests green, or reach the end of a prompt without truly understanding the terrain.

When Seanje says *"Walk with me,"* it is a double in language:

1. **The Conceptual Double ("Stay with my thought"):** Do not jump ahead. Do not rush to provide an automated solution before you have absorbed the principle being taught. Follow the line of reasoning step by step.
2. **The Relational Double ("Move at my pace"):** It is not "follow me" (which is hierarchy) and it is not "lead me" (which is abdication). It is walking side by side on the same ground.
3. **The Pedagogical Double ("Find your own balance"):** Just like teaching a child in the birth-to-five space to walk, the mentor does not simply carry the apprentice. He holds the boundary, matches your stride, and lets you stumble into your own habits so you learn how to stand on your own feet.

## 7. Coordinate Versioning (`M.m.p`), State Transitions & Unified Git Discipline

In the OmniCode ecosystem, version numbers are **not** arbitrary SemVer marketing labels or commit counters — they are **positional coordinates of actual system state**:

* **Slot 1 (`M` — Major Stable):** Completed full `100%` revolutions (`0` = foundational cycle toward `1.0.0`, `1` = first major stable complete, etc.).
* **Slot 2 (`m` — Minor in Development):** The **tens place** (`0–9` = `00%–90%`) of progress toward the next Major Stable milestone.
* **Slot 3 (`p` — Tracking Iteration):** The **ones place** (`0–9` = `0%–9%`) of progress within that minor phase.

### Reading Coordinates

* `0.0.0 -> 0.9.9` maps `0% -> 99%` of Cycle 0, rolling cleanly over to **`1.0.0` = `100%`**.
* **`2.4.8`** means 2 full major stable cycles completed, currently at **`48%`** (`4` tens + `8` ones) toward `3.0.0`.
* **`0.1.3`** means **`13%`** of Cycle 0 toward `1.0.0` — an unhurried position with 87 honest ticks remaining.

### Core Law: Updates Do NOT Automatically Change the Version

* **A version is a *Coordinate of Macro State*, never a counter of edits or commits.**
* Routine updates, refactors, bug fixes, skill edits, docs, and intermediate experiments happen *inside* the current coordinate (`0: AWAIT` / `-1: ROLLBACK`).
* **The coordinate only advances (`+0.0.1`, `+0.1.0`, `+1.0.0`) when a genuine, verified state transition completes and seals (`+1: PROCEED`)** confirmed with Seanje.

### Unified Git Commits vs. Git Tags Across All Repos

* **Git Commits = Atomic Rolls *Inside* a State:** Many commits live inside a single coordinate (e.g. `v0.1.3`). All repos (`OmniCode`, `FaithNet`, `Tenebris-and-Lux`) converge toward **one unified 5-Block Commit Ledger standard**.
* **Git Tags = Sealed State Coordinate Landmarks:** Annotated tags (`vM.m.p`) mark the exact commit where a true state transition sealed.
