> **Why Language and Lineage Live Together:** You cannot responsibly evolve a living language if you forget why its symbols were forged in the first place — and you cannot mine two years of ancestral files without protecting what must stay bedrock vs. what stays safely quarantined for trial (`SYNTAX.md -> .omni -> Prompt`).

## Block 2 (Opening): Evolve Without Calcifying, Harvest Without Leaking

OmniCode grammar grows deliberately along the **Generational Core-Anchored Growth Model**: core axioms stay solid (`tier_0_bedrock`), settled working `.omni`/`.tape` grammar carries active code (`tier_1_working`), and experimental domain verbs live cleanly as lowercase `<concept>` or `{context}` forms (`tier_2_quarantine`) until proven across multiple real modules.

Meanwhile, [`lineage/`](../../lineage/lineage_quarry_guide.md) preserves how those foundations emerged (`Oct 2024` bootloaders -> `Nov-Dec 2025` balanced ternary & 5-Block laws -> `Apr 2026` Assembly reset -> `v0.1.3` present), stopping agents from inventing broken "new" designs that were already solved cleanly years earlier.

## 1. Three Stability Tiers & Concept Quarantine (`language/`)

Governed by [`language_living_charter.md`](../../language/language_living_charter.md) and indexed in [`language_registry.json`](../../language/language_registry.json):

* **`tier_0_bedrock` (Sticky Bedrock):** Symbolic Assembly core — `T4` (`+1`, `0`, `-1`, `NULL`), `16` ontological borders, `14` directionals, Freestanding weights, and Five-Block lifecycle headers ([`SYNTAX.md`](../../language/reference/SYNTAX.md)). Never casually mutate Tier 0 symbols!
* **`tier_1_working` (Promoted Working Grammar):** Verified `.tape` -> `.omni` keywords documented cleanly in [`KEYWORD.md`](../../language/reference/KEYWORD.md).
* **`tier_2_quarantine` (Lowercase Trial Sandbox):** Emerging domain verbs remain `lowercase` inside `<concept>` / `{context}` blocks (recorded in [`quarantine_and_change_ledger.md`](../../language/evolution/quarantine_and_change_ledger.md)).

### The 5-Question Change Gate Before Any Grammar Promotion

Use [`language_change_proposal.template.md`](../../language/evolution/language_change_proposal.template.md) and confirm all five questions pass before hardening a trial word:
1. Does the concept fail cleanly with existing Tier 0 borders + Tier 1 words?
2. Is it proven across distinct tracks rather than single-file convenience?
3. Does it preserve zero-ambiguity ASCII parsing and `SYNTAX.md` round-trip symmetry?
4. Have alternative phrasings been weighed without colliding against existing weights?
5. Is the change explicitly recorded in `quarantine_and_change_ledger.md` and `language_registry.json`?

## 2. The Lossless Three-Rung Stack (`Assembly <-> Prose <-> Prompt`)

Demonstrated in [`manifest_omni_translation_scratchpad.md`](../../scratchpads/studies/manifest_omni_translation_scratchpad.md) and [`03_omnicode_abstraction_stack_assembly_to_prose.md`](../../lineage/proofs_and_bridges/03_omnicode_abstraction_stack_assembly_to_prose.md):

```text
  Level 3: Natural Human Prompt   (Plain English intent over proven axioms)
              ^
              |  lossless semantic round-trip
              v
  Level 2: Structured Prose .omni (Domain-readable clauses & named contracts)
              ^
              |  compiles deterministically
              v
  Level 1: Symbolic Assembly      (manifest.omni / SYNTAX.md 16 borders & T4)
              ^
              |  binds onto
              v
  Level 0: Ternary / Bare Silicon (libtrit, Tryte5, Cornerstone C23/MillenniumOS)
```

Whenever auditing or proposing `.omni` changes, check both directions: readable Assembly must lift into natural speech without guessing, and human prompts must map downward cleanly into unambiguous structure.
