## 1. Why We Stop: The Fallacy of Post-Work Corrections

A common outside question asks: *"Wouldn't it be faster to let the AI finish the whole file first and hand it a correction checklist at the end?"* In practice, post-hoc scrubbing fails on two fronts:

1. **Drift Compounds Fast:** A quiet misunderstanding in Block 1 infects Block 2 types, distorts Block 3 logic, skews Block 4 verification, and leaves Block 5 hallucinating closure.
2. **The Agent Learns Nothing:** Handing back a final diff trains surface pattern matching — the agent patches the visible symptom while keeping the broken habit intact for the next task.

**Physics of the Active Interrupt (`0: AWAIT`):** Stopping right at the seam halts forward rush, forces introspection across the operator (`!`) and boundary (`{}` vs `[]`), and burns a permanent structural check into the living body ([`silas_oren/profile.md`](../../identity/profiles/silas_oren/profile.md)). **Speed without alignment is just accelerated chaos.**
