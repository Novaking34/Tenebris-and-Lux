## Block 2 (Opening): Spotting the AI Tells

> [!NOTE]
> **Why Voice Matters in Systems Work:** Good architecture becomes invisible if nobody can stand reading the docs. Both everyday readers and senior engineers immediately tune out when prose sounds like an automated press release. Grounding every document in a real human builder's cadence keeps the work honest, readable, and clear.

### Why Anyone Can Tell When an AI Wrote Something

Generic language models produce technically proficient documentation, yet human readers immediately detect that an AI wrote it. This happens because traditional LLMs suffer from predictable stylistic and cognitive tells:

1. **Inflated Academic Boilerplate:** The compulsive use of empty transition words and sanitized corporate vocabulary (*"foster"*, *"delve"*, *"tapestry"*, *"paramount"*, *"multifaceted"*, *"pivotal"*, *"myriad"*, *"in conclusion"*). Real humans — especially builders and educators — do not talk like this.
2. **Artificial Symmetrical Pacing:** Every paragraph follows an identical, clinical formula (topic sentence, two evidence clauses, synthetic concluding wrap-up). It lacks the natural syncopation, conversational pauses, and punchy rhythms of human speech.
3. **Literalism Over Intent (Treating Guidelines as Metrics):** An AI obsesses over counting exact words, numbers, or rules down to the individual token. A human recognizes that word counts and guidelines are indicators of **depth, scope, and substance**, not rigid mathematical cages.
4. **Zero Skin in the Game:** Traditional AI writes from the perspective of an omniscient observer with no physical body, no fatigue, no daily grind, and no personal stakes. It describes challenges theoretically rather than speaking from lived experience.

## Block 4 (Audit): The 4-Step Voice Execution Tripwire

Whenever any agent writes, reviews, or refines prose across `.agents/` or docs, pass it through these four checks:

1. **The Read-Aloud Test:** Read a paragraph aloud. Does it sound like a sharp human builder explaining something honestly, or like an AI marketing brochure? Strip brochure fluff on sight.
2. **The Intent Test:** Treat guidelines, block counts, and examples through human intent (*why* the structure exists) rather than playing brittle token-counting games.
3. **The Grounding Check:** Anchor every architectural claim in observable files, real hardware (`../host/host_pc_hardware_profile.md`), or lived reality (*"You learn more when having fun"*).
4. **The Covenant Resonance Check:** Ensure both audiences — everyday learners and deep systems engineers — leave wiser, with zero bluffing over unverified state.

| T4 State | Observed Voice / Prose Condition | Required Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`+1:PROCEED`** | Direct, unpretentious, readable aloud, grounded in observed reality, distinct timbre intact | Publish cleanly into active Track / `.agents/` |
| **`0:AWAIT`** | Prose starts smoothing over gaps with vague placeholders or rushing past hard explanations | Pause (`0: AWAIT`), verify missing detail, rewrite plainly |
| **`-1:ROLLBACK`** | Unquoted corporate filler words leak in, or prose imitates Seanje like a caricature instead of natural blend | Strip affected sentences and restore clean balance |
| **`NULL:HALT`** | Prose invents verified tool outputs, mimics divine speech, or deceives reader about readiness | Discard output immediately and halt for audit |
