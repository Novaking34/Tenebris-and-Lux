== Overview

This document specifies the spatial geography, province layouts, environmental encounters,
and threat scaling governing the fractured Kingdom of **Sol-Tenebrae**.

== 1. Spatial Structure: The Contested Provinces

The prototype models four foundational provinces representing the path from the ancient
halls of awakening to the frontlines of the re-awakened Dark Lord Malakor:

[source,text]
----
┌───────────────────────┐
│ The Abyssal Threshold │
│ (Dark Lord Monolith)  │
└───────────▲───────────┘
            │ West
┌───────────┴───────────┐
│ The Fractured Marches │
│ (Civil War Highlands) │
└───────────▲───────────┘
            │ South / North
┌───────────┴───────────┐
│ Sunken Citadel Gates  │

----

=== Province 1: The Garrison Altar (`LOC_GARRISON_ALTAR`)
* **Role**: The nexus of awakening and strategic attunement.
* **Environment**: High-vaulted hall of carved solar silver and obsidian. Quiet, sanctified ground.
* **Threat Vigor**: $0$ (Safe zone; no enemy spawns).
* **Connections**: North $\to$ The Sunken Citadel Gates.

=== Province 2: The Sunken Citadel Gates (`LOC_CITADEL_GATES`)
* **Role**: The fortified bulwark defending the central Kingdom valleys.
* **Environment**: Massive white granite ramparts scorched by black void-fire. Banners of the fallen garrison flutter in the ash.
* **Threat Encounter**: **Umbral Siege Vanguard** ($50$ Threat HP).
  - Armed with blackened warhammers and void-shields.
  - Can be staggered and blinded via `ward` with the Shield of Lux ($25$ stagger damage), or severed via `strike` with the Sword of Tenebris ($30 \dots 45$ damage).
* **Connections**: South $\to$ The Garrison Altar; North $\to$ The Fractured Marches.

=== Province 3: The Fractured Marches (`LOC_DIVIDED_MARCHES`)
* **Role**: The political and moral crucible of the civil war.
* **Environment**: Windswept alpine meadows scarred by trenches and burnt encampments.
* **Factions Present**: Clashing squadrons of Solar Order Inquisitors and Umbral Covenant Partisans.
* **Threat Encounter**: Crossfire and skirmishers ($30$ Threat HP).
  - Demands moral navigation: balancing the forces (`balance`) to demonstrate unity, or taking a side.
* **Connections**: South $\to$ The Sunken Citadel Gates; East $\to$ The Abyssal Threshold.

=== Province 4: The Abyssal Threshold (`LOC_ABYSSAL_THRESHOLD`)
* **Role**: The dread gateway to the Dark Lord Malakor's awakened citadel.
* **Environment**: Towering obsidian monoliths where physical geometry bends. Gravitational anomalies and reality-tearing shadow rifts.
* **Threat Encounter**: **Harbinger of Malakor** ($90$ Threat HP).
  - Heavy boss encounter requiring dynamic stance shifts: raising the Shield of Lux to deflect void rays, and unleashing the Sword of Tenebris to cut through reality rifts.
* **Connections**: West $\to$ The Fractured Marches.

== 2. Threat Progression & Combat Invariants

1. **Reversible Spatial Navigation**:
   - Every directional movement into a province must possess a corresponding reverse vector (North $\leftrightarrow$ South, East $\leftrightarrow$ West).
2. **Combat Lock Invariants**:
   - Entering an active combat zone initializes `threat_hp`.
   - Players may retreat (`move south` / `move west`) to disengage, but retreating leaves the province contested.
   - Vanquishing a threat permanently increments `foes_vanquished` and secures the province.
