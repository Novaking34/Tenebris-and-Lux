== Overview

This document specifies the spatial geography, province layouts, environmental encounters,
and tactical grid battlefields governing the **Kingdom of Sacyra** (pronounced *"saw-kee-ruh"*)
as Elijah Ro-enheart and Katrina Jaren defend the realm from Dark Lord Malakor.

=== 1. The Garrison Altar (`LOC_GARRISON_ALTAR`)
* **Role**: The ancestral redoubt of Sacyra's Royal Guard and the starting staging ground.
* **Environment**: High-vaulted halls of carved silver-white granite and polished obsidian pedestals where Elijah's father once held council.
* **Significance**: Here, Elijah Ro-enheart and Katrina Jaren first awaken the dormant Shield of Lux and Sword of Tenebris as reports of Malakor's vanguard arrive.
* **Grid Battlefield**: Smooth stone flags, defensive pillars providing $+15\%$ cover, training dummies, weapon racks.
* **Threat Vigor**: $0$ (Sanctified safe zone; tactical deployment and stance tutorial).
* **Connections**: North $\to$ Sunken Citadel Ramparts.

=== 2. Sunken Citadel Ramparts (`LOC_CITADEL_GATES`)
* **Role**: The primary fortified bastion guarding the central valleys of Sacyra.
* **Environment**: Colossal stone ramparts scorched by void-fire; fractured portcullises and smoking ballista batteries.
* **Encounter**: **Malakor's Void Vanguard Captain** ($50$ Threat HP) commanding shock troops and blighted footmen.
* **Tactical Grid Elements**:
  - Narrow stone choke-points where Elijah's *Royal Intercept* and Shield of Lux can hold off numerical superiority.
  - Rampart parapets offering Katrina elevated flanking routes and line-of-sight plunging strikes.
* **Connections**: South $\to$ Garrison Altar; North $\to$ Fractured Marches.

=== 3. The Fractured Marches (`LOC_DIVIDED_MARCHES`)
* **Role**: The windswept highlands along the Kingdom's frontier, home to forward outposts of the **Adventurers' Guild** and noble house estates.
* **Environment**: Rolling rocky hills, abandoned watchtowers, and misty ravines where royal army patrols and guild outriders clash with vanguard skirmishers.
* **Encounter**: **Corrupted Golem Vanguard & Shadow Stalkers** ($70$ Threat HP).
* **Tactical Grid Elements**:
  - Uneven terrain, forest thickets granting $+20\%$ evasion, and muddy ditches that slow heavy armor.
  - Katrina's knowledge of local guild ranger paths allows the party to surprise enemy artillery from the rear.
* **Connections**: South $\to$ Sunken Citadel Ramparts; West $\to$ Abyssal Threshold.

=== 4. The Abyssal Threshold (`LOC_DARK_LORD_THRESHOLD`)
* **Role**: The dark frontier where the fabric of reality bends into the subterranean vaults of Dark Lord Malakor.
* **Environment**: Jagged obsidian monoliths, boiling void-mist chasms, and anti-radiant storms.
* **Encounter**: **Dread Lord Vanguard Commander** ($90 \dots 100$ Threat HP).
* **Tactical Grid Elements**:
  - Void Mire tiles causing $-5$ HP per turn, demanding strict stance management and the Shield of Lux's purifying aura to cross safely.
  - Multi-tile boss presence demanding coordinated tandem strikes between Elijah and Katrina to shatter dread shields.
