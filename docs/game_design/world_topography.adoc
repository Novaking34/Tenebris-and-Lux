== Overview

This document specifies the authentic spatial geography, province layouts, environmental encounters,
and tactical grid battlefields governing the **Kingdom of Sacyra** (pronounced *"saw-kee-ruh"*, `/sɔːˈkiːrə/`)
and **The Dreamscape**, grounded in the recovered 2017–2018 demo baseline.
While **The Dark Lord** looms as the overarching existential threat in the background ("A Plot"),
the narrative begins within the ethereal trials of **The Dreamscape** before awakening into the pastoral
hamlet of **Dio Village**. Players may guide the canonical champions (**Elijah Ro-enheart** and
**Katrina Jaren**) or customize their chosen bearer's designation.

=== 1. The Dreamscape (`LOC_THE_DREAMSCAPE`)
* **Role**: The ethereal realm of ancient trials guided by Chrono where the illusion of the dream shatters.
* **Environment**: Floating crystal archways, shifting astral mist, and mechanism trial chambers.
* **Significance**: The hero awakens here to learn the fundamentals of combat and relic resonance.
  Dream Eaters gather under the distant influence of **The Dark Lord**.
* **Encounter**: **Dream Eater** ($70$ Threat HP, $11$ DEF, $8$ MDF, $70$ Flank Vigor).
* **Grid Battlefield**: Astral tiles, floating platforms, and ethereal leylines granting MP regeneration.
* **Connections**: North / Awaken $\to$ Dio Village.

=== 2. Dio Village (`LOC_DIO_VILLAGE`)
* **Role**: Pastoral ancestral hamlet in Sacyra; the true awakening point of the physical journey.
* **Environment**: Cozy stone cottages with thatched roofs, Grandma Magolia's welcoming hearth,
  Eric the lumberjack's timber yard, and the local general trading post.
* **Significance**: Here the hero recovers Grandma's Watch and the *Compass of Morality*, encountering
  village folk before venturing into the perilous wilds.
* **Threat Vigor**: $0$ (Sanctified village sanctuary; recovery, dialogue, and lore exchange).
* **Connections**: South $\to$ The Dreamscape (slumber meditation); North $\to$ Whispering Woods.

=== 3. Whispering Woods (`LOC_WHISPERING_WOODS`)
* **Role**: Dense ancient forest enveloping Dio Village, separating the hamlet from the kingdom's interior.
* **Environment**: Towering ironwood boughs, rustling autumn canopy, and hidden woodland trails.
* **Encounter**: **Umbral Wolf** ($85$ Threat HP, $14$ DEF, $10$ MDF, $60$ Flank Vigor).
* **Tactical Grid Elements**:
  - Forest thickets providing $+20\%$ evasion against ranged physical assault.
  - Tree root chokepoints where *Lux Barrio* shields flanking allies from pack ambushes.
* **Connections**: South $\to$ Dio Village; East / North $\to$ The Sacyran Marches.

=== 4. The Sacyran Marches (`LOC_SACYRA_MARCHES`)
* **Role**: The windswept frontier marches leading toward the Royal Court of King Jihad and the Capital.
* **Environment**: Craggy hills, fortified signal towers, and military encampments.
* **Encounter**: **Shadow Scout & Vanguard Patrol** ($110$ Threat HP, $18$ DEF, $14$ MDF, $80$ Flank Vigor).
* **Tactical Grid Elements**:
  - Trenches, palisades, and elevated ramparts where *Cuttatio Tenebris* cleaves through enemy formations.
  - Broad fields allowing full maneuverability of twin-relic equilibrium strikes.
* **Connections**: West / South $\to$ Whispering Woods.

== 2. The Overarching Menace: The Dark Lord

In the authentic 2017–2018 canonical script, the ultimate antagonist is designated strictly as **The Dark Lord**:
* He is never named "Malakor" or burdened with synthetic titles in canon.
* The Dark Lord represents the primordial threat looming in the distance ("The A Plot"), while regional
  intrigues—such as King Jihad's political maneuvers, Noble House power struggles, and the Adventurers' Guild
  missions—unfold on the active stage.
* The wielder's mastery of the Shield of Lux and Sword of Tenebris prepares them for this looming confrontation
  without rushing directly into the final battle.
