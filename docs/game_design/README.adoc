== Overview

This directory houses the foundational game design pillars, combat mechanics, campaign progression,
spatial topography, testing philosophy, and historical baseline records for *Tenebris and Lux*
in the **Kingdom of Sacyra** and **The Dreamscape**.

== Game Design Manifest

1. xref:tactical_trpg_system.adoc[*tactical_trpg_system.adoc*]::
   *Tactical Strategy RPG (TRPG) System Specification*: Comprehensive blueprint of the dual-layer combat system:
   - Phase 1: Grid-based tactical battlefield (tile movement, terrain, facing).
   - Phase 2: Cinematic combat resolution (traditional side-scroll / action duels).
   - Dual-protagonist synergy and moral sway integration.

2. xref:relics_and_combat.adoc[*relics_and_combat.adoc*]::
   *The Twin Relics, Combat Stances & Tactical Abilities*:
   - Specifications of the Shield of Lux, Sword of Tenebris, the 4-stance state machine, and the `[-100, +100]` moral continuum.
   - Core skills: `Lux Barrio`, `Cuttatio Tenebris`, and `Duo-Loca Bashin n Slash`.
   - The Law of Tempering and Dissipation.
   - The Relic Embellishment System (Ring of Fire, Book of Monsters, Medusa's Sword).

3. xref:campaign_structure_and_endings.adoc[*campaign_structure_and_endings.adoc*]::
   *Campaign Architecture, The King's Curse & Multi-Ending Specification*:
   - The Two-Half Campaign Structure (Act I King Jihad, Act II The Dark Lord).
   - The Origin of "The King's Curse" (open planar wound under the High Throne) and King Jihad's possession.
   - The Midpoint Fork: Neutral Path vs. True Ending Path ("The Ugly Truth").
   - Player consent to know the deeper lore and missable nocturnal trials.
   - The Complete 5-Route Ending Matrix (Canonical Harmony, Benevolent Stasis, Calcified Tyrant, Abyssal Usurper, Secret Chronicler).

4. xref:dreamscape_and_temporal_architecture.adoc[*dreamscape_and_temporal_architecture.adoc*]::
   *The Dreamscape & Temporal Architecture*:
   - Engine integration (`LOC_THE_DREAMSCAPE`), LeTBS platform battlefields, and Dream Eaters.
   - Opening astral rite with Chrono the Sage and the "AWAKEN!" transition.
   - Chapter 1 demo gating as a terrestrial anchor in Dio Village.
   - Grandma's Watch evolving into The Dream Compass (Layer 0 monotonic clock).
   - Nighttime synchronization and event-driven temporal phasing.

5. xref:world_topography.adoc[*world_topography.adoc*]::
   *World Topography & Tactical Encounter Design*: The geography of the Kingdom of Sacyra,
   defensive provinces, battlefield terrain modifiers, and threat scaling.

6. xref:game_testing.adoc[*game_testing.adoc*]::
   *Game Dev Testing vs Software Testing*: Deep dive into continuous state invariants,
   golden master replay parity, softlock immunity stress tests, and 60 FPS frame-budget latency headroom.

7. xref:demo_baseline/README.adoc[*demo_baseline/README.adoc*]::
   *2017–2018 Historical Demo Baseline & Extraction Compendium*: Verified baseline extracted
   from the original RPG Maker MV datasets (`Old Game Data` and `Old_Game_Data_2/data`), detailing
   the complete 43-map scenario walkthrough, full cast compendium, LeTBS tactical grid mechanics,
   grimoire, bestiary, and system switches/variables.
