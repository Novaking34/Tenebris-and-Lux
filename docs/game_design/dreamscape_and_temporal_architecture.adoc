== Overview & Engine Integration

This document specifies the technical, mechanical, and temporal architecture of **The Dreamscape**
in *Tenebris and Lux*. Grounded in the recovered 2017–2018 RPG Maker MV demo baseline (Maps 006, 008,
011, 041, 042) and integrated into the ISO C11 engine architecture, it formalizes the planar opening
sequence with Chrono the Sage, the terrestrial demo gating, the evolution of **Grandma's Watch** into
**The Dream Compass**, and nocturnal event-driven temporal phasing.

== 1. Engine Architecture & Topological Verification

The Dreamscape is not an ephemeral cutscene or narrative metaphor; it is an active, fully integrated
subsystem registered within the Layer 1 simulation engine:

=== 1.1. Explicit Province Registration
The plane is codified as an explicit topological enumeration in `include/sim/sim_types.h`:

[source,c]
----
typedef enum {
    LOC_THE_DREAMSCAPE    = 0,  /* Astral origin, ancestral archive, planar rift */
    LOC_DIO_VILLAGE       = 1,  /* Pastoral woodland hamlet; Grandma's hearth    */
    LOC_WHISPERING_WOODS  = 2,  /* Outlying frontier timberlands; beast paths    */
    LOC_SACYRA_MARCHES    = 3,  /* Fortified royal road to the capital citadel   */
    LOC_COUNT             = 4
} location_id_t;
----

=== 1.2. Tactical LeTBS Battlefields
The Dreamscape encompasses five dedicated grid environments extracted from the historical archives:

* **Map 006 (Chrono's Archive / The Hall of Sages):** Archetype stone pedestals, floating crystal
  columns, and ancestral book pedestals.
* **Map 008 (The Astral Crucible):** Tutorial grid where the bearer tests `Lux Barrio` and
  `Cuttatio Tenebris` against encroaching void scouts.
* **Map 011 (The Shifting Leyline Bridges):** Dynamic tactical grid featuring floating platforms
  and crystal floor switches that alter terrain heights.
* **Maps 041 & 042 (The Deep Astral Depths):** High-level nocturnally phased battlefields featuring
  astral leyline conduits that replenish MP while inflicting planar shear.

=== 1.3. Parameterized Combatants: The Dream Eaters
The Dreamscape is populated by **Dream Eaters**—parasitic entities dispatched across the thinning veil
by The Dark Lord to devour ancestral records prior to the Festival of Heroes:

[source,text]
----
Threat Profile: Dream Eater
├── Threat HP: 70
├── Physical Defense (DEF): 11
├── Arcane Defense (MDF): 8
├── Flank Vigor: 70
└── Tactical AI: Rushes fragile casters; vulnerable to radiant deflection (Lux Barrio).
----

=== 1.4. Bidirectional Topology
The spatial graph connects seamlessly between the metaphysical and physical planes:
* Northward: `Awaken` $\to$ `LOC_DIO_VILLAGE` (Grandma Magolia's Hearth).
* Southward: `Slumber Meditation` $\to$ `LOC_THE_DREAMSCAPE` (Requires Dream Compass nighttime flag).

== 2. Narrative Inception: The Astral Rite of Awakening

=== 2.1. Unformed Potential in the Astral Archive
The game opens not in a mundane tavern or military camp, but inside the timeless ether of **The Dreamscape**.
Because this plane houses 3,000 years of ancestral memory—and the marooned soul of the First King—having
**Chrono the Sage** guide the player through class archetype stones elevates character creation into
a canonical ritual of spiritual attunement.

=== 2.2. Tri-Perspective Adaptability
Opening in unformed ether allows the simulation to cleanly accommodate any of the three playable vantage
points—**Elijah Ro-enheart**, **Lady Katrina Jaren**, or a custom-named bearer (`<chosen_name>`)—before
their identity crystallizes into physical form.

=== 2.3. The Ludonarrative Shock of "AWAKEN!"
After proving tactical mastery against Dream Eaters in the abstract void, Chrono banishes the player
with an urgent command: _"AWAKEN!"_. The immediate cut to Grandma Magolia's crackling birch hearth in
Dio Village shocks the player into physical reality, establishing a stark contrast between cosmic
destiny and human vulnerability.

== 3. Terrestrial Gating & The Dream Compass

=== 3.1. Chapter 1 Demo Gating as a Terrestrial Anchor
Throughout Chapter 1 (the entire demo narrative), the player is strictly locked out of returning to
The Dreamscape. This design rule prevents premature cosmic dilution, forcing the player to invest
emotionally in the grounded human stakes of Dio Village:
* Grandma Magolia's failing health and maternal wisdom.
* Eric the Lumberjack's economic ruin from monster-infested logging trails.
* John the Thief's moral crusade against the predatory St. Baron.
* Monica the Mystic's grief beside the totem of fallen border soldiers.

=== 3.2. From Grandma's Watch to The Dream Compass
In the opening sequence, the player retrieves **Grandma's Watch** from the upstairs chest in Dio Village
(Map 020). Mechanically, this initializes the Layer 0 `time_utils` 24-hour monotonic clock:

[source,text]
----
Layer 0 Monotonic Clock Subsystem
├── Seconds / Minutes
├── Hours (00:00 to 23:59)
├── Weekdays (1: Sol, 2: Luna, 3: Ignis, 4: Aqua, 5: Terra, 6: Ventus, 7: Aether)
├── Weeks / Months
└── Calendar Countdown to The Festival of Heroes
----

Upon concluding the Chapter 1 demo introduction, Grandma's Watch undergoes its first metaphysical
transmutation, evolving into **The Dream Compass**.

=== 3.3. Nocturnal Synchronization & Scarcity
The Dream Compass acts as a dimensional resonant tuning fork with strict activation criteria:
* **Nighttime Window Only:** The Dream Compass functions exclusively during nocturnal hours
  (`20:00 <= hour || hour <= 04:00`). During daylight hours, the compass needle spins aimlessly.
* **Event-Driven Astral Windows:** Nocturnal side quests and main story trials in The Dreamscape
  manifest only during specific astrological alignments and calendar milestones.
* **Scarcity and Sacred Weight:** By restricting access to nocturnal windows, The Dreamscape is
  prevented from becoming a trivial grinding hub, preserving its sacred mystery and directly linking
  quest availability to the monotonic clock's relentless countdown toward the Festival of Heroes.
