== Overview & Ludological Philosophy

This specification articulates the structural macro-architecture of the campaign in *Tenebris and Lux*,
including the two-half campaign progression, the metaphysical etiology of "The King's Curse," the midpoint
narrative fork, player agency over lore discovery, and the complete five-route ending matrix. Built upon
the premise of ludonarrative consent, the engine allows casual players to experience a coherent linear
tactical campaign, while reserving the mythological truth for players who actively engage with nocturnal
Dreamscape trials.

== 1. The Two-Half Campaign Structure

The overarching campaign is organized into two distinct dramatic and mechanical halves:

[source,text]
----
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                   ACT I: THE SACYRAN SUCCESSION CRISIS                 │
│  • Setting: Dio Village, Whispering Woods, Capital Outposts            │
│  • Antagonist Focus: King Jihad & Corrupt Royal Court                  │
│  • Themes: Provincial neglect, family mysteries, state censorship     │
│  • Climax: The Festival of Heroes & Siege of Sacyra Castle             │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                   MIDPOINT FORK: THE KING'S CURSE                      │
│  • Defeat of King Jihad reveals possession by The Dark Lord's remnant  │
│  • Structural branching based on nocturnal Dream Compass completion    │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                   ACT II: THE COSMIC RECKONING                         │
│  • Setting: Sacyra Marches, Abyssal Vaults, The Dreamscape Core        │
│  • Antagonist Focus: The Dark Lord (Usuru's Ancient Void Essence)      │
│  • Themes: Severance vs. Cohesion, Atonement, Planar Reconciliation   │
│  • Climax: Confrontation at the Cosmic Threshold & Ending Resolution   │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
----

=== 1.1. Act I: Stopping King Jihad (The Sovereign of Ambition)
Act I centers on terrestrial, socio-political conflict. Operating from Dio Village through the Whispering
Woods and Capital marches, the party uncovers King Jihad's treasonous suppression of border alerts. The
act culminates during the **Festival of Heroes** at Sacyra Castle, where King Jihad is confronted and defeated.

=== 1.2. The Etiology of "The King's Curse" & Royal Possession
Court superstition long spoke of a hereditary madness afflicting monarchs of Sacyra. Forensic narrative
architecture reveals that "The King's Curse" is not a demonic hex, but an active **metaphysical hazard**:

* **The Open Planar Wound:** When eleven-year-old Usuru shattered the First King's stasis projection ritual
  3,000 years ago, a jagged planar rift tore open directly beneath the dias of the **High Throne**.
* **Psychic Backwash:** For three millennia, reigning monarchs sitting upon the High Throne were subjected
  to perpetual psychic shear and planar backwash from across the veil.
* **Jihad's Fatal Gambit:** Seeking to usurp the Dark Lord's void power during the thinned veil of the
  Festival of Heroes, King Jihad intentionally opened the conduit beneath his throne. Because the ethereal
  medium mirrors mortal intent, Jihad's megalomania inverted the flow, allowing a dormant psychic remnant
  of Usuru (**The Dark Lord**) to possess his body.

== 2. The Midpoint Fork: Neutral Path vs. True Ending Path

Defeating King Jihad reveals the void possession, forcing the narrative into its primary structural fork:

[source,text]
----
                         ACT I CLIMAX: DEFEAT OF KING JIHAD
                   Possession Revealed via "The King's Curse"
                                       │
            ┌──────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────┐
            ▼                                                     ▼
   THE NEUTRAL PATH (Basic Completion)                   THE TRUE ENDING PATH (100% Synthesis)
   • Dreamscape side quests missed/skipped.              • Nocturnal Dream Compass quests completed.
   • Treats possession as an external demon hex.         • Recognizes the severed ritual's energy signature.
   • Marches to slay or supplant The Dark Lord.          • Uncovers the First King's seal & Usuru's grief.
            │                                                     │
     ┌──────┴──────┐                                       ┌──────┴──────┐
     ▼             ▼                                       ▼             ▼
"Good" Ending  "Bad" Ending                        Canonical Harmony  Secret Chronicler
(Heroic Myth)  (Tyrant/Usurper)                    (Elijah + Katrina) (Custom Bearer)
----

=== 2.1. Why Basic Completion Is the Neutral Ending
By design, any player who simply plays the main story linearly from start to finish will reach the end
of the campaign. However, skipping the missable nocturnal **Dream Compass** trials produces the **Neutral Ending**:

* The player defeats King Jihad and destroys The Dark Lord's physical manifestation.
* The kingdom is saved in the present, but the **First King remains permanently marooned** in The Dreamscape.
* Usuru's ancient wound of betrayal is left unhealed, and the systemic neglect between the Capital and frontier
  provinces remains unchanged, ensuring the cycle will repeat.

=== 2.2. Player Consent to Know the Deeper Lore
Video games are distinguished by interactive agency. Forcing forty minutes of historical exposition down the
player's throat violates ludic pacing. By gating the full mythological truth behind optional, nocturnal
Dreamscape trials, the game honors the player's agency: players who desire a fast-paced tactical campaign
can enjoy a satisfying heroic conclusion, while players who actively investigate the mystery are rewarded
with the full mythological reality.

=== 2.3. Why the True Ending Is "The Ugly Truth"
The canonical True Ending is neither a simplistic fairy-tale triumph nor a dark nihilistic collapse. It is
**"The Ugly Truth"** that dismantles the self-serving propaganda of both sides:

* It exposes that Prince Mi-ren's loving preservation created a catastrophic institutional lie that deceived
  a continent for six festivals.
* It acknowledges that Usuru's childhood outrage was entirely valid before his unguided fury curdled into
  monstrous destruction.
* It reveals that Sacyra's three millennia of golden peace were bought at the cost of deliberate neglect
  toward frontier provinces like Libira and Dio Village.

== 3. Relic Embellishment & The Ten-Ending Campaign Matrix

Throughout both acts, the player's choices, side quests, and stance preferences actively **embellish**
the Shield of Lux and Sword of Tenebris along the `[-100, +100]` **Moral Sway** continuum.

=== 3.1. Telemetry Over Point Locks & The Bystander Corridor
Players do not aim for brittle, exact integers to unlock endings. Instead, `moral_sway` acts as
continuous behavioral telemetry showing where the player's philosophy is steering the world:

* **The Bystander Corridor (`[-50, +50]`):** Represents **Net Moral Inertia**—a mutual cancellation trap
  where opposing moral vectors offset each other. Floating in this corridor produces status-quo stagnation
  or repeating cycles.
* **The Threshold of Conviction:** Escaping the bystander corridor requires sustained virtue (`[+50, +100]`)
  or despotic malice (`[-100, -50]`).
* **Equilibrium (`0`):** An active, razor-thin discipline where Lux and Tenebris temper each other without
  collapsing into calcification or entropic rot.

=== 3.3. The Witness Protagonist, Keystone Route & Observer Effect

Reaching the True Ending ("The Ugly Truth") relies on three formal game design structures:

1. **The Witness Protagonist (Internal vs. External Focalization):**
   - **Elijah Ro-enheart** and **Lady Katrina Jaren** live inside the story (*Internal Focalization*).
     They are shaped by their personal trauma, social caste, and military or aristocratic backgrounds.
   - **The Custom Bearer (`<chosen_name>`)** serves as the *External Focalizer* (the Observer/Chronicler
     alongside Chrono in the Room of Sages), possessing the detachment required to perceive the full truth.
2. **The Keystone Route (Synthesis Scenario):**
   - Elijah uncovers the military conspiracy surrounding Commander Gareth and King Jihad.
   - Katrina uncovers noble and underworld exploitation alongside John the Thief and the Guild.
   - The Custom Bearer's campaign acts as the architectural keystone locking both partial perspectives together.
3. **The Quantum Observer Effect:**
   - Left to themselves, Sacyra's internal factions repeat the tragic 3,000-year cycle.
   - The outside Observer's conscious engagement collapses the tragic wavefunction into historical reconciliation.

=== 3.4. Chrono's Chronicle Ledger: NG+ Timeline Navigation

To ensure that exploring the full matrix never devolves into repetitive grinding:

* **Persistent Archival Memory:** Compendiums (*Book of Monsters*, *Book of Items*), unlocked embellishments,
  and combat masteries persist across runs.
* **Timeline Branching:** Once a campaign route is cleared, **Chrono's Chronicle Ledger** unlocks in the
  Room of Sages, functioning as an interactive timeline tree. Completionists can jump directly to pivotal
  decision bookmarks (such as Eric's honesty test, the St. Baron infiltration, or the Act I climax) to
  explore all 9 matrix endings without replaying introductory chapters.
* **Diegetic Saves:** Saving is rooted diegetically at **Grandma Magolia's Hearth** and **Chrono's Room of Sages**.

== 4. Systemic Invariants & Engine Triggers

All ending routes and campaign forks are evaluated through deterministic engine invariants in Layer 1:

1. **`player_state_t` Integrity:** The moral sway value (`int16_t moral_sway`) is clamped strictly
   within `[-100, +100]` via `math_clamp_int`.
2. **Dream Compass Nocturnal Flag:** Entry into `LOC_THE_DREAMSCAPE` requires `hour >= 20 || hour <= 4`
   from the Layer 0 monotonic clock (`time_get_monotonic_ms`).
3. **Equilibrium State Check:** Unlocking Route 7 or Route 10 requires `abs(moral_sway) <= 15` and both
   `shield_power >= 80` and `sword_edge >= 80` during the final turn sequence.
4. **Observer Flags:** The 10th True Ending requires validation flags `flag_elijah_cleared`,
   `flag_katrina_cleared`, and `dreamscape_trials_mask == 0xFF`.
