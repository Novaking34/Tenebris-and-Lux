== Overview

This document specifies the core ludological mechanics of *Tenebris and Lux* in the **Kingdom of Sacyra**
and **The Dreamscape**: the twin primordial relics (**The Shield of Lux** and **The Sword of Tenebris**),
the dynamic combat stance state machine, the moral sway continuum (`[-100, +100]`), and the tactical action
skills: **Defend** (Lux Barrio), **Attack** (Cuttatio Tenebris), and **Special** (Basic Magic, Basic Ward,
Basic Charm, and Duo-Loca Bashin n Slash).

== 1. Primordial Neutrality & Relic Mechanics

Neither relic is inherently good nor evil. They are manifestations of cosmic apex forces whose morality is
dictated solely by the intentions and choices of their wielders—canonically **Elijah Ro-enheart** and
**Katrina Jaren**, or a customizable bearer chosen by the player:

[cols="1,2,2", options="header"]
|===
| Relic | The Shield of Lux | The Sword of Tenebris
| **Aspect** | Apex Radiance & Cohesion | Apex Entropy & Dissolution
| **Energy Gauge** | `shield_power` ($0 \dots 100$) | `sword_edge` ($0 \dots 100$)
| **Canonical Bearer** | **Elijah Ro-enheart** (Vanguard Defense) | **Katrina Jaren** (Agile Severance)
| **Grid Role** | Zone-of-Control denial, sanctuary aura, ally protection | High-mobility flanking, armor penetration, mist evasion
| **Cinematic Action** | Timed kinetic blocks, shield-bashes, radiant shockwaves | Rapid dash strikes, armor severance, twilight blade waves
| **Resource Dynamics** | Regenerates on guard; absorbs incoming assault | Consumes edge on strikes; hones outside combat
|===

== 2. The Combat Stance State Machine

The battle posture shifts dynamically across four discrete combat stances:

[source,text]
----
                   ┌───────────────────┐
                   │  STANCE_NEUTRAL   │
                   └─────────┬─────────┘
                             │
         ┌───────────────────┼───────────────────┐
         ▼                   ▼                   ▼
┌─────────────────┐ ┌─────────────────┐ ┌─────────────────┐
│ STANCE_WARDING  │ │ STANCE_STRIKING │ │STANCE_EQUILIBRIUM
│ (Shield Guard)  │ │ (Sword Drawn)   │ │ (Twin Harmony)  │
└─────────────────┘ └─────────────────┘ └─────────────────┘
----

1. **`STANCE_NEUTRAL`**: Relics held at waist. Balanced baseline.
   - Standard movement on the tactical grid; baseline attack parameters.
2. **`STANCE_WARDING`**: Shield raised in protective vanguard high-guard.
   - Grid Effect: Increases allied defense within adjacent tiles by $+25\%$.
   - Combat Effect: Boosts Shield power recovery ($+15$), increases stagger damage against attacking foes ($25$ HP), and wards against incoming status curses.
3. **`STANCE_STRIKING`**: Greatsword held in aggressive two-handed assault posture.
   - Grid Effect: Increases unit movement by $+1$ tile and grants armor-piercing flank bonuses.
   - Combat Effect: Increases strike damage from $30$ to $45$ (or base $+15$ bonus); reduces edge cost from $12$ to $8$ points.
4. **`STANCE_EQUILIBRIUM`**: Harmonized twin-relic synchronization.
   - Grid Effect: Immunity to positioning debuffs and void mire terrain penalties.
   - Combat Effect: Simultaneously restores $+15$ Shield power and $+15$ Sword edge; parries incoming strikes while counter-attacking for $20$ damage; nudges moral sway toward center ($0$).

== 3. The Moral Sway Continuum & Decoupled Elemental Expression

The moral trajectory of Sacyra's champions is tracked along a continuous bounded integer axis:

[source,text]
----
[ -100: Sovereign of Ruin ] ◄──── [ 0: Equilibrium ] ────► [ +100: Saint of Mercy ]
                 ▲                                ▲
                 └─── [ -50 to +50: Bystander ] ──┘
----

=== 3.1. Decoupling Morality ("The Why") from Elemental Expression ("The How")
Neither Lux nor Tenebris dictates a character's moral alignment:
* **Virtuous Lux (`+1 ... +100`):** Selfless sanctuary, kinetic mitigation (*Lux Barrio*), and restorative peace.
* **Tyrannical Lux (`-100 ... -1`):** Blinding glare, totalitarian enforcement of rigid order, and calcifying living will into petrified stasis (The Mi-ren Fallacy).
* **Virtuous Tenebris (`+1 ... +100`):** Surgical severance (*Cuttatio Tenebris*), cutting corrupt bindings, and executing Absolute Justice without unnecessary bloodshed (The Casatti Ideal).
* **Malevolent Tenebris (`-100 ... -1`):** Creeping entropic rot, nihilistic bloodlust, and devouring devastation (The Dark Lord).

=== 3.2. The Bystander Corridor (`[-50, +50]`) as Net Moral Inertia
The `[-50, +50]` band represents **Net Moral Inertia**, where opposing deeds cancel out into zero net displacement. Staying within this corridor traps the protagonists in 3,000 years of repeating cyclical tragedy. Breaking past $\pm 50$ through deliberate micro-choices (such as aiding Eric or John in Dio Village) asserts heroic agency: *"Trauma is Inherited, Destiny is Chosen."*

=== 3.3. Equilibrium (`0`)
Maintained by active discipline rather than passive fence-sitting; balancing the blade and the aegis so that radiant light tempers the void without collapsing into calcified stasis or entropic rot. Unlocks the tandem secret art *Duo-Loca Bashin n Slash*.

== 4. Tactical Actions & Combat Skills

In the revised, authentic combat framework, basic actions expand into dedicated martial and arcane skills:

=== 4.1. Defend (`defend` / `ward`)
* **Standard Defend**: Braces behind the Shield of Lux, regenerating $+10 \dots +15$ shield power and mitigating incoming damage.
* **Basic Skill: `Lux Barrio` (`barrio`)**:
  - Manifests a radiant, geometric barrier of pure light.
  - **Effect**: Blocks incoming attack and reduces subsequent incoming damage by **$25\%$** for a rolled turn duration.
  - **Duration Roll**: Deterministically rolls for **$2 \dots 5$ turns** (`2 + (turns % 4)`).
  - **Resonance**: Saturates Shield of Lux by $+20$ points (capped at $100$) and awards $+3$ moral virtue.

=== 4.2. Attack (`attack` / `strike`)
* **Standard Attack**: Strikes the target enemy using the Sword of Tenebris, consuming edge points and dealing physical damage (`(ATK * 2) - DEF`).
* **Basic Skill: `Cuttatio Tenebris` (`cuttatio`)**:
  - A sweeping abyssal crescent slash infused with dark mist.
  - **Direct Damage**: Deals full primary severance damage to the selected target.
  - **Splash Damage**: Deals **$20\%$ splash damage** to surrounding and flanking foes (`threat_surrounding_hp`).
  - **Edge Dynamic**: Consumes $8 \dots 12$ edge points.

=== 4.3. Special (`special` / `balance`)
Replaces balance with a four-fold technique tree honoring the twin relics and arcane arts:

1. **`Basic Magic` (`magic`)**:
   - Neutral elemental magic damage attacking enemy Magic Defense (`(MAT * 2) - MDF`).
   - Costs $10$ MP. Pure, unaligned cosmic energy.
2. **`Basic Ward` (`ward` under special)**:
   - Magic-based defend weaving an arcane barrier.
   - Restores $+15$ Shield power and staggers incoming assault without relying purely on physical bracing.
   - Costs $6$ MP.
3. **`Basic Charm` (`charm`)**:
   - Enhances astral focus, granting **$+20\%$ Magic Attack (MAT)** for **$1 \dots 3$ turns** (`1 + (turns % 3)`).
   - Costs $8$ MP.
4. **`Duo-Loca Bashin n Slash` (`duo`)**:
   - The signature dual-relic strike uniting the Shield of Lux and Sword of Tenebris.
   - **Adaptive Damage**: Intelligently evaluates whether the player's Magic Attack or Physical Attack is superior, or whichever enemy defense (DEF vs MDF) is lower, channeling into the foe's weakest resistance:
     * If enemy MDF < DEF or MAT > ATK: executes as **Magical Twilight Slash**.
     * Otherwise: executes as **Physical Radiant Bash**.
   - **Relic Harmony**: Simultaneously restores $+15$ Shield power and $+15$ Sword edge, while nudging moral sway toward absolute cosmic equilibrium ($0$).
   - Costs $12$ MP.

=== 4.4. Hero Customization & Protagonist Lore
* While **Elijah Ro-enheart** and **Katrina Jaren** remain the canonical dual protagonists representing the Shield of Lux and Sword of Tenebris, the player can dynamically designate their own hero identity using the `name <chosen_name>` command.
* All narrative chronicle logs, character severity tags, and telemetry traces adapt cleanly to the bearer's active name.

== 5. The Metaphysical Law of Tempering & Dissipation

The interaction between the Shield of Lux and the Sword of Tenebris is governed by the ancient law established during the 7-Year Great War:

* **Lux as the Singular Tempering Agent:** Raw Tenebris is entropic and severing; left unchecked, it erodes both target and wielder. Lux is the only force capable of containing and tempering Tenebris, providing structural cohesion so that void strikes can be directed safely.
* **The Fragility of Radiance (Dissipation):** While Lux tempers Tenebris, it requires emotional and ethical stability. Any sudden surge of terror, malice, or panic causes Lux to **dissipate immediately**, collapsing protective barriers and leaving Tenebris unbridled.
* **Ludonarrative Impact:** In combat, executing dark commands with negative moral sway degrades Lux Barrio duration rolls, while maintaining Equilibrium (`0` sway) maximizes barrier resilience and tandem skill execution.

== 6. The Relic Embellishment System

Throughout the campaign, the Shield of Lux and Sword of Tenebris are actively embellished through quest outcomes, moral choices, and auxiliary relics:

1. **Eric's Test of Honesty (Map 028) $\to$ The Ring of Fire:**
   - Donating rent to Eric rewards the **Ring of Fire**, imbuing strikes and barriers with flame-weave properties, connecting directly to the LeTBS *Burning Tile Spread* tactical state.
2. **Monica's Offering (Maps 027/029) $\to$ The Book of Monsters:**
   - Sponsoring an offering to **Lyri, Goddess of Peace**, attunes the relics to arcane resonance and awards the **Book of Monsters**, unlocking advanced MDF-targeting formulas.
3. **John the Thief vs. St. Baron (Maps 030/031):**
   - Aiding John enhances critical severance and stealth penetration; upholding feudal property law reinforces kinetic poise and bastion authority.
4. **Armory Relics (Medusa's Sword & Heart Shield):**
   - Optional material infusions granting petrification stasis or passive HP vitality warding.

== 7. Relic Profiles & The Ten-Ending Campaign Matrix

The final profile of relic embellishments along the `[-100, +100]` Moral Sway continuum maps into the 3x3 matrix crowned by the 10th synthesis ending:
* **Lux-Good (`[+50, +100]`):** *The Radiant Sanctuary* — Selfless protection without tyranny.
* **Lux-Neutral (`[-50, +50]`):** *The Benevolent Stasis* — The Mi-ren Fallacy; golden crystal cocoon.
* **Lux-Bad (`[-100, -50]`):** *The Calcified Tyrant* — Radiance weaponized into an inquisitorial police state.
* **Tenebris-Good (`[+50, +100]`):** *Absolute Justice* — The Casatti Ideal; surgical severance of corruption.
* **Tenebris-Neutral (`[-50, +50]`):** *The Cynical Outlaw* — Threat slain; realm abandoned to decay.
* **Tenebris-Bad (`[-100, -50]`):** *The Abyssal Usurper* — The Cycle of Usuru; rules from obsidian ashes.
* **Statera-Good (`[+50, +100]`):** *Harmonized Concord* — Both relics united in restorative justice.
* **Statera-Neutral (`[-50, +50]`):** *Armed Deterrence* — Uneasy status quo; frontier rift unhealed.
* **Statera-Bad (`[-100, -50]`):** *The Dual Despot* — Totalitarian martial hegemony.
* **Synthesis (10th Route):** *The True Ending ("The Ugly Truth")* — Unlocked via Elijah, Katrina, and the Custom Bearer (`<chosen_name>`) alongside nocturnal Dreamscape trials, mending the 3,000-year planar tear.
