== Overview

This document specifies the core ludological mechanics of *Tenebris and Lux* in the **Kingdom of Sacyra**:
the twin primordial relics (**The Shield of Lux** and **The Sword of Tenebris**), the dynamic combat stance
state machine, the moral sway continuum (`[-100, +100]`), and their integration into the Tactical Strategy RPG
system featuring grid maneuvering and cinematic side-view combat resolution.

== 1. Primordial Neutrality & Relic Mechanics

Neither relic is inherently good nor evil. They are manifestations of cosmic apex forces whose morality is
dictated solely by the intentions and choices of their wielders—**Elijah Ro-enheart** and **Katrina Jaren**:

[cols="1,2,2", options="header"]
|===
| Relic | The Shield of Lux | The Sword of Tenebris
| **Aspect** | Apex Radiance & Cohesion | Apex Entropy & Dissolution
| **Energy Gauge** | `shield_power` ($0 \dots 100$) | `sword_edge` ($0 \dots 100$)
| **Primary Bearer** | **Elijah Ro-enheart** (Vanguard Defense) | **Katrina Jaren** (Agile Severance)
| **Grid Role** | Zone-of-Control denial, sanctuary aura, ally protection | High-mobility flanking, armor penetration, mist evasion
| **Cinematic Action** | Timed kinetic blocks, shield-bashes, radiant shockwaves | Rapid dash strikes, armor severance, twilight blade waves
| **Resource Dynamics** | Regenerates $+20$ on ward; absorbs incoming assault | Consumes edge on strikes; hones $+15$ outside combat
|===

== 2. The Combat Stance State Machine

The battle posture shifts dynamically across four discrete combat stances:

[source,text]
----
                   ┌───────────────────┐
                   │  STANCE_NEUTRAL   │
                   └─────────┬─────────┘
                             │
         ┌───────────────────┼───────────────────┐
         ▼                   ▼                   ▼
┌─────────────────┐ ┌─────────────────┐ ┌─────────────────┐
│ STANCE_WARDING  │ │ STANCE_STRIKING │ │STANCE_EQUILIBRIUM
│ (Shield Guard)  │ │ (Sword Drawn)   │ │ (Twin Harmony)  │
└─────────────────┘ └─────────────────┘ └─────────────────┘
----

1. **`STANCE_NEUTRAL`**: Relics held at waist. Balanced baseline.
   - Standard movement on the tactical grid; baseline attack parameters.
2. **`STANCE_WARDING`**: Shield raised in protective vanguard high-guard.
   - Grid Effect: Increases allied defense within adjacent tiles by $+25\%$.
   - Cinematic Duel: Boosts Shield power recovery ($+15$), increases stagger damage against attacking foes ($25$ HP), and wards against incoming status curses.
3. **`STANCE_STRIKING`**: Greatsword held in aggressive two-handed assault posture.
   - Grid Effect: Increases unit movement by $+1$ tile and grants armor-piercing flank bonuses.
   - Cinematic Duel: Increases strike severance from $30$ to $45$ damage; reduces edge cost from $12$ to $8$ points.
4. **`STANCE_EQUILIBRIUM`**: Harmonized twin-relic synchronization.
   - Grid Effect: Immunity to positioning debuffs and void mire terrain penalties.
   - Cinematic Duel: Simultaneously restores $+15$ Shield power and $+15$ Sword edge; parries incoming strikes while counter-attacking for $20$ damage; nudges moral sway toward center ($0$).

== 3. The Moral Sway Continuum

The moral trajectory of Sacyra's champions is tracked along a continuous bounded integer axis:

[source,text]
----
[ -100: Sovereign of Ruin ] ◄──── [ 0: Equilibrium ] ────► [ +100: Saint of Mercy ]
----

* **Virtue & Mercy ($+1 \dots +100$)**:
  - Gained by shielding fleeing militia, sparing defeated enemy officers, resolving noble-court corruption through justice, and protecting the innocent with the Shield of Lux.
  - Unlocks royal alliances, Knight Commander endorsements, and sanctuary miracles.
* **Tyranny & Ruin ($-1 \dots -100$)**:
  - Accumulated through ruthless executions, collateral damage in villages, using scorched-earth radiance to blind subjects, or giving in to the void's bloodlust.
  - Unlocks forbidden void arts, mercenary enlistments, and intimidation tactics.
* **Equilibrium ($0$)**:
  - Maintained by balancing the blade and the aegis; recognizing that light without shadow is a calcified wasteland, and shadow without light is an endless void.
  - Unlocks the ultimate secret synthesis arts of ancient Sacyra.
