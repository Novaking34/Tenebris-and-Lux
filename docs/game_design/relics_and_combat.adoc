== Overview

This document specifies the core ludological mechanics of *Tenebris and Lux*: the twin
primordial relics (**The Shield of Lux** and **The Sword of Tenebris**), the dynamic
combat stance state machine, and the continuous moral sway continuum ($-100 \dots +100$).

== 1. Primordial Neutrality & Relic Mechanics

Neither relic is inherently good nor evil. They are manifestations of cosmic extremes:

[cols="1,2,2", options="header"]
|===
| Relic | The Shield of Lux | The Sword of Tenebris
| **Aspect** | Apex Radiance & Cohesion | Apex Entropy & Dissolution
| **Energy Gauge** | `shield_power` ($0 \dots 100$) | `sword_edge` ($0 \dots 100$)
| **Primary Action** | `ward` (or `shield`) | `strike` (or `slash`)
| **Tactical Role** | Damage deflection, stagger, barrier formation, illumination | Severance, armor penetration, mist cloaking, finishing strikes
| **Resource Dynamics** | Regenerates $+20$ on ward; absorbs incoming blows | Consumes edge on strikes; hones $+15$ outside combat
|===

== 2. The Combat Stance State Machine

The player shifts combat footing via the `stance <type>` command:

[source,text]
----
                   ┌───────────────────┐
                   │  STANCE_NEUTRAL   │
                   └─────────┬─────────┘
                             │
         ┌───────────────────┼───────────────────┐
         ▼                   ▼                   ▼
┌─────────────────┐ ┌─────────────────┐ ┌─────────────────┐
│ STANCE_WARDING  │ │ STANCE_STRIKING │ │STANCE_EQUILIBRIUM
│ (Shield Guard)  │ │ (Sword Drawn)   │ │ (Twin Harmony)  │
└─────────────────┘ └─────────────────┘ └─────────────────┘
----

1. **`STANCE_NEUTRAL`**: Relics held at waist. Balanced baseline.
2. **`STANCE_WARDING`**: Shield locked forward in high guard.
   - Warding boost: $+15$ Shield power regeneration on action.
   - Combat deflection: Staggers active foes for $25$ damage and protects innocents.
3. **`STANCE_STRIKING`**: Greatsword gripped in two-handed assault posture.
   - Strike damage increased from $30$ to $45$ severance damage.
   - Edge cost reduced from $12$ to $8$ points.
4. **`STANCE_EQUILIBRIUM`**: Harmonized twin-wield posture.
   - Simultaneously restores $+15$ Shield power and $+15$ Sword edge.
   - Parries incoming attacks while riposting for $20$ counter-damage.
   - Nudges moral sway toward cosmic center ($0$).

== 3. The Moral Sway Continuum

The moral trajectory of the Champion is tracked on a continuous integer axis:

[source,text]
----
[ -100: Sovereign of Ruin ] ◄──── [ 0: Equilibrium ] ────► [ +100: Saint of Mercy ]
----

* **Virtue & Mercy ($+1 \dots +100$)**:
  - Gained by shielding the defenseless (`ward`), routing foes without unnecessary brutality, and seeking reconciliation between the Kingdom's warring orders.
* **Tyranny & Ruin ($-1 \dots -100$)**:
  - Accumulated through relentless slaughter, excessive use of scorched-earth light to blind subjects, or feeding the Dark Lord's chaos.
* **Equilibrium ($0$)**:
  - Maintained by balancing the blade and the aegis; recognizing that light without shadow is a blinding wasteland, and shadow without light is a stagnant abyss.
