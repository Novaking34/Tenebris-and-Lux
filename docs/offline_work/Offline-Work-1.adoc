== 1. World-Building & Game Development Philosophy

=== 1.1. Pre-Production, Technical Foundations & AI Collaboration

The foundational first step in game development is **Pre-Production and Conceptualization**,
where core gameplay loops, narrative pillars, and technical architecture are locked in before
production assets or gameplay scripts are written. In *Tenebris and Lux*, this phase was executed
by stripping the repository down to standard ISO C11 with zero external downloads and enforcing
the **OmniCode Universal 5-Block Lifecycle Protocol** (Metadata, Opening/Types, Body, Audit Procedures,
Closing/Test & CLI). By establishing a zero-allocation **Central Manifest Registry** (`ALLOC_ZERO_HEAP`)
and pre-flight diagnostic tripwires under the axiom _"Don't start a machine you know has loose bolts,"_
the engine guarantees mathematical and state integrity prior to runtime simulation.

Within this workflow, an AI collaborator functions as an information-bounded external compiler and documentation accelerator:

* **Eliminating Transcription Drag:** Translating a complete, fluid mental model into 80-column AsciiDoc manuals, comparative triage matrices, and C11 data structures removes hundreds of hours of clerical friction while preserving creative bandwidth for high-level direction.
* **Acting as a "Dry-Run Compiler":** Because the AI only knows what has been explicitly provided in context, any predictive guess immediately highlights where an unwritten assumption resides in the creator's head, allowing that rule to be formally codified.
* **Cross-Dataset Triage & Extraction:** Automated tooling (`extract_demo_data.py`) and structured analysis reconcile the 2017–2018 RPG Maker MV archives—combining **Folder 1** (`Old Game Data`, Version 40730002, 36 maps, complete narrative/morality branches) and **Folder 2** (`Old_Game_Data_2/data`, Version 2078310, 43 maps, 5-unit LeTBS tactical combat)—while triaging out Tier 1 RTP boilerplate, evolving Tier 2 sandbox prototypes, and preserving Tier 3 canonical pillars.

=== 1.2. Why the Shape of the Overarching World Matters

Establishing the macro-shape of the overarching world is just as vital as detailing individual people, places, and cultures because the macro-structure acts as the architectural mold that dictates the pressures, physics, and emotional gravity of every micro-detail inside it:

* **Systemic Causality Over Narrative Contrivance:** When macro-rules are defined first—such as a seven-day courier delay to a frontier kingdom or an ethereal veil that thins for one week a year—character motivations emerge as inevitable consequences of their environment rather than artificial plot devices.
* **Negative Space Defines the Inhabitants:** Charting the geopolitical fault lines between the six powers of The Great War creates predefined societal currents. Every future village, merchant, or scholar automatically inherits historical weight based on where they sit along those fault lines.
* **Ludonarrative Cohesion Between Canon and Code:** The overarching metaphysical rules translate directly into deterministic C11 mathematical invariants. The lore rule that *Lux tempers Tenebris, but dissipates under violent emotional shifts* directly governs the simulation engine's `[-100, +100]` Moral Sway clamping, *Lux Barrio* duration rolls (`2 ... 5` turns), and *Cuttatio Tenebris* splash cleaves (`20%`).
* **Fractal Storytelling (The Micro Mirrors the Macro):** Small, local interactions in the opening village naturally echo ancient continental history. When Elijah hesitates before Grandma Magolia's chest out of respect for family rules, or Katrina opens it immediately looking for a will, their domestic choices mirror the ancient ideological split between Prince Mi-ren and Prince Casatti.

== 2. Core Metaphysics, Power Systems & The Ethereal Veil

=== 2.1. The Primordial Motif: Morality in the Heart, Not the Element

Good and evil exist as real moral absolutes within the world, yet neither Light (**Lux**) nor Darkness (**Tenebris**) carries an inherent ethical charge. Both are morally neutral cosmic forces; morality is determined strictly by the **actions, conscience, and intentions of the heart behind the weapon**, tracked mechanically along the `[-100, +100]` **Moral Sway** continuum via the **Compass of Morality**:

[cols="1,2,2,2", options="header"]
|===
| Cosmic Force | Ontological Essence | Virtuous Manifestation (Heart of Mercy / Justice) | Corrupted Manifestation (Heart of Tyranny / Malice)

| **Lux** (Radiance)
| Cohesion, illumination, temporal anchoring, and preservation.
| Projects protective golden barriers (*Lux Barrio*), wards the vulnerable, and preserves peace through reconciliation.
| Enforces calcifying stasis, denies natural mortality, blinds dissenters, and traps the world in inflexible order.

| **Tenebris** (Darkness)
| Severance, dissolution, finality, and the fertile void.
| Cuts through tyrant armor (*Cuttatio Tenebris*), severs corrupt bindings, and enacts swift, surgical **Absolute Justice**.
| Spreads entropic rot, feeds bitter vengeance, dissolves conscience, and fuels destructive conquest.
|===

=== 2.2. The Metaphysical Dynamic: Tempering vs. Dissipation

The interaction between Lux and Tenebris is governed by a strict law of equilibrium:

  tempers destructive void           grants decisive motion

     Tenebris runs unchecked into devouring void;

1. **Lux as the Singular Tempering Agent:** Raw Tenebris is entropic and severing; left unchecked, it erodes everything it touches. Lux is the only force capable of containing and tempering Tenebris, providing structural cohesion so that the void's cutting edge can be wielded safely.
2. **The Fragility of Radiance (Dissipation):** While Lux can temper Tenebris, it requires emotional and ethical stability to hold its form. Any sudden, violent shift in the heart—such as terror, betrayal, panic, or malice—causes Lux to **dissipate immediately**, collapsing protective barriers and leaving Tenebris unbridled.

=== 2.3. The Power System Schism: Magic vs. Science

Following the discovery of Tenebris and Lux during the crisis of the First King, an ideological conflict emerged regarding the nature of power in the world:

* **The Academic Rationalists ("Science"):** Championed by **The Academy**, this camp views Lux, Tenebris, and elemental properties as measurable, reproducible natural laws governed by resonance geometries and leyline mathematics.
* **The Traditional Mystics & Prelates ("Magic"):** Championed by provincial mystics and religious orders (such as followers of **Lyri, the Goddess of Peace**), this camp views these forces as spiritual miracles, blessings, or curses channeled through faith and offerings.

=== 2.4. The Ethereal Substrate: The Dreamscape & Legends of Crossing the Veil

Alongside the waking physical world exists **The Dreamscape**, an ethereal plane of consciousness, ancestral memory, and timeless trials watched over by **Chrono** in the Room of Sages.

* **The Annual Thinning of the Veil:** For one week each year—coinciding with the grand annual festival—the boundary ("the Veil") between the temporal world and the Dreamscape thins to gossamer, creating a window where mortals can "make their dreams come true."
* **Legends of Old Regarding "Crossing the Veil":**
** *The Mirror of Intent:* The ethereal medium reflects the heart of whoever touches it. Collective joy and reverence manifest benevolent projections, whereas sudden shock or spite inverts the ether into waking nightmares.
** *The Severed Tether:* Channeling the thinned veil in short bursts is stable, but sustaining a bridge across an entire week carries extreme peril. If a ritual is violently interrupted while a spirit bridges the veil, the tether snaps—permanently sealing the consciousness inside the Dreamscape.
** *The Infiltration of the Dream Eaters:* Widening or tearing the veil attracts **Dream Eaters**—parasitic entities native to the deep ether that feast upon mortal memories, hopes, and unresolved grief.

=== Era I: The Founding of Sacyra, Self-Running Systems & The Two Princes

1. **The Mundane Armaments of Inception:** At the beginning of the creation of the **Kingdom of Sacyra** (pronounced *"saw-kee-ruh"*, `/sɔːˈkiːrə/`), the **Shield of Lux** and the **Sword of Tenebris** were forged as ordinary, non-magical weapons acting as civic symbols for **Peace** and **War**.
2. **A Reign So Peaceful War Was Merely an Idea:** The First King was deeply beloved by his people. Under his leadership, the kingdom's administrative, legal, and civic systems were so well-engineered that the realm practically governed itself. Citizens did not lack access to the King because he was aloof, but because the systems functioned so smoothly that a personal royal audience was only needed "once in a blue moon".
3. **The Two Sons (Mi-ren and Casatti):** The First King had two sons who chose distinct paths while still young teenagers:
* **Prince Mi-ren:** The elder son and heir to the throne, raised to carry on his father's governance.
* **Prince Casatti:** The younger son, who chose to begin **The Academy** rather than accepting the birthright of the throne, dedicating himself to studying the Dreamscape and the elemental properties of the world.

=== Era II: The International Festival of Peoples & The Four-Year Coma

1. **Why the Festival Lasted a Full Week:** Every year, the Capital hosted the **Festival of Peoples**—an international celebration used to build out borders, negotiate major kingdom projects, and give all peoples a chance to celebrate together. Rather than holding a single-day event after gather days, the First King designed it as a full week-long festival out of deep consideration for distant subjects: those who needed to leave early for harvest or travel obligations could still attend, and delegations traveling from remote corners of the known world were guaranteed meaningful participation.
2. **The Coma Following the First Festival & Discovery of the Dreamscape:** Immediately after the very first major Festival of Peoples, the First King fell into a coma. However, the King was not unconscious; his spirit was fully awake and aware inside **The Dreamscape**. By tracking his father's displaced consciousness, Casatti and the Academy discovered and mapped the Dreamscape in full.
3. **Festivals 1 Through 4 (The Rescue Effort):** During the first four years of the King's coma, he was not completely inaccessible during the rest of the year, but the week of the festival—when the veil thinned—was the **surest, strongest window** to access him. Throughout those four festivals, Mi-ren, Casatti, and the Castle worked tirelessly to pull the First King out of his coma and back into his waking body.

=== Era III: The 5th Festival Passing, The Vigil & What "Of Age" Meant

1. **The Death at the 5th Festival:** During the **5th Festival of Peoples**, the First King's physical body died, while his consciousness remained lucid on the other side of the veil.
2. **Why the Festival Continued (Festivals 6–10):** Once the King's body passed away, pulling him out of the coma was no longer possible. However, the annual festival continued because that thinned-veil week was now the **only reliable lifeline** left for Mi-ren and the Castle to commune with the First King's spirit and project him into the temporal world in short bursts when rare royal audiences were required. Everyone in the Castle knew the truth; concealing his passing was not a malicious lie, but a protective vigil to allow the Festival of Peoples to continue and keep the peace until Mi-ren was officially "of age."
3. **The Meaning of "Of Age" in the First Royal Succession:** Although princes are normally crowned immediately upon a monarch's death, Sacyra had never experienced a royal succession before. The entire international peace was anchored to the First King's personal covenant, and no legal precedent existed for a regency. Furthermore, Mi-ren and Casatti were still teenagers; in Sacyra's chivalric tradition, being "of age" required full physical and martial adulthood to lead the Royal Guard, alongside mastery of the Academy's statecraft systems so the self-running realm would not fracture under surrounding kingdoms.

=== Era IV: Usuru the Wanderer, Libira's "Quiet Complaint" & The 11th Year Tragedy

1. **The Kingdom of Libira & The "Quiet Complaint":** Far from the Capital lay the **Kingdom of Libira**, a remote, sparsely populated kingdom that was a full week's travel away just to deliver announcements from the Capital. Because Libira was not yet established enough as a kingdom to support a relay outpost system, this seven-day delay created a simmering **"quiet complaint"** among Libirans regarding the gap between what was known in the Capital and what remained unknown on the periphery.
2. **The Three-Day Wanderer:** During the First King's peaceful reign, a boy was born in Libira to a humble family without being given a name. For ten years, he grew up in Libira and traveled the world as a restless wanderer who **never visited any place for more than three days**—except for the annual week-long Festival of Peoples in the Capital, which was a sacred time for him to celebrate and see the King he revered.
3. **The 11th Visitation & The Accidental Interruption:** During the boy's eleventh year visiting the festival, a "plague" was announced as having struck the First King, leaving him unseen for the entire festival. Concerned because he had not seen the King, the unnamed eleven-year-old boy went into the castle to seek an audience. Inside, he discovered the secret: the First King had passed away years ago, and Mi-ren had been using knowledge from the Academy each year to keep this at bay until he came of age.
4. **The Devastating Sway & The Birth of Tenebris:** From the boy's frontier perspective—knowing nothing of the Castle's protective vigil—the Capital's entire promise looked like a monstrous fraud. In his shock, he accidentally tampered with the ritual array, triggering four catastrophic consequences:
* **The Eternal Seal:** The First King was permanently sealed inside **The Dreamscape**.
* **The Discovery of Tenebris:** The shift in that one boy's heart was so devastating that it caused a violent metaphysical sway—an anomalous energy spike that birthed **Tenebris** into the waking world that very day.
* **The Warped Distance:** The backlash physically warped the distance between Libira and the Capital, which the boy perceived as an intentional, hostile separation imposed by the Crown.
* **The Naming of Usuru & Call to War:** Taking the name **Usuru**, the boy returned to Libira, tapped into the people's long-standing grievances, and convinced the Libirans not only to crown him as their young ruler, but to declare war and annexation against the Capital—pushing the surrounding kingdoms to do the same.

=== Era V: The 7-Year Great War, Dual Infusion & The Joint Realm Settlement

1. **The Six Fractured Powers:** Usuru's uprising ignited **The Great War**, tearing six major powers and territories into open conflict:
* **The Libira Kingdom** (led by young ruler Usuru)
* **The Capital** (defended by Prince Mi-ren)
* **The Great Mau Kingdom**
* **Old Sacyra**
* **The Academy** (led by Prince Casatti)
* **The Yun-yin Provinces**
2. **Casatti's Infusion of Tenebris (The Sword of War):** Driven by the sword, Prince Casatti wanted to put a swift end to Usuru's campaign to return the kingdom to peace. Combining his years of studying the Dreamscape, the elemental properties of the world, and the strange energy spike released during the ritual interruption, Casatti learned how to channel **Tenebris** and carefully infused it into the mundane sword of War to enact **Absolute Justice**.
3. **Mi-ren's Discovery of Lux (The Shield of Peace):** Prince Mi-ren had to take command of an army, but his goal was to restore peace to the realm and earn **forgiveness from Usuru** for the unintended betrayal of trust. Drawing upon what he had learned from the ritual used to project the First King into the temporal world, Mi-ren discovered a new form of energy—**Lux**—and channeled it into the mundane shield of Peace.
4. **Why the War Lasted Only 7 Years & The Political Settlement:** Lux possessed a unique property: it was the only energy capable of tempering Tenebris, though any violent emotional shift would cause Lux to dissipate just as it did on the day the ritual broke. Paired together, Lux and Tenebris brought The Great War to a close in just **seven years**, solidifying the **"Joint Realm, Separate Kingdoms"** political structure seen throughout the game—where autonomous provinces and kingdoms share a federated realm while retaining distinct local rule.

=== Era VI: The Prophecy of Kings

Because the Shield and the Sword were the original symbols of Peace and War chosen by the First King—whose sons' ideologies manifested into the cosmic artifacts of the **Shield of Lux** and **Sword of Tenebris**—their pairing became known throughout history as the **Prophecy of Kings**. Over the centuries following his defeat, Usuru's unhealed bitterness festered in the abyss until he became known to history simply as **The Dark Lord**.

== 5. Continuity Bridge: Historical Parallels & Inversions in the 2017–2018 Demo

=== 5.1. Usuru's Strategic Calculus: The Festival as a Metaphysical Siege Window

In Act I of the demo (`Map 002: Start-Up / Walk of Fear` and `Map 003`), Senior Outrider **Sir Roté** rushes to Sacyra Castle in terror: _"The storm... The rising island... All of these events... All before the Festival of Heroes. The king will know how to stop this, he must know..."_.

* **Exploiting the Thinned Veil:** The modern **Festival of Heroes** is the direct descendant of the ancient **Festival of Peoples**. Usuru (**The Dark Lord**) was defeated years ago, as **Monica the Mystic** recalls in Dio Village, but he purposefully times his new manifestation to coincide with the festival week when the veil to the Dreamscape is at its thinnest.
* **The Dream Eater Vanguard:** In the opening tutorial (`Map 006` / `Map 008`), **Dream Eaters** invade the Dreamscape crying _"feast! Our lord commands us too!"_ and _"Foolish mortal. We will return. Our lord demands it!"_. They are Usuru's advance scouts sent across the thinning veil to overwhelm **Chrono** and devour the ancestral records before the festival begins.

=== 5.2. King Jihad as the Grotesque Inverse of the Founding Dynasty

== 6. Ludological Architecture: Relics, Side Quests & Ending Routes

=== 6.1. Tri-Perspective Narrative Architecture

The canonical story of *Tenebris and Lux* is an unfolding mystery that requires synthesis across perspectives:

1. **Elijah Ro-enheart (`\n[1]` — Vanguard of Lux):** Investigates the military and institutional conspiracy surrounding his vanished father, **Commander Gareth Ro-enheart**, and King Jihad's Royal Guard.
2. **Lady Katrina Jaren (`\n[2]` — Outrider of Tenebris):** Investigates the aristocratic, societal, and underground corruption alongside the Adventurers' Guild and figures like John the Thief.
3. **The Custom Bearer (`<chosen_name>` — Secret Chronicler Route):** Using the engine's `name <str>` customization, the player can decouple the relics from the default protagonists. While playing both Elijah and Katrina unlocks the canonical **True Ending**, a custom bearer who puts in the "double work" along the `[-100, +100]` Moral Sway continuum weaves along the canonical timeline, catching fragments of both stories to unlock the **Secret True Ending**.

=== 6.2. Early Relic Acquisition & The Embellishment System

Elijah and Katrina acquire the ancient relics—the **Shield of Lux** and the **Sword of Tenebris**—at the very beginning of the game alongside **Grandma's Watch** (which activates the Layer 0 `time_utils` 24-hour monotonic clock across Seconds, Minutes, Hours, Days, Weeks, Months, and Seasons) and the **Compass of Morality**.

Just as Mi-ren and Casatti infused the original mundane weapons with their convictions, players choose how to **embellish** their relics through side quests, elemental weaves, and moral choices:

* **Canonical Core Combat Verbs (DevLog 008 Baseline):**
** **Defend (`Lux Barrio`):** Rolls a deterministic `2 ... 5` turn duration, granting `25%` incoming damage mitigation and shifting moral alignment toward virtue.
** **Attack (`Cuttatio Tenebris`):** Strikes primary target and deals `20%` area splash cleave to adjacent enemies.
** **Special (`Basic Magic`, `Basic Ward`, `Basic Charm`, and `Duo-Loca Bashin n Slash`):** *Duo-Loca Bashin n Slash* harmonizes both relics in **Stance of Equilibrium**, dynamically targeting the enemy's lower defensive stat (`DEF` vs. `MDF`).
* **Provincial Side Quests as Relic Embellishments:**
** **Eric's Test of Honesty (`Map 028`):** Filling Eric's pot to cover two months of rent rather than stealing from it shifts Moral Sway positively and rewards the **Ring of Fire** (_"I used to be a lumberjack until that weird portal appeared. I found this ring and held on to it every since"_), embellishing the relics with fire-weave properties (connecting to Folder 2's *Burning Tile Spread* LeTBS state).
** **Monica's Offering to Goddess Lyri (`Map 027` / `Map 029`):** Stopping the kid blocking the road (`Troop 5: Random Kid / Fire Mago`) and purchasing a base potion from the newly arrived shopkeeper for **Lyri, the Goddess of Peace**, grants the **Book of Monsters** and attunes the relics to spiritual/arcane resonance.
** **John the Thief vs. The St. Baron (`Map 030` / `Map 031`):** Helping John confront the greedy St. Baron (_"the Baron has outlived his welcome. Think you can help with this...issue"_) versus upholding feudal property laws shapes the stealth/penetration or bastion/authority embellishments of the weapons.
** **Armory Relics:** Discovering **Medusa's Sword** and the **Heart Shield** provides further optional material infusions for petrification/stasis or vitality warding.

=== 6.3. Complete Ending Matrix & Player Agency Over Lore

By design, the main story is linear from start to finish so that any player can complete a coherent tactical campaign, while **Dreamscape side quests** remain optional, time-sensitive (tied to the monotonic clock), and missable. You cannot achieve `100%` completion without the Dreamscape quests, giving the player full agency to decide *whether they want to know the deeper story*:
