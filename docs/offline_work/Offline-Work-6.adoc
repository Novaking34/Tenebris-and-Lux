=== 2.1. Bidirectional Stat Coupling (Combat ↔ Exploration)

Your **Class** powers the **Core 9 Combat Stats** on the 2D LeTBS tactical grid and in side-scroll action duels, while your **Job** powers the **8 Exploration Stats** used to traverse provinces, solve dungeon puzzles, and navigate faction politics:

* **`ATK` ↔ `STR` (Strength):** Physical weapon damage and armor sundering ↔ shoving boulders, forcing jammed iron portcullises, chopping timber, and inventory carry weight.
* **`AGI` ↔ `DEX` (Dexterity):** Turn timeline velocity, evasion, and grid movement points ↔ lockpicking, disarming mechanical traps, stealth tread, and clearing chasms.
* **`HP / DEF` ↔ `VIT` (Vitality):** Life capacity, physical mitigation, and poise resistance ↔ overland travel stamina and immunity to briers, miasma, and spoiled rations.
* **`MATK / MP` ↔ `INT` (Intelligence):** Arcane spell potency and mana pool ("Science") ↔ translating dead scripts, runic logic puzzles, and alchemical distillation.
* **`MDEF` ↔ `WIS` (Wisdom):** Spiritual warding and ailment resistance ("Magic") ↔ detecting NPC deception, sensing Dreamscape tears, and non-magical wound triage.
* **`ACC / CRIT` ↔ `PER` (Perception):** Flanking critical hit rates and line-of-sight scopes ↔ spotting concealed chests, darkvision radius, and tracking wilderness quarry.
* **`TP` ↔ `CHA` (Charisma):** Combat momentum generation and summon duration ↔ merchant bartering, companion morale, and pacifying wild fauna.
* **`Relic Resonance` ↔ `REP` (Reputation):** *Lux Barrio* and *Cuttatio Tenebris* synergy ↔ faction standing across Sacyra, bypassing military checkpoints, and court audiences.

=== 2.2. Diegetic Framing: "Unofficial" Story Identity vs. "Official" Hard Mode

The narrative of *Tenebris and Lux* operates across two planes: the historical waking campaign in Sacyra and the instructional framing archive inside **The Dreamscape** (Map 006: Room of Sages), where **Chrono the Sage** teaches a newly initiated **Mystic**.

. **Elijah Ro-enheart & Lady Katrina Jaren (Unofficial "Trainees"):** Inside the story, Elijah and Katrina are 15-to-18-year-old youths—Elijah is an unseasoned cadet grieving his vanished father (Commander Gareth), and Katrina is a runaway noblewoman trying to qualify for the Adventurers' Guild. Story-wise, neither is a master Warrior or Archmage yet; they are **Trainees** forced to learn steel, magic, and stealth simultaneously on the road.
. **The Custom Bearer `<chosen_name>` (Unofficial "Mystic"):** Story-wise, the Custom Player is a **new Mystic** being told the tale of *Tenebris and Lux* by Chrono, experiencing Elijah and Katrina's memories through the Ancient Book to complete their own ascension toward becoming a **Sage of Ancients**.
. **Why the Game Changes When It Becomes "Official":** On a first playthrough, touching a single Base Class stone (Warrior, Mage, or Rogue) gives the player "training wheels" with a standard single-domain EXP curve. Once the player beats the game (100% milestone) and unlocks the Special Classes, they can **officially** select **Trainee** or **Mystic** in New Game+—removing the training wheels, aligning their mechanical sheet with their true story identity, and enduring a slower multi-domain "Hard Mode" progression that leads directly to **The Hero / The Dark One** or **Sage of Ancients**.

==== New Game+ Carryover Rules

* **Unlocked Base Persistence:** Any Base Class unlocked in a previous campaign (*Warrior, Mage, Rogue, Priest, Summoner*, plus *Trainee* and *Mystic*) is available from minute one in the Room of Sages on NG+.
* **15–25% Mastery Head Start ("Attunement Resonance"):** Re-selecting a class that was mastered in a prior campaign grants a **15% head start** on its mastery gauge (expanding to **25%** if mastered across both Elijah and Katrina's routes).
* **NG+ "Encore Mastery" Exclusive Skills:** Re-selecting a previously mastered Base Class in NG+ grants a pinnacle technique evolved from the 2017–2018 grimoire:
** **Warrior → Sovereign Phantom Slash:** Teleports behind a target ignoring ZOC, inflicts armor-piercing damage, and triggers a 1-tile *Intimidating Aura*.
** **Mage → Cataclysmic Convergence:** Detonates active *Ice Walls* or *Volcano Ground* tiles for double elemental burst while refunding `50%` MP.
** **Rogue → Shadow Transposition:** Steals an enemy's active buff/item while swapping grid coordinates with them and planting an *Explosive Mark* on the vacated cell.
** **Priest → Chrono's Emergency Decree:** Grants an ally an immediate out-of-turn *Emergency Turn* while cleansing all debuffs and applying *Basic Ward*.
** **Summoner → Astral Triad Covenant:** Summons two auxiliary spirits simultaneously (e.g., *Sentinel Totem* + *Pixie*) that inherit `25%` of the Summoner's DEF and MDEF.

== 6. Grand Tour Architecture & Two-Pass Spiral World Design

In game and narrative architecture, sending the player across the entire world map during Act I without letting them stay in any single region long enough to fully understand it is known as a **Grand Tour Architecture** (or **Two-Pass Spiral World Design**).

Within the specific lore and mechanics of *Tenebris and Lux*, this structure serves a deliberate dual purpose: mechanically, it establishes the pre-war baseline of the continent before the 50% midpoint shatters the realm; narratively, it forces the player to unconsciously re-enact Usuru’s childhood "Three-Day Rule".

=== 6.1. Formal Classification of the Structure

. **The "Grand Tour" / Reconnaissance Circuit (Breadth-Before-Depth Topology):**
** **Pass 1 (Act I) is Horizontal and Shallow:** You touch every major hub, cross the borders of the Six Powers (Central Sacyra, Libira, Great Mau, Old Sacyra, The Academy, and the Yun-yin Provinces), and catch brief glimpses of their cultures and tensions while the itinerary keeps you moving.
** **Pass 2 (Act II) is Vertical and Deep:** After the midpoint catastrophe, you return to those exact same regions—now transformed by war and time—to live in them, excavate their secrets, and resolve their root conflicts.
. **"Establish and Rupture" (The *World of Balance → World of Ruin* Pattern):** Seen in *Dragon Quest XI* (collecting orbs across Erdrea before the Fall of Yggdrasil), *Final Fantasy VI* (World of Balance vs. World of Ruin), *Tales of Symphonia*, and *Trails in the Sky FC*, the first act establishes the living world before shattering it.
. **Spiral World Design (Open-World Metroidvania Teasing):** Sweeping across high-level regions as unseasoned Tier 1 Trainees displays locked Academy tablets, cracked mines, and guarded checkpoints that tempt later return once Rank 5 Job Perks unlock.
. **The Picaresque / Itinerant Chronicle & Reconnaissance Loop:** Constant transit under an urgent Festival schedule turns the youths into transients and surface observers rather than settled masters.

=== 6.2. Why *Tenebris and Lux* Sends You Around the World Once in Act I

* **A. Re-Enacting Usuru’s "Three-Day Wanderer" Pilgrimage:** Growing up under Libira's *Three-Day Law*, young Usuru helped villagers for three days before leaving at dawn—long enough to hear a farmer's *"quiet complaint"* or spot a corrupt bailiff, never long enough to grasp a community's full truth. Pass 1 puts Elijah, Katrina, and `<chosen_name>` in those exact same transient boots.
* **B. Youthful Naivety & Surface Facades:** Teenaged Trainees mistake proud March banners for genuine strength and see noble vanity without understanding the 3,000-year **Bystander Corridor** pressures trapping each province—setting up Act II revelations (`"We walked right past this in Year 1"`).
* **C. Setting the Dominoes of the Six Powers:** Physically crossing Great Mau, Libira's courier gap, The Academy, and Old Sacyra makes the **2nd Great War** visceral rather than an abstract map war when the Throne Room clash detonates.
* **D. Recruiting the 5-Member LeTBS Squad:** Cross-continent travel naturally recruits **Sebastien** (back-alley Rogue), **Angela** (Academy Mage), and **Lucius** (parish Priest) to complete the 5-unit tactical party.
