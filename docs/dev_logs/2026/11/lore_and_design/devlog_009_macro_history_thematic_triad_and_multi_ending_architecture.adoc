== Abstract

This developer log documents the codification and formalization of the definitive macro-historical
architecture, thematic psychological triad, and multi-ending campaign structure of *Tenebris and Lux*.
Synthesizing the recovered 2017–2018 RPG Maker MV demo baseline (Folders 1 & 2) with the native ISO C11
simulation engine, this milestone formalizes the 3,000-year dynastic wave from the First King to King Jihad,
the psychological triad linking past (Usuru, Casatti, Mi-ren) to present (Elijah, Katrina, Custom Bearer),
the etiology of "The King's Curse," the evolution of Grandma's Watch into The Dream Compass, and the
five-route ending matrix.

== 1. Macro-Historical Canon: The 3,000-Year Dynastic Wave

Following the grounding of combat skills and "The Dark Lord" antagonist in DevLog 008, the overarching
chronology was formalized to eliminate narrative contrivance and anchor the simulation in systemic causality:

1. **The Inception of Sacyra (Era I–III):**
   - The Shield of Lux and Sword of Tenebris were originally forged as ordinary, non-magical symbols of
     Peace and War under the First King's golden reign.
   - Following the inaugural week-long Festival of Peoples, the First King collapsed into a coma, his spirit
     lucid across the gossamer veil in The Dreamscape.
   - His physical death during the 5th Festival inaugurated a protective vigil by teenage sons Mi-ren and
     Casatti, sustaining ethereal projections during festival week until Mi-ren officially came "of age."
2. **The 11th Year Rupture & The 7-Year Great War (Era IV–V):**
   - An eleven-year-old wanderer from remote, communication-starved Libira accidentally tampered with the
     projection ritual, permanently sealing the First King in The Dreamscape.
   - The shock birthed raw **Tenebris** into reality. Taking the name **Usuru**, the youth led a six-power
     coalition war against the Capital.
   - Prince Casatti infused Tenebris into the Sword of War for **Absolute Justice**; Prince Mi-ren discovered
     **Lux** to defend the realm and seek Usuru's forgiveness.
   - Demonstrating that Lux tempers Tenebris but dissipates under violent emotional shifts, the war concluded
     in seven years under the federated **"Joint Realm, Separate Kingdoms"** accord and the **Prophecy of Kings**.
3. **The 3,000-Year Dynastic Wave:**
   - Spanning 30 to 50 monarchs across three millennia, the succession oscillates between Long Stasis Crests
     (60–90+ years), Tether-Sickness Troughs (5–20 years), and Bureaucratic Consolidations (35–50 years).
   - This established order exposes frontier neglect (Dio Village) as systemic apathy and transforms
     King Jihad from a petty villain into an existential traitor.

== 2. The Thematic Triad: "Trauma is Inherited, Destiny is Chosen"

Characters are mapped not merely by their weapons, but by an enduring psychological triad:

* **The Frontier Wound:** **Usuru** (Past) mirrors **Elijah Ro-enheart** (Present). Both endure ten years
  of childhood reverence before experiencing a fracture in their tenth/eleventh year. While Usuru succumbed
  to wrath, Elijah raises the Shield of Lux with independent conscience, achieving redemptive grace.
* **The Renouncing Blade:** **Prince Casatti** (Past) mirrors **Lady Katrina Jaren** (Present). Both renounce
  high aristocratic privilege (the High Throne / House Jaren) in pursuit of empirical truth and meritocratic
  brotherhood (The Academy / The Adventurers' Guild), wielding the Sword of Tenebris to slice through hypocrisy.
* **The Steward of Balance:** **Prince Mi-ren** (Past) mirrors **The Custom Bearer (`<chosen_name>`)** (Present).
  Both bear the burden of holding a fractured world together across the veil, synthesizing both perspectives
  to achieve true Equilibrium.

== 3. Ludological Architecture & Multi-Ending Specification

1. **The King's Curse as a Planar Wound:**
   - Court madness is revealed not as a demonic hex, but as the open planar wound beneath the High Throne
     left when Usuru shattered the stasis ritual. King Jihad's megalomania inverted the conduit, leading to
     possession by the Dark Lord's psychic remnant.
2. **The Midpoint Fork & Player Consent:**
   - Defeating King Jihad triggers the campaign fork. Players skipping missable nocturnal Dreamscape quests
     reach the **Neutral Ending** (saving Sacyra while leaving the First King marooned and the cycle dormant).
   - Players completing nocturnal Dream Compass trials unlock **The True Ending ("The Ugly Truth")**, which
     dismantles the comfortable propaganda of both factions and reconciles the realm.
3. **The Five Ending Routes:**
   - *Route A:* The Canonical Harmony (True Equilibrium Ending — Elijah + Katrina tandem).
   - *Route B:* The Benevolent Stasis (Neutral-Good / The Mi-ren Fallacy — `+100` Moral Sway).
   - *Route C:* The Calcified Tyrant (Neutral-Bad / Inquisitorial Stasis — `-100` Moral Sway).
   - *Route D:* The Abyssal Usurper (Neutral-Bad / The Cycle of Usuru — `-100` Moral Sway).
   - *Route E:* The Secret Chronicler Ending (Custom Bearer 100% Route).

== 4. Verification Telemetry

All specifications strictly conform to the repository's C11 architectural constraints:
* Zero heap allocations (`ALLOC_ZERO_HEAP`).
* Layer 0 monotonic clock tracking seconds, minutes, hours, weekdays, weeks, and seasons.
* Layer 1 bounded simulation invariants: `player_state_t` moral sway `[-100, +100]`, shield power `[0, 100]`,
  and edge sharpness `[0, 100]`.
* 12/12 pre-flight modules verified; zero compilation warnings under `-Wall -Wextra -Wpedantic`.
