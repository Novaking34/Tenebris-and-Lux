== Abstract

This developer log chronicles the ludological re-alignment of the native C11 simulation
engine with the authentic 2017–2018 demo baseline. It details the transition from generic
combat verbs to concrete tactical skills: Defend (*Lux Barrio* with turn duration rolls
and $25\%$ damage mitigation), Attack (*Cuttatio Tenebris* with $20\%$ area splash cleave),
and Special (*Basic Magic*, *Basic Ward*, *Basic Charm*, and *Duo-Loca Bashin n Slash* with
adaptive defense targeting). Furthermore, it documents the critical correction of the
antagonist identity to **"The Dark Lord"** (the overarching background "A Plot"), the
reinstatement of **The Dreamscape** opening trial, and protagonist customization.

== 1. Lessons Learned: Game Developer vs. Software CRUD Thinking

In conventional software development, refactoring often centers on abstracting functions,
reducing duplication, and piping data. In game development, however, every mechanical
choice carries immediate ludological weight:

1. **Mechanical Texture Over Generic Verbs**:
   - Generic commands like "ward" and "strike" felt mechanically hollow without distinct
     tactical identities.
   - Transforming "Ward" into **`Lux Barrio`** introduces active damage mitigation ($-25\%$)
     and tactical duration rolls ($2 \dots 5$ turns). Defending ceases to be a passive stall
     and becomes a strategic barrier phase.
   - Transforming "Strike" into **`Cuttatio Tenebris`** with $20\%$ flank splash damage
     solves the issue of target tunneling, rewarding spatial positioning against swarms.

2. **Ground Truth Over Synthetic Assumptions**:
   - In earlier drafts, the antagonist was arbitrarily labeled "Dark Lord Malakor".
     Forensic inspection of the original 2017–2018 demo revealed that in canon he was
     consistently referred to as **"The Dark Lord"**.
   - The Dark Lord is an omnipresent cosmic presence ("The A Plot") menacing from afar,
     while immediate gameplay focuses on regional factions, village survival, and
     ancient trials. Rushing into the final boss ruins narrative pacing.

3. **Narrative Pacing & The Dreamscape**:
   - The game does not begin in a high-stakes fortress war; it begins in **The Dreamscape**
     (guided by Chrono facing Dream Eaters), allowing the player to awaken spiritually
     before physically waking up in Dio Village at Grandma Magolia's hearth.
