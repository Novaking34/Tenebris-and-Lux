# World-Building & Lore Bedrock: The Cosmology of Lux & Tenebris

> **Project**: Tenebris and Lux  
> **Volume**: 01 — World & Lore Foundations  
> **Status**: Living Canon  

---

## 1. The Great Axiom: Light is Life, Shadow is Entropy

In the cosmos of *Tenebris and Lux*, the universe does not consist of inert matter, but of a precarious equilibrium between two primal states:

- **Lux (The Primordial Radiance)**:
  - The source of form, memory, cohesion, and warm life.
  - Lux is not infinite; it decays in the presence of time and silence.
  - When Lux is concentrated, thought is sharp, reality is stable, and stone chambers hold their physical integrity.
- **Tenebris (The Creeping Veil)**:
  - The cold, encroaching void of oblivion and entropy.
  - Tenebris is patient. It does not rage; it seeps into quiet crevices, dulling memory and dissipating light into cold gray stillness.
  - Total absence of Lux ($0$ Lux) causes complete dissolution into the veil.

---

## 2. The Artifacts: Lumina Shards

Scattered throughout ancient stone chambers lie **Lumina Shards**—crystalline petrifications of primordial light that fell when the first sanctuaries fractured:

- **Kindling**: When crushed between the palms of a traveler, a Lumina Shard releases its dormant luminescent energy, restoring vitality and dispelling encroaching shadows.
- **Scarcity & Value**: Lumina Shards cannot be created anew; they can only be recovered from the forgotten chambers where ancient keepers once stored them.

---

## 3. The World Topography: The Fading Sanctuaries

The world is composed of subterranean ruins and sacred geometries built by an extinct order of Lightbearers:

1. **The Fading Sanctuary**: The starting point; a cracked stone altar with dying embers offering temporary respite.
2. **The Corridor of Shadows**: A transitional labyrinth where shadows stretch unnaturally along damp stone walls.
3. **The Hall of Whispers**: A cavernous hall resonant with acoustic echoes that carry fragments of ancient knowledge and warning.
4. **The Chamber of Ancient Runes**: A central sanctum inscribed with luminous glyphs that channel the deeper currents of the subterranean world.

---

## 4. Expanding the World

As development progresses, the world will expand across four axes:

1. **The Inhabitants**: Shadow-wraiths, blind sentinels, and lingering spectral echoes.
2. **The Mechanics**: Light refraction puzzles, mirror conduits, and darkness curses.
3. **The Relics**: Lanterns, prism staves, and sanctified oils.
4. **The Map**: Branching catacombs, sunken vaults, and the legendary Solar Spire.
