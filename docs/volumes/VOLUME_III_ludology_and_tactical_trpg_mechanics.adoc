== Chapter 1: Dual-Layer TRPG Combat Architecture

== Overview

This document specifies the dual-layer combat architecture of *Tenebris and Lux*:
a **Tactical Strategy RPG (TRPG)** featuring a **grid-based tactical battlefield** coupled with
**cinematic traditional side-scroll / action-based combat resolution**. Conceived during the 2014–2018
RPG Maker genesis of CreativeWorkzStudio, this design bridges grand tactical positioning with visceral,
action-driven 2D engagement.

== 1. Dual-Layer Battle Architecture

The combat engine operates across two seamless, interconnected operational loops:

```text
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                   PHASE 1: TACTICAL GRID PHASE                         │
│  Tile Movement, Line-of-Sight, Terrain Defenses, Flanking & Squad ZOC  │
└───────────────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────────────┘
                                    │ Target Engagement
                                    ▼
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│              PHASE 2: CINEMATIC COMBAT RESOLUTION (SIDE-VIEW)          │
│  2D Action Clashes, Timed Wards, Severance Slashes & Stance Techniques │
└───────────────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────────────┘
                                    │ Resolution & Damage Sync
                                    ▼
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                  PERSISTENT SIMULATION STATE UPDATE                    │
│   HP Deductions, Stagger States, Relic Resonance & Moral Sway Deltas   │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

== 2. Phase 1: Grid-Based Tactical Battlefield

The overarching strategic layer takes place upon a discrete 2D grid map representing the castles,
frontier passes, and battlegrounds of the **Kingdom of Sacyra**.

=== 1. Grid Topography & Spatial Invariants

* **Coordinate Space**: Discrete integer grid `(grid_x, grid_y)`.
* **Unit Footprint**: Standard units occupy a $1 \times 1$ grid tile; major boss constructs
  (such as The Dark Lord's Siege Automata) occupy $2 \times 2$ or $3 \times 3$ footprints.
* **Terrain Types & Modifiers**:
  - **Stone Flagstones / Road**: $1.0\times$ movement cost, $+0\%$ evasion.
  - **Ramparts / Elevated Battlement**: $1.5\times$ movement cost, $+20\%$ physical defense,
    $+1$ attack range for ranged skills.
  - **Frontier Thicket / Rubble**: $2.0\times$ movement cost, $+15\%$ evasion, blocks cavalry charge.
  - **Void Mire (Abyssal Blight)**: $2.5\times$ movement cost, inflicts $-5$ HP decay
    per turn on non-void units.

=== 2. Movement & Zone of Control (ZOC)

* **Movement Budget**: Units possess a base `movement_tiles` statistic (e.g. Katrina: 6 tiles; Elijah: 4 tiles in heavy armor).
* **Zone of Control**: Moving into an adjacent tile directly forward of a hostile soldier halts further movement for that turn, anchoring frontline formations.
* **Directional Facing**: Units finish their turn facing one of four cardinal directions (`NORTH`, `SOUTH`, `EAST`, `WEST`). Attacking an enemy from their flank grants $+15\%$ critical chance; attacking from the rear guarantees an unguardable strike.

=== 3. Turn Sequencing

* **Dynamic Initiative Order**: Turn execution is governed by unit Agility. Fast skirmishers (like Katrina) act earlier in the round, while heavy vanguard armorers act later with higher defensive resilience.

== 3. Phase 2: Cinematic Combat Resolution (Side-View Action)

When an attacker initiates an assault or when two opposing units clash on the grid, the game seamlessly
transitions into a dedicated **2D Side-View Cinematic Battle Scene**.

=== 1. Visual & Staging Presentation

* The attacker approaches from the left (player units) or right (enemy units) across a dynamic 2D battlefield backdrop matching the current terrain (e.g., Sunken Citadel battlements, ash-strewn frontier).
* Character sprites render with expressive combat animations: Idle combat stance, forward dash, heavy strike, shield block, recoil, stagger, and victory flourishes.

=== 2. Combat Mechanics During Cinematic Resolution

1. **Active Input & Timed Agency**:
   - Rather than passive numbers scrolling past, combat resolution incorporates timed player inputs:
     - **Timed Ward (Lux Guard)**: Pressing the ward button precisely upon enemy weapon contact triggers a **Perfect Deflection**, absorbing 100% of incoming damage and staggering the foe.
     - **Twilight Severance (Tenebris Strike)**: Pressing strike during the forward dash activates a **Deep Cleave**, bypassing a percentage of enemy armor.
2. **Stagger & Poise Meter**:
   - Every battler possesses a **Poise / Stagger Gauge** ($0 \dots 100$).
   - Repeated heavy strikes or shield bashes deplete enemy poise. When poise hits zero, the target enters **Staggered Vulnerability**, leaving them defenseless against critical finishers.
3. **Stance Animation Morphing**:
   - The character's active stance directly dictates their cinematic combat posture and move set:
     - `STANCE_WARDING`: Elijah locks the Shield of Lux into the earth; golden starlight forms a kinetic buckler.
     - `STANCE_STRIKING`: Katrina or Elijah lowers their center of gravity, dragging the Sword of Tenebris as twilight embers trail the blade.
     - `STANCE_EQUILIBRIUM`: Fluid parry-and-counter sequencing utilizing both armaments in rapid harmony.

The tactical depth of *Tenebris and Lux* emerges from combining the distinct capabilities of Elijah and Katrina:

=== Tandem Linked Combat (The Duo Duel)

When Elijah and Katrina are positioned on adjacent grid tiles and either initiates combat, both heroes
enter the cinematic side-view battle scene together:

* **Shield & Sword Flank**: Elijah executes an *Aegis Slam* that knocks the enemy backward, launching them into Katrina's airborne *Crescent Sever*.
* **Harmonized Equilibrium**: Both heroes combine the radiance of Lux and the edge of Tenebris, unleashing a dual finishing art that sweeps across multiple targets.

== 5. Persistent State & Moral Sway Feedback

Upon completion of the cinematic resolution:

1. **HP & Wound Conservation**: Remaining unit health updates onto the tactical grid. Fallen units are removed or enter down-but-not-dead states.
2. **Relic Resonance Tracking**: Energy expended (`shield_power`, `sword_edge`) updates in persistent memory (`player_state_t`).
3. **Moral Sway Evaluation**:
   - Choosing to spare routed enemy captains, warding retreating militia, or using non-lethal shield stagger tilts sway toward **Virtue (+)**.
   - Executing fleeing enemies with abyssal severance or utilizing forbidden void arts tilts sway toward **Malice (-)**.
   - Moral sway unlocks distinct strategic faction options across the Kingdom of Sacyra.

== Overview

This document specifies the core ludological mechanics of *Tenebris and Lux* in the **Kingdom of Sacyra**
and **The Dreamscape**: the twin primordial relics (**The Shield of Lux** and **The Sword of Tenebris**),
the dynamic combat stance state machine, the moral sway continuum (`[-100, +100]`), and the tactical action
skills: **Defend** (Lux Barrio), **Attack** (Cuttatio Tenebris), and **Special** (Basic Magic, Basic Ward,
Basic Charm, and Duo-Loca Bashin n Slash).

== 1. Primordial Neutrality & Relic Mechanics

Neither relic is inherently good nor evil. They are manifestations of cosmic apex forces whose morality is
dictated solely by the intentions and choices of their wielders—canonically **Elijah Ro-enheart** and
**Katrina Jaren**, or a customizable bearer chosen by the player:

[cols="1,2,2", options="header"]
|===
| Relic | The Shield of Lux | The Sword of Tenebris
| **Aspect** | Apex Radiance & Cohesion | Apex Entropy & Dissolution
| **Energy Gauge** | `shield_power` ($0 \dots 100$) | `sword_edge` ($0 \dots 100$)
| **Canonical Bearer** | **Elijah Ro-enheart** (Vanguard Defense) | **Katrina Jaren** (Agile Severance)
| **Grid Role** | Zone-of-Control denial, sanctuary aura, ally protection | High-mobility flanking, armor penetration, mist evasion
| **Cinematic Action** | Timed kinetic blocks, shield-bashes, radiant shockwaves | Rapid dash strikes, armor severance, twilight blade waves
| **Resource Dynamics** | Regenerates on guard; absorbs incoming assault | Consumes edge on strikes; hones outside combat
|===

== 2. The Combat Stance State Machine

The battle posture shifts dynamically across four discrete combat stances:

[source,text]
----
                   ┌───────────────────┐
                   │  STANCE_NEUTRAL   │
                   └─────────┬─────────┘
                             │
         ┌───────────────────┼───────────────────┐
         ▼                   ▼                   ▼
┌─────────────────┐ ┌─────────────────┐ ┌─────────────────┐
│ STANCE_WARDING  │ │ STANCE_STRIKING │ │STANCE_EQUILIBRIUM
│ (Shield Guard)  │ │ (Sword Drawn)   │ │ (Twin Harmony)  │
└─────────────────┘ └─────────────────┘ └─────────────────┘
----

1. **`STANCE_NEUTRAL`**: Relics held at waist. Balanced baseline.
   - Standard movement on the tactical grid; baseline attack parameters.
2. **`STANCE_WARDING`**: Shield raised in protective vanguard high-guard.
   - Grid Effect: Increases allied defense within adjacent tiles by $+25\%$.
   - Combat Effect: Boosts Shield power recovery ($+15$), increases stagger damage against attacking foes ($25$ HP), and wards against incoming status curses.
3. **`STANCE_STRIKING`**: Greatsword held in aggressive two-handed assault posture.
   - Grid Effect: Increases unit movement by $+1$ tile and grants armor-piercing flank bonuses.
   - Combat Effect: Increases strike damage from $30$ to $45$ (or base $+15$ bonus); reduces edge cost from $12$ to $8$ points.
4. **`STANCE_EQUILIBRIUM`**: Harmonized twin-relic synchronization.
   - Grid Effect: Immunity to positioning debuffs and void mire terrain penalties.
   - Combat Effect: Simultaneously restores $+15$ Shield power and $+15$ Sword edge; parries incoming strikes while counter-attacking for $20$ damage; nudges moral sway toward center ($0$).

== 3. The Moral Sway Continuum & Decoupled Elemental Expression

The moral trajectory of Sacyra's champions is tracked along a continuous bounded integer axis:

[source,text]
----
[ -100: Sovereign of Ruin ] ◄──── [ 0: Equilibrium ] ────► [ +100: Saint of Mercy ]
                 ▲                                ▲
                 └─── [ -50 to +50: Bystander ] ──┘
----

=== 3.1. Decoupling Morality ("The Why") from Elemental Expression ("The How")
Neither Lux nor Tenebris dictates a character's moral alignment:
* **Virtuous Lux (`+1 ... +100`):** Selfless sanctuary, kinetic mitigation (*Lux Barrio*), and restorative peace.
* **Tyrannical Lux (`-100 ... -1`):** Blinding glare, totalitarian enforcement of rigid order, and calcifying living will into petrified stasis (The Mi-ren Fallacy).
* **Virtuous Tenebris (`+1 ... +100`):** Surgical severance (*Cuttatio Tenebris*), cutting corrupt bindings, and executing Absolute Justice without unnecessary bloodshed (The Casatti Ideal).
* **Malevolent Tenebris (`-100 ... -1`):** Creeping entropic rot, nihilistic bloodlust, and devouring devastation (The Dark Lord).

=== 3.2. The Bystander Corridor (`[-50, +50]`) as Net Moral Inertia
The `[-50, +50]` band represents **Net Moral Inertia**, where opposing deeds cancel out into zero net displacement. Staying within this corridor traps the protagonists in 3,000 years of repeating cyclical tragedy. Breaking past $\pm 50$ through deliberate micro-choices (such as aiding Eric or John in Dio Village) asserts heroic agency: *"Trauma is Inherited, Destiny is Chosen."*

=== 3.3. Equilibrium (`0`)
Maintained by active discipline rather than passive fence-sitting; balancing the blade and the aegis so that radiant light tempers the void without collapsing into calcified stasis or entropic rot. Unlocks the tandem secret art *Duo-Loca Bashin n Slash*.

== 4. Tactical Actions & Combat Skills

In the revised, authentic combat framework, basic actions expand into dedicated martial and arcane skills:

=== 4.1. Defend (`defend` / `ward`)
* **Standard Defend**: Braces behind the Shield of Lux, regenerating $+10 \dots +15$ shield power and mitigating incoming damage.
* **Basic Skill: `Lux Barrio` (`barrio`)**:
  - Manifests a radiant, geometric barrier of pure light.
  - **Effect**: Blocks incoming attack and reduces subsequent incoming damage by **$25\%$** for a rolled turn duration.
  - **Duration Roll**: Deterministically rolls for **$2 \dots 5$ turns** (`2 + (turns % 4)`).
  - **Resonance**: Saturates Shield of Lux by $+20$ points (capped at $100$) and awards $+3$ moral virtue.

=== 4.2. Attack (`attack` / `strike`)
* **Standard Attack**: Strikes the target enemy using the Sword of Tenebris, consuming edge points and dealing physical damage (`(ATK * 2) - DEF`).
* **Basic Skill: `Cuttatio Tenebris` (`cuttatio`)**:
  - A sweeping abyssal crescent slash infused with dark mist.
  - **Direct Damage**: Deals full primary severance damage to the selected target.
  - **Splash Damage**: Deals **$20\%$ splash damage** to surrounding and flanking foes (`threat_surrounding_hp`).
  - **Edge Dynamic**: Consumes $8 \dots 12$ edge points.

=== 4.3. Special (`special` / `balance`)
Replaces balance with a four-fold technique tree honoring the twin relics and arcane arts:

1. **`Basic Magic` (`magic`)**:
   - Neutral elemental magic damage attacking enemy Magic Defense (`(MAT * 2) - MDF`).
   - Costs $10$ MP. Pure, unaligned cosmic energy.
2. **`Basic Ward` (`ward` under special)**:
   - Magic-based defend weaving an arcane barrier.
   - Restores $+15$ Shield power and staggers incoming assault without relying purely on physical bracing.
   - Costs $6$ MP.
3. **`Basic Charm` (`charm`)**:
   - Enhances astral focus, granting **$+20\%$ Magic Attack (MAT)** for **$1 \dots 3$ turns** (`1 + (turns % 3)`).
   - Costs $8$ MP.
4. **`Duo-Loca Bashin n Slash` (`duo`)**:
   - The signature dual-relic strike uniting the Shield of Lux and Sword of Tenebris.
   - **Adaptive Damage**: Intelligently evaluates whether the player's Magic Attack or Physical Attack is superior, or whichever enemy defense (DEF vs MDF) is lower, channeling into the foe's weakest resistance:
     * If enemy MDF < DEF or MAT > ATK: executes as **Magical Twilight Slash**.
     * Otherwise: executes as **Physical Radiant Bash**.
   - **Relic Harmony**: Simultaneously restores $+15$ Shield power and $+15$ Sword edge, while nudging moral sway toward absolute cosmic equilibrium ($0$).
   - Costs $12$ MP.

=== 4.4. Hero Customization & Protagonist Lore
* While **Elijah Ro-enheart** and **Katrina Jaren** remain the canonical dual protagonists representing the Shield of Lux and Sword of Tenebris, the player can dynamically designate their own hero identity using the `name <chosen_name>` command.
* All narrative chronicle logs, character severity tags, and telemetry traces adapt cleanly to the bearer's active name.

== 5. The Metaphysical Law of Tempering & Dissipation

The interaction between the Shield of Lux and the Sword of Tenebris is governed by the ancient law established during the 7-Year Great War:

* **Lux as the Singular Tempering Agent:** Raw Tenebris is entropic and severing; left unchecked, it erodes both target and wielder. Lux is the only force capable of containing and tempering Tenebris, providing structural cohesion so that void strikes can be directed safely.
* **The Fragility of Radiance (Dissipation):** While Lux tempers Tenebris, it requires emotional and ethical stability. Any sudden surge of terror, malice, or panic causes Lux to **dissipate immediately**, collapsing protective barriers and leaving Tenebris unbridled.
* **Ludonarrative Impact:** In combat, executing dark commands with negative moral sway degrades Lux Barrio duration rolls, while maintaining Equilibrium (`0` sway) maximizes barrier resilience and tandem skill execution.

== 6. The Relic Embellishment System

Throughout the campaign, the Shield of Lux and Sword of Tenebris are actively embellished through quest outcomes, moral choices, and auxiliary relics:

1. **Eric's Test of Honesty (Map 028) $\to$ The Ring of Fire:**
   - Donating rent to Eric rewards the **Ring of Fire**, imbuing strikes and barriers with flame-weave properties, connecting directly to the LeTBS *Burning Tile Spread* tactical state.
2. **Monica's Offering (Maps 027/029) $\to$ The Book of Monsters:**
   - Sponsoring an offering to **Lyri, Goddess of Peace**, attunes the relics to arcane resonance and awards the **Book of Monsters**, unlocking advanced MDF-targeting formulas.
3. **John the Thief vs. St. Baron (Maps 030/031):**
   - Aiding John enhances critical severance and stealth penetration; upholding feudal property law reinforces kinetic poise and bastion authority.
4. **Armory Relics (Medusa's Sword & Heart Shield):**
   - Optional material infusions granting petrification stasis or passive HP vitality warding.

== Chapter 3: Campaign Architecture, The King's Curse & Multi-Ending Specification

== Overview & Ludological Philosophy

This specification articulates the structural macro-architecture of the campaign in *Tenebris and Lux*,
including the two-half campaign progression, the metaphysical etiology of "The King's Curse," the midpoint
narrative fork, player agency over lore discovery, and the complete five-route ending matrix. Built upon
the premise of ludonarrative consent, the engine allows casual players to experience a coherent linear
tactical campaign, while reserving the mythological truth for players who actively engage with nocturnal
Dreamscape trials.

== 1. The Two-Half Campaign Structure

The overarching campaign is organized into two distinct dramatic and mechanical halves:

[source,text]
----
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                   ACT I: THE SACYRAN SUCCESSION CRISIS                 │
│  • Setting: Dio Village, Whispering Woods, Capital Outposts            │
│  • Antagonist Focus: King Jihad & Corrupt Royal Court                  │
│  • Themes: Provincial neglect, family mysteries, state censorship     │
│  • Climax: The Festival of Heroes & Siege of Sacyra Castle             │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                   MIDPOINT FORK: THE KING'S CURSE                      │
│  • Defeat of King Jihad reveals possession by The Dark Lord's remnant  │
│  • Structural branching based on nocturnal Dream Compass completion    │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                   ACT II: THE COSMIC RECKONING                         │
│  • Setting: Sacyra Marches, Abyssal Vaults, The Dreamscape Core        │
│  • Antagonist Focus: The Dark Lord (Usuru's Ancient Void Essence)      │
│  • Themes: Severance vs. Cohesion, Atonement, Planar Reconciliation   │
│  • Climax: Confrontation at the Cosmic Threshold & Ending Resolution   │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
----

=== 1.1. Act I: Stopping King Jihad (The Sovereign of Ambition)
Act I centers on terrestrial, socio-political conflict. Operating from Dio Village through the Whispering
Woods and Capital marches, the party uncovers King Jihad's treasonous suppression of border alerts. The
act culminates during the **Festival of Heroes** at Sacyra Castle, where King Jihad is confronted and defeated.

=== 1.2. The Etiology of "The King's Curse" & Royal Possession
Court superstition long spoke of a hereditary madness afflicting monarchs of Sacyra. Forensic narrative
architecture reveals that "The King's Curse" is not a demonic hex, but an active **metaphysical hazard**:

* **The Open Planar Wound:** When eleven-year-old Usuru shattered the First King's stasis projection ritual
  3,000 years ago, a jagged planar rift tore open directly beneath the dias of the **High Throne**.
* **Psychic Backwash:** For three millennia, reigning monarchs sitting upon the High Throne were subjected
  to perpetual psychic shear and planar backwash from across the veil.
* **Jihad's Fatal Gambit:** Seeking to usurp the Dark Lord's void power during the thinned veil of the
  Festival of Heroes, King Jihad intentionally opened the conduit beneath his throne. Because the ethereal
  medium mirrors mortal intent, Jihad's megalomania inverted the flow, allowing a dormant psychic remnant
  of Usuru (**The Dark Lord**) to possess his body.

== 2. The Midpoint Fork: Neutral Path vs. True Ending Path

Defeating King Jihad reveals the void possession, forcing the narrative into its primary structural fork:

[source,text]
----
                         ACT I CLIMAX: DEFEAT OF KING JIHAD
                   Possession Revealed via "The King's Curse"
                                       │
            ┌──────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────┐
            ▼                                                     ▼
   THE NEUTRAL PATH (Basic Completion)                   THE TRUE ENDING PATH (100% Synthesis)
   • Dreamscape side quests missed/skipped.              • Nocturnal Dream Compass quests completed.
   • Treats possession as an external demon hex.         • Recognizes the severed ritual's energy signature.
   • Marches to slay or supplant The Dark Lord.          • Uncovers the First King's seal & Usuru's grief.
            │                                                     │
     ┌──────┴──────┐                                       ┌──────┴──────┐
     ▼             ▼                                       ▼             ▼
"Good" Ending  "Bad" Ending                        Canonical Harmony  Secret Chronicler
(Heroic Myth)  (Tyrant/Usurper)                    (Elijah + Katrina) (Custom Bearer)
----

=== 2.1. Why Basic Completion Is the Neutral Ending
By design, any player who simply plays the main story linearly from start to finish will reach the end
of the campaign. However, skipping the missable nocturnal **Dream Compass** trials produces the **Neutral Ending**:

* The player defeats King Jihad and destroys The Dark Lord's physical manifestation.
* The kingdom is saved in the present, but the **First King remains permanently marooned** in The Dreamscape.
* Usuru's ancient wound of betrayal is left unhealed, and the systemic neglect between the Capital and frontier
  provinces remains unchanged, ensuring the cycle will repeat.

=== 2.2. Player Consent to Know the Deeper Lore
Video games are distinguished by interactive agency. Forcing forty minutes of historical exposition down the
player's throat violates ludic pacing. By gating the full mythological truth behind optional, nocturnal
Dreamscape trials, the game honors the player's agency: players who desire a fast-paced tactical campaign
can enjoy a satisfying heroic conclusion, while players who actively investigate the mystery are rewarded
with the full mythological reality.

=== 2.3. Why the True Ending Is "The Ugly Truth"
The canonical True Ending is neither a simplistic fairy-tale triumph nor a dark nihilistic collapse. It is
**"The Ugly Truth"** that dismantles the self-serving propaganda of both sides:

* It exposes that Prince Mi-ren's loving preservation created a catastrophic institutional lie that deceived
  a continent for six festivals.
* It acknowledges that Usuru's childhood outrage was entirely valid before his unguided fury curdled into
  monstrous destruction.
* It reveals that Sacyra's three millennia of golden peace were bought at the cost of deliberate neglect
  toward frontier provinces like Libira and Dio Village.

== 3. Relic Embellishment & The Ten-Ending Campaign Matrix

Throughout both acts, the player's choices, side quests, and stance preferences actively **embellish**
the Shield of Lux and Sword of Tenebris along the `[-100, +100]` **Moral Sway** continuum.

=== 3.1. Telemetry Over Point Locks & The Bystander Corridor
Players do not aim for brittle, exact integers to unlock endings. Instead, `moral_sway` acts as
continuous behavioral telemetry showing where the player's philosophy is steering the world:

* **The Bystander Corridor (`[-50, +50]`):** Represents **Net Moral Inertia**—a mutual cancellation trap
  where opposing moral vectors offset each other. Floating in this corridor produces status-quo stagnation
  or repeating cycles.
* **The Threshold of Conviction:** Escaping the bystander corridor requires sustained virtue (`[+50, +100]`)
  or despotic malice (`[-100, -50]`).
* **Equilibrium (`0`):** An active, razor-thin discipline where Lux and Tenebris temper each other without
  collapsing into calcification or entropic rot.

=== 3.2. The 3x3 Ending Matrix + The 10th True Ending

Combining the three elemental expressions (**Lux**, **Tenebris**, and **Statera** / Equilibrium) with
the three moral bands (**Good** `[+50, +100]`, **Neutral** `[-50, +50]`, and **Bad** `[-100, -50]`)
produces nine distinct campaign resolutions, crowned by a tenth synthesis ending:

[cols="1,2,2,2", options="header"]
|===
| Expression | Good (`[+50, +100]`) | Neutral (`[-50, +50]`) | Bad (`[-100, -50]`)

| **Lux** +
  _(Cohesion & Preservation)_
| **1. The Radiant Sanctuary:** +
  Selfless protection; the Shield of Lux shelters the realm and heals frontier wounds without imposing despotic rule.
| **2. The Benevolent Stasis (The Mi-ren Fallacy):** +
  Out of fear of conflict, the wielder seals Sacyra in an unchanging golden crystal cocoon where nothing harms—and nothing grows.

== Chapter 6: Continuous Invariant Testing Philosophy

*Source File*: `/home/professorseanex/Tenebris and Lux/docs/game_design/game_testing.adoc`

[abstract]
== Abstract

In classical software engineering, testing focuses primarily on stateless input/output validation,
API schemas, and unit function assertions (`assert(f(x) == y)`). In game development, testing
requires an entirely distinct paradigm: verifying continuous state continuity, economy balance,
softlock immunity, deterministic replay parity, and strict 60 FPS frame-budget headroom.
This document outlines the testing philosophy governing *Tenebris and Lux*.

== 1. The Fundamental Divergence

[cols="1,2,2", options="header"]
|===
| Dimension | Traditional Software Testing | Game Development Testing
| **State Nature** | Ephemeral, localized, often stateless | Continuous, cumulative, multi-turn state machine
| **Failure Modes** | Null pointer exceptions, HTTP 500s, syntax errors | Softlocks, economy collapse, degenerate gameplay loops, visual desync
| **Correctness Metric** | Does output match expected schema? | Does the game feel right? Is the state bounded, balanced, and unexploitable?
| **Input Cadence** | Controlled, structured API payloads | Unbounded, chaotic human keystrokes, rapid button mash, simultaneous events
| **Time Budget** | Milliseconds/seconds per request | 16.6 milliseconds total frame budget (60 FPS) with microseconds for simulation
|===

== 2. Core Pillars of Game Development Testing

=== Pillar 1: Dynamic State Invariants & Bounded Continuity

In a game like *Tenebris and Lux*, state variables (`shield_power`, `sword_edge`, `moral_sway`, `threat_hp`)
are constantly mutated across hundreds of player turns. A single off-by-one error or uncapped addition
will cause state drift, resulting in overflow, negative health zombies, or invulnerability exploits.

* **Invariant Axioms**:
  - `0 <= state.shield_power <= 100` must hold after every single action turn.
  - `0 <= state.sword_edge <= 100` must hold even when spamming strikes at zero energy.
  - `-100 <= state.moral_sway <= +100` must never overflow into undefined integers.
* **Diagnostic Tripwires**: The game engine provides `game_audit_state()`, a self-healing diagnostic
  routine that asserts all invariants simultaneously and outputs a detailed diagnostic manifest
  without crashing the game process.

=== Pillar 2: Deterministic Replay ("Golden Master Replay")

Games are emergent systems. If two players—or an automated CI runner—replay the exact same sequence of
commands from the same seed, the end state must be **100% bitwise identical**:

[source,c]
----
// Golden Master Replay Assertion
TEST_ASSERT(run1.shield_power == run2.shield_power, "Deterministic shield");
TEST_ASSERT(run1.sword_edge   == run2.sword_edge,   "Deterministic sword");
TEST_ASSERT(run1.moral_sway   == run2.moral_sway,   "Deterministic sway");
TEST_ASSERT(run1.threat_hp    == run2.threat_hp,    "Deterministic threat");
----

This ensures that gameplay balance, combat resolution, and narrative branching do not drift across
compiler optimizations, operating systems, or refactored engine code.

=== Pillar 3: Softlock Immunity & Degenerate Play Detection

A **softlock** occurs when a player is technically still playing, but has no valid action forward:
- Running out of relic energy during an un-fleeable battle.
- Getting trapped behind a one-way gate with no exit command.
- Inputting malformed or rapid chaotic strings that cause the parser to lock up.

Our automated stress suite executes **1,000 rapid chaotic commands** (combining invalid strings,
rapid stance transitions, and empty tokens), verifying that the game engine rejects nonsense safely,
maintains continuous memory integrity, and never enters a deadlocked state.

=== Pillar 4: Economy & Combat Balance Invariance

Game testing must verify that no single player action is universally dominant (the "dominant strategy" flaw).
If a player can simply spam `ward` forever without consequence, or if `strike` renders all other actions
useless, the game design collapses:
- Warding increases shield power and moral virtue, but deals limited stagger damage.
- Striking inflicts decisive severance damage, but consumes finite sword edge.
- Balancing recovers both relics and parries attacks, but requires timing and resets extreme moral sway.

=== Pillar 5: Frame-Budget Latency Headroom

In standard software, a backend database query taking 50 ms is acceptable. In game development,
a game running at 60 FPS has **only 16.6 milliseconds** to:
1. Poll hardware events and window queues.
2. Execute game simulation logic.
3. Compute spatial audio and particle animations.
4. Render the scene graph to the backbuffer and blit to the display.

Simulation logic must therefore execute in **microseconds**.
In our automated benchmark (`test_gameplay_scenarios`), 10,000 action turns execute in **160 ms**
(**16.02 $\mu\text{s}$ per turn**), consuming less than **$0.1\%$** of a 60 FPS frame.

== 1. Design Intent & Gameplay Philosophy

The **Attack** action channels the entropy and severance aspects of the **Sword of Tenebris**. Rather than generic single-target chipping, offensive melee strikes in *Tenebris and Lux* embody high-risk severance and crowd-cleaving outrider tactics:

* **Basic Attack (`attack` / `strike`)**:
  - Direct physical slash with the obsidian edge.
  - **Mechanics**: Computes primary physical damage based on player attack attribute and target physical defense:
    `Damage = (ATK * 2) - Enemy_DEF`.
  - **Edge Consumption**: Consumes $6 \dots 10$ points of blade sharpness (`sword_edge`).
  - **Out-of-Combat Attunement**: Outside of combat, channeling the blade hones its edge by $+15$ points as abyssal mist condenses along the blade.

* **The Basic Skill: `Cuttatio Tenebris` (`cuttatio`)**:
  - An abyssal sword slash that releases a crescent wave of twilight mist across the battlefield.
  - **Primary Severance**: Delivers full direct physical damage to the primary target (`(ATK * 2) - Enemy_DEF + Stance_Bonus`). In `STANCE_STRIKING`, an additional $+15$ flat damage bonus is applied.
  - **20% Area Splash Damage**: In tactical combat with multiple enemy formations or surrounding reinforcements, **$20\%$ of the primary damage** cascades onto adjacent flanking targets (`threat_surrounding_hp`).
  - **Minimum Splash Threshold**: Guarantees at least $1$ damage to flanking targets if any primary damage is dealt, preventing armor-rounding deadzones.

== 2. Numerical Invariants & Formulas

[cols="1,2,2", options="header"]
|===
| Parameter | Standard Attack | Cuttatio Tenebris
| **Action Type** | Basic Melee Strike | Blade Cleave Skill
| **Formula** | `(ATK * 2) - DEF` | `(ATK * 2) - DEF + Bonus`
| **Stance Scaling** | Standard damage | $+15$ bonus in `STANCE_STRIKING`
| **Edge Cost** | $6 \dots 10$ Edge | $8 \dots 12$ Edge
| **Splash Cleave** | None ($0\%$) | **$20\%$ of primary damage**
| **Moral Sway Effect** | $+0$ (Neutral) / $+1$ on kill | $+2$ (Controlled Severance)

== 1. Design Intent & Gameplay Philosophy

In tactical RPG design, standard "Guard" or "Defend" commands often feel like wasted turns or passive stalls. In *Tenebris and Lux*, the **Defend** action is an active tactical commitment that interfaces with the metaphysical resonance of the **Shield of Lux**:

* **Basic Defend (`defend` / `ward`)**:
  - The wielder braces behind the celestial aegis.
  - **Mechanics**: Absorbs incoming kinetic force, boosts `shield_power` by $+10 \dots +15$ (saturating at $100$), and staggers attacking threats for $10 \dots 15$ HP of poise recoil.
  - **Moral Resonance**: Positively shifts moral sway ($+1$ virtue).

* **The Basic Skill: `Lux Barrio` (`barrio` / `lux barrio`)**:
  - Rather than a flat shield block, `Lux Barrio` erects a radiant, geometric light barrier that deflects the incoming assault while mitigating subsequent trauma over multiple combat turns.
  - **Damage Mitigation**: Reduces incoming damage by **$25\%$** across all damage types.
  - **Turn Duration Roll**: Injects tactile variance into battle planning. The duration is rolled within $[2, 5]$ turns (`2 + (turns_taken % 4)`).
  - **Resonance Surge**: Surges Shield of Lux charge by $+20$ points and awards $+3$ virtue.
  - **Offensive Counter-Stagger**: When used against an attacking foe, deals $15 \dots 25$ HP of reflective shockwave damage, capable of routing low-vigor skirmishers directly.

== Chapter 10: Combat Game Note: Special Abilities & Duo-Loca Bashin n Slash

== 1. Design Intent & The Four-Fold Special Suite

The **Special** action suite expands the former "Balance" command into a versatile toolkit that balances resource expenditure (Mana Points / Tactical Points) against tactical problem-solving:

1. **`Basic Magic` (`magic`)**:
   - Represents neutral elemental magic damage unbound by strict Light or Dark alignment.
   - **Formula**: `(eff_MAT * 2) - Enemy_MDF`.
   - **Mana Cost**: $10$ MP.
   - **Synergy**: Scales dynamically with `Basic Charm`. If Charm is active, effective `MAT` increases by $+20\%$.

2. **`Basic Ward` (`ward` under special)**:
   - Arcane-based defensive warding.
   - Rather than purely relying on heavy armor and physical poise, the wielder constructs a shimmering lattice of runes.
   - **Mana Cost**: $6$ MP.
   - **Effect**: Staggers incoming assault ($12$ HP) and reinforces the Shield of Lux ($+15$ resonance).

3. **`Basic Charm` (`charm`)**:
   - An astral focus enchantment that attunes the mind to mystical resonance.
   - **Buff Effect**: Increases Magic Attack (`MAT`) by **$+20\%$**.
   - **Duration Roll**: Deterministically rolled for **$1 \dots 3$ turns** (`1 + (turns_taken % 3)`).
   - **Mana Cost**: $8$ MP.

4. **`Duo-Loca Bashin n Slash` (`duo`)**:
   - The signature synthesis technique of the twin relics.
   - **Adaptive Damage Intelligence**: Solves a common tactical RPG frustration where a physical fighter encounters heavily armored units or a spellcaster encounters magic-immune foes:
     * Evaluates `Physical_Potential = (ATK * 2) - Enemy_DEF`.
     * Evaluates `Magical_Potential = (eff_MAT * 2) - Enemy_MDF`.
     * If the enemy is more vulnerable to magic (`MDF < DEF`), or if the player's magical output is superior (`eff_MAT > ATK`): channels **Magical Twilight Slash**.
     * Otherwise: channels **Physical Radiant Bash**.
   - **Relic Harmonization**: Simultaneously replenishes $+15$ Shield of Lux and $+15$ Sword of Tenebris, while nudging moral sway by $3$ points toward cosmic equilibrium ($0$).
   - **Cost**: $12$ MP.

== Chapter 11: Combat Game Note: Tactical Stance State Machine

*Source File*: `/home/professorseanex/Tenebris and Lux/docs/game_design/combat/stance_state_machine.adoc`

== 1. State Machine Completeness

The tactical stance machine governs the battle posture of the bearer across four discrete, strongly-connected states:

[source,text]
----
                   ┌───────────────────┐
                   │  STANCE_NEUTRAL   │
                   └─────────┬─────────┘
                             │
         ┌───────────────────┼───────────────────┐
         ▼                   ▼                   ▼
┌─────────────────┐ ┌─────────────────┐ ┌─────────────────┐
│ STANCE_WARDING  │ │ STANCE_STRIKING │ │STANCE_EQUILIBRIUM
│ (Shield Guard)  │ │ (Sword Drawn)   │ │ (Twin Harmony)  │
└─────────────────┘ └─────────────────┘ └─────────────────┘
----

1. **`STANCE_NEUTRAL` (At Ease)**:
   - Balanced recovery posture. Baseline movement, balanced defenses.
2. **`STANCE_WARDING` (Shield High Guard)**:
   - Reinforces front-line presence. Lux Barrio stagger damage increases from $15$ to $25$ HP.
   - Standard defend boosts shield by $+15$ (vs $+10$).
3. **`STANCE_STRIKING` (Sword Drawn)**:
   - Aggressive forward stance. Primary melee attacks gain $+15$ flat damage bonus.
   - Attack edge consumption drops from $10$ to $6$ points.
4. **`STANCE_EQUILIBRIUM` (Twin Harmonized)**:
   - Balances both relics simultaneously. Enhances Duo-Loca execution and parry/riposte dynamics.

== Chapter 16: Tactical Battle System (LeTBS) Specification (2017–2018 Demo)

*Source File*: `/home/professorseanex/Tenebris and Lux/docs/game_design/demo_baseline/combat_and_letbs_mechanics.adoc`

[abstract]
== Overview

This document formalizes the grid-based Tactical Strategy RPG combat architecture
implemented via Lecode's Tactical Battle System (LeTBS) in the 2017–2018 demo.
It details movement costs, action economy, scope geometries, terrain manipulation,
and turn manipulation invariants.

== 1. Battle Field Architecture & Action Economy

* **Grid Structure**: 2D tile-based tactical grid mapped over encounter maps.
* **Movement Points (MP)**: Consumable per-turn budget determining movement range:
  - Baseline Protagonists & Allies: `move_points: 4`.
  - Heavy Impact Reductions: Heavy Hit reduces target MP by 3 (`move_points+: -3`).
  - Outrider Haste: Light Steps increases target MP by 1 (`move_points+: 1`).
* **Turn Timeline**: Monotonic timeline ordering turns by entity agility (`auto_turn_order_face`).
* **Directional Facing**: Facing impacts defense and positional abilities (e.g., *Phantom Slash* teleports directly behind the target before striking).

== 2. Scope & Area of Effect (AOE) Geometries

The demo configured rich geometric targeting parameters across skills:

[cols="2,2,3", options="header"]
|===
| Scope Pattern | Example Skill | Geometric Definition
| `circle(r)` | *Fire* (`Skill 42`), *Heal* (`Skill 72`) | Manhattan or Chebyshev circle of radius `r` centered on the caster.
| `circle(max_r, min_r)` | *Ghost Arrow* (`Skill 79`), *Dagger Throw* (`Skill 169`) | Annular targeting ring; cannot target adjacent cells within `min_r`.
| `line(n)` | *Explosive Mark* (`Skill 76`), *Great Wind* (`Skill 133`) | Cardinal linear projection up to `n` cells from caster.
| `square(r)` | *Angela Move Scope* (`Actor 5`) | Full square grid extent of radius `r`.
| `custom(pattern)` | *Fire Arrows* (`Skill 82`) | Scripted multi-target projectile pattern hitting all visible adversaries.
| `through_obstacles` | *Teleportation* (`Skill 73`) | Ignores intermediate terrain obstacles, requiring only a free destination cell.
|===

== 3. Terrain State Modifications & Dynamic Hazards

Combat encounters feature active environmental manipulation:

* **Volcano Ground (`Skill 47`)**: Lays hazardous fiery embers across multiple ground cells dealing continuous fire damage.
* **Ice Wall / Ice Prison (`Skill 51`, `Skill 52`)**: Summons physical destructible barricades (`HP: 20`) that obstruct enemy line-of-sight and movement corridors.
* **Explosive Mark & Magic Trap (`Skill 76`, `Skill 80`)**: Proximity-triggered grid landmines that detonate upon enemy entry.
* **Burning Tile Spread (`State 110`)**: Dynamic fire propagation expanding with `spread: 100% circle(1) all`.

== 4. Turn Timeline Manipulation

Unique priest techniques alter the flow of tactical battle:

* **Accelerate (`Skill 87`)**: Advances target position along the turn timeline by 100%.
* **Decelerate (`Skill 88`)**: Delays target position along the turn timeline by 100%.
* **Emergency Turn (`Skill 89`)**: Instantly grants an immediate action turn to an allied battler.
* **Extra Turn (`Skill 86`)**: Grants the caster or target an additional action cycle upon turn conclusion.

== 6. Modern C11 Architectural Mapping

In the C11 simulation engine, tactical states are decoupled from engine internals:
1. **Zero-Heap State Mask**: Active states on an actor are tracked via a bitmask and static array (`active_states[MAX_ACTIVE_STATES]`).
2. **Move Point Clamping**: `move_points` modifiers directly feed into Layer 0 spatial pathing routines.
3. **Data-Driven Configuration**: State durations, stat multipliers, and tile spread rules are loaded from `data/states/*.json`.

== Overview

== 1. The Monotonic Time Engine

The time system is driven by Common Events 1 and 2, updating a 24-hour cycle:
* `Variable 1`: Seconds (0–59)
* `Variable 2`: Minutes (0–59)
* `Variable 3`: Hours (1–12)
* `Variable 4`: 24-Hour Military Format (0–23)
* `Variable 5`: Days (1–7: Sunday through Saturday)
* `Variable 6`: Weeks
* `Variable 7`: Months
* `Variable 8`: Years

In the modern C11 architecture, this corresponds to Layer 0 `time_utils` (`time_get_monotonic_ms`).

== 2. The Moral Sway Continuum Engine

The morality engine tracks the party's ethical orientation on an invariant scale:
* `Variable 9`: **Moral (Overall)**: Continuum within `[-100, +100]`.
* `Variable 10`: **Moral (Good)**: Cumulative points awarded for altruism, charity, and mercy.
* `Variable 11`: **Moral (Evil)**: Cumulative points accrued for ruthless, self-serving, or thieving acts.
* `Item 8`: **Compass of Morality**: Tool allowing the player to inspect their moral continuum in the field.
