## Chapter 1: The Primordial Neutrality of Lux and Tenebris

> **Source**: `docs/lore/world_and_lore.adoc`

### Canonical Introduction

This document establishes the living world-building canon, cosmological ontology, and historical
epic of *Tenebris and Lux* ("Darkness and Light"). Conceived between 2014–2018 in RPG Maker as the
premier game of **CreativeWorkzStudio**, this is the epic of two primordial relics—**The Shield of Lux**
and **The Sword of Tenebris**—set within the storied **Kingdom of Sacyra** (pronounced *"saw-kee-ruh"*,
`/sɔːˈkiːrə/`). The story is driven by a tri-perspective narrative architecture: **Elijah Ro-enheart**,
son of a former head of the Royal Guard; **Lady Katrina Jaren**, an aristocratic daughter seeking autonomy
in the Adventurers' Guild; and the customizable **Bearer** (`<chosen_name>`), as the re-awakening
**Dark Lord** rises from the subterranean abyss and **King Jihad** plots treason on the High Throne.

### The Primordial Cosmology: Neutrality of the Apex Forces

"Light without shadow is a blinding desert that calcifies the spirit.
Shadow without light is a suffocating void where purpose drowns.
The Shield preserves and confines; the Sword severs and liberates.
The relics know neither virtue nor malice—only the soul that wields them."

In the cosmology of *Tenebris and Lux*, the cosmos is governed by two apex forces that transcend mortal morality.
Neither is inherently good nor evil:

#### Lux (The Apex of Radiance & Cohesion)
* **Ontological Essence:** Manifestation, illumination, cohesion, preservation, temporal anchoring, and structure.
* **The Dual Nature of Light:**
  - **Virtuous Manifestation:** Projects golden protective barriers (*Lux Barrio*), wards the vulnerable,
    preserves cultural heritage, and seeks peace through reconciliation.
  - **Tyrannical Manifestation:** Scorches the earth in unyielding glare, blinding dissenters, enforcing
    calcifying stasis, denying natural growth and mortality, and petrifying living will into stone.

#### Tenebris (The Apex of Darkness & Severance)
* **Ontological Essence:** Mystery, dissolution, rest, severance, finality, and the fertile void.
* **The Dual Nature of Darkness:**
  - **Virtuous Manifestation:** Cuts through tyrant armor (*Cuttatio Tenebris*), severs corrupt bindings,
    veils the oppressed from predators, and executes swift, surgical **Absolute Justice**.
  - **Malevolent Manifestation:** Spreads entropic rot, dissolves conscience into nihilism, feeds bitter
    vengeance, and fuels tyrannical conquest.

#### The Law of Tempering and Dissipation
The relationship between Lux and Tenebris is governed by a strict metaphysical law:
* **Lux as the Singular Tempering Agent:** Raw Tenebris is entropic and severing; left unchecked, it erodes
  everything it touches. Lux is the only force capable of containing and tempering Tenebris, providing
  structural cohesion so that the void's cutting edge can be safely directed.
* **The Fragility of Radiance (Dissipation):** While Lux tempers Tenebris, it requires steadfast emotional
  and ethical harmony. Any sudden, violent surge of fear, wrath, or malice causes Lux to **dissipate immediately**,
  collapsing protective barriers and leaving Tenebris unbridled.

#### The Moral Sway Continuum (`[-100, +100]`) & 13-Tier Taxonomy

Neither Lux nor Tenebris dictates a character's morality. Good and evil belong entirely to the **intent,
conscience, and choices of the heart behind the weapon**, tracked along the `[-100, +100]` **Moral Sway**
axis via the **Compass of Morality**. A knight channeling blinding radiance can act as a calcified tyrant,
while an outrider wielding the void-edged blade of Tenebris can stand as the people's ultimate savior.

### Genesis: The Mundane Inception & The Two Princes

#### The Mundane Armaments of Inception
At the founding of the Kingdom of Sacyra, the Shield of Lux and Sword of Tenebris were forged as ordinary,
non-magical weapons of steel and silver by royal smiths, presented to the First King as civic emblems:
* **The Mundane Shield:** Represented **Peace**, communal defense, and internal harmony.
* **The Mundane Sword:** Represented **War**, territorial justice, and border vigilance.

#### The Self-Running Systems
The First King was deeply beloved by his subjects across all provinces. Under his leadership, the realm's
civic, legal, and municipal systems were engineered so effectively that the kingdom practically governed
itself. Personal royal audiences were required only "once in a blue moon".

#### The Ideological Fork
The First King had two teenage sons who divided the responsibilities of civilization:
* **Prince Mi-ren:** The elder son and heir to the High Throne, raised in statecraft to preserve his father's peace.
* **Prince Casatti:** The younger son, who voluntarily renounced the throne to establish **The Academy**,
  an independent institution dedicated to natural philosophy, elemental sciences, and mapping **The Dreamscape**.

### The 11th Festival Rupture & The 7-Year Great War

#### The Coma and the Silent Vigil (Festivals 1–10)
Following the first week-long **Festival of Peoples**, the First King fell into a coma, though his consciousness
remained awake across the gossamer veil in **The Dreamscape**. When his physical body passed away during the
5th Festival, teenage Mi-ren and Casatti maintained a protective vigil: using the annual thinned-veil festival
week to commune with the First King's spirit and project him into the temporal world in short bursts until
Mi-ren was officially "of age."

#### Usuru and the 11th Year Catastrophe
In the 11th year, an eleven-year-old wanderer from remote Libira (a frontier kingdom suffering from a seven-day
courier delay and no relay outposts) snuck into the royal sanctum to see the "plagued" king. Discovering the
mummified corpse and projection array, the shocked boy accidentally tampered with the ritual:
1. The spiritual tether snapped, permanently sealing the First King in The Dreamscape.
2. The violent shift in the boy's heart caused an unprecedented metaphysical surge that birthed **Tenebris**
   into waking reality.
3. The planar backlash physically warped continental distances between Libira and the Capital.
4. Taking the name **Usuru**, the youth led Libira and neighboring border realms in a war of annexation
   against the Capital.

#### The 7-Year Great War & The Awakening of the Relics
Usuru's uprising ignited **The Great War** across six fractured powers: The Capital, Libira, Great Mau,
Old Sacyra, The Academy, and the Yun-yin Provinces:
* **Casatti's Infusion of Tenebris:** Prince Casatti isolated the anomalous void energy and infused it into
  the mundane Sword of War to execute **Absolute Justice** and end the war swiftly.
* **Mi-ren's Discovery of Lux:** Prince Mi-ren adapted the temporal projection technique to discover **Lux**,
  infusing it into the mundane Shield of Peace with a mandate of **atonement and forgiveness for Usuru**.
* **The Accord of Kings:** Lux tempered Tenebris, bringing the war to an armistice in exactly **seven years**.
  The six powers ratified the **"Joint Realm, Separate Kingdoms"** accord, solidifying autonomous regional rule
  under the High Crown and enshrining the **Prophecy of Kings**. Refusing reconciliation, Usuru retreated
  into the abyssal vaults, degenerating over three millennia into **The Dark Lord**.

### The 3,000-Year Dynastic Wave & Modern Sacyra

Between the Great War and the modern campaign lies a three-thousand-year chasm ($3,000\text{ years}$)
spanning between **30 and 50 monarchs** (averaging $60\text{ to }100\text{ years}$ per reign):

* **The Succession Wave:** The dynasty cycled between Long Stasis Crests (60–90+ years of high peace and
  calcified bureaucracy), Tether-Sickness Troughs (5–20 years of royal paranoia and premature death near the
  High Throne's planar wound), and Bureaucratic Consolidations (35–50 years).
* **The Systemic Neglect:** Millennia of Capital stability fostered systemic apathy toward frontier settlements
  like **Dio Village**, replicating ancient Libira's "quiet complaint."
* **"The King's Curse" & King Jihad:** For three millennia, sovereigns on the High Throne were exposed to
  psychic backwash from the planar wound left by Usuru. Coveting void power, **King Jihad** opened himself
  to the abyss prior to the modern Festival of Heroes, resulting in possession by The Dark Lord's psychic remnant.

### The Tri-Perspective Protagonists

The heart of *Tenebris and Lux* is an unfolding mystery navigated across three distinct vantage points:

#### Elijah Ro-enheart (The Vanguard of Lux)
* **Lineage & Heritage:** Son of legendary **Commander Gareth Ro-enheart**, former Head of the Royal Guard,
  who vanished under King Jihad's sealed orders on Elijah's 10th birthday.
* **Archetype & Role:** Frontline Heavy Vanguard / Fortress Anchor. Wields the **Shield of Lux**.

#### Lady Katrina Jaren (The Outrider of Tenebris)
* **Lineage & Heritage:** Estranged daughter of aristocratic **House Jaren**, one of the wealthiest noble

* **Archetype & Role:** High-Mobility Skirmisher / Flanking Striker. Wields the **Sword of Tenebris**.
* **Thematic Parallel:** Mirrors ancient **Prince Casatti** (The Renouncing Blade). Renounces aristocratic

* **Archetype & Role:** Emergent champion in Chrono's astral archives. Holds **The Dream Compass**.

### Era I: The Founding of Sacyra & The Two Princes

#### The Mundane Armaments of Inception

At the founding of the Kingdom of Sacyra, the **Shield of Lux** and the **Sword of Tenebris** were
not magical or celestial artifacts. They were forged as ordinary, non-magical armaments of steel and
silver by royal smiths, presented to the First King as civic symbols of statecraft:

* **The Mundane Shield:** Represented **Peace**, communal defense, and agricultural stability.
* **The Mundane Sword:** Represented **War**, territorial vigilance, and border law enforcement.

#### The Self-Running Systems of the First King

The First King was deeply beloved by his subjects across all provinces. Under his leadership, the
kingdom's municipal, legal, and civic institutions were so well-engineered that the realm practically
governed itself. Citizens did not lack access to the King out of tyranny or aloofness, but because
the systems functioned with such frictionless harmony that a personal royal audience was required
only "once in a blue moon".

#### The Ideological Fork: Mi-ren and Casatti

The First King had two sons who chose distinct paths while still young teenagers:

* **Prince Mi-ren (The Elder Son):** The rightful heir to the High Throne, raised in administrative
  discipline to inherit his father's covenant of golden peace.
* **Prince Casatti (The Younger Son):** Renounced his royal birthright to the throne to establish
  **The Academy**, dedicating his life to empirical inquiry, elemental sciences, and mapping the
  astral geometries of the ethereal plane.

### Era II & III: The Festival of Peoples, The Coma & The Silent Vigil

#### Why the Festival Lasted a Full Week

Every year, the Capital hosted the **Festival of Peoples**—an international gathering where border
demarcations were ratified, continental infrastructure treaties negotiated, and subjects celebrated.
Rather than holding a single-day event, the First King mandated a full seven-day festival out of
deliberate consideration for distant subjects: those with harvest or trade obligations could depart
early, while delegations traveling from the furthest frontiers of the known world were guaranteed
meaningful participation.

#### The First Festival Coma & Discovery of The Dreamscape

Immediately following the first grand Festival of Peoples—where the collective emotional consciousness
of an entire continent converged—the First King collapsed into a deep coma. His body remained dormant,
yet his spirit was fully lucid and awake on the other side of the ethereal boundary in **The Dreamscape**.
By tracing his father's displaced consciousness, Prince Casatti and The Academy mapped the Dreamscape
in full.

#### Festivals 1 Through 4: The Four-Year Rescue Effort

During the first four years of the coma, the annual festival week—when the boundary ("the Veil")
thinned to gossamer—served as the **surest, most stable window** to access the King. Throughout these
four years, Mi-ren, Casatti, and the court worked tirelessly across the thinned veil attempting to
pull the First King back into his mortal body.

#### Era III: The 5th Festival Death & The Meaning of "Of Age"

During the **5th Festival of Peoples**, the First King's physical body died. Pulling him out of the
coma was no longer possible. However, the Castle court continued the festival because that thinned-veil
week was now the **sole remaining lifeline** allowing Mi-ren to commune with his father's lucid spirit
and project his consciousness into the temporal hall in short bursts when rare royal audiences were needed.

This secret was maintained across Festivals 5 through 10 not out of malice, but as a protective vigil:

* **No Precedent for Succession:** Sacyra had never experienced the death of a monarch before; international
  peace treaties were bound to the First King's personal covenant, and no constitutional law existed for a regency.
* **The Chivalric Definition of "Of Age":** Mi-ren and Casatti were teenagers. In Sacyran chivalric law,
  attaining majority required martial maturity to personally command the Royal Guard alongside mastery
  of the Academy's statecraft systems so the self-running realm would not shatter under foreign pressure.

### Era IV: Usuru the Wanderer & The 11th Year Tragedy

#### The "Quiet Complaint" of Remote Libira

Far on the southern frontier lay the **Kingdom of Libira**, a sparse, underdeveloped territory separated
from the Capital by a grueling seven-day journey. Because Libira was not yet established enough to maintain
an imperial relay outpost network, communications suffered a chronic seven-day lag. This systemic gap
fostered a simmering **"quiet complaint"** among Libirans regarding the disparity between Capital prosperity
and border isolation.

#### The Three-Day Wanderer

During the First King's peaceful reign, a boy was born in Libira to a humble family without being given
a name. For ten years, he grew up traveling the continent as a restless wanderer who lived by a strict
personal rule: **never stay more than three days anywhere**—except for the annual week-long Festival of
Peoples in the Capital, which he attended with pure devotion to honor the King he revered.

#### The 11th Year Rupture

During the boy's eleventh annual pilgrimage to the festival, heralds announced that a "royal plague"
kept the First King unseen on his balcony. Anxious for the monarch he adored, the eleven-year-old boy
slipped past castle guards into the royal sanctum to seek an audience.

Inside, the boy discovered the long-dead body and Prince Mi-ren utilizing Academy projection glyphs.
Lacking the statecraft context to understand the court's desperate vigil, the boy viewed the revelation
as a monstrous, hypocritical deception. In his terror and shock, he accidentally collided with the
ritual array, triggering four catastrophic consequences:

1. **The Eternal Astral Seal:** The First King's spiritual tether snapped; his consciousness was permanently
   marooned in **The Dreamscape**.
2. **The Birth of Tenebris:** The violent collapse of the boy's innocent faith into profound betrayal produced
   an anomalous metaphysical shockwave, releasing the cosmic apex force of **Tenebris** (severance and dissolution)
   into the waking temporal world that very day.
3. **The Warped Distance:** The planar backwash physically warped continental leylines, stretching the geographical
   distance between Libira and the Capital into an unnatural chasm that the boy interpreted as an act of deliberate
   hostile separation by the Crown.
4. **The Rise of Sovereign Usuru:** Taking the name **Usuru**, the youth fled back to Libira, revealed the Capital's
   deception, was crowned young ruler of Libira, and rallied neighboring border kingdoms into an alliance to declare
   a war of total annexation against the Capital.

### Era V: The 7-Year Great War & Awakening of the Relics

#### The Six Fractured Powers

Usuru's uprising ignited **The Great War**, plunging six sovereign powers into continental war:

| Faction | Leadership | Strategic Role in the Conflict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The Libira Kingdom** | Sovereign Usuru | Vanguard of the frontier coalition; driven by righteous betrayal and territorial annexation. |
| **The Capital** | Prince Mi-ren | Citadel defense of the High Throne, administrative continuity, and royal army deployment. |
| **The Great Mau Kingdom** | High Chieftains | Mighty inland territorial power maneuvering along contested eastern borders. |
| **Old Sacyra** | Traditional Fiefdoms | Ancestral heartland baronies fighting to preserve chivalric feudal order. |
| **The Academy** | Prince Casatti | Independent intellectual and arcane bastion deploying experimental elemental resonance. |
| **The Yun-yin Provinces** | Provincial Assemblies | Western agricultural riverlands caught between coalition supply routes and capital levies. |


#### Casatti's Infusion of Tenebris (The Sword of War)

Determined to end Usuru's siege quickly and preserve the realm, Prince Casatti analyzed the anomalous
energy spike recorded during the ritual rupture. Synthesizing his knowledge of the Dreamscape and elemental
properties, Casatti isolated **Tenebris** and infused it into the mundane Sword of War. He forged the
**Sword of Tenebris** not out of bloodlust, but to execute **Absolute Justice**—creating a surgical blade
capable of severing siege lines and unyielding armor to halt the war decisively.

#### Mi-ren's Discovery of Lux (The Shield of Peace)

Taking command of the Capital's forces, Prince Mi-ren sought not conquest or vengeance, but **atonement
and forgiveness from Usuru**. By reverse-engineering the projection ritual used to anchor ethereal concepts
into physical reality, Mi-ren unlocked **Lux** (cohesion, preservation, and radiance) and infused it into
the mundane Shield of Peace, creating the **Shield of Lux**.

#### The Law of Tempering and Dissipation

Battlefield confrontations between the two armaments revealed the foundational cosmic dynamic:

* **Lux as the Singular Tempering Agent:** Raw Tenebris is purely severing and entropic; left unchecked,
  it erodes both wielder and target. Lux is the only force capable of containing and stabilizing Tenebris,
  granting structural cohesion so void strikes can be directed with precision.
* **The Fragility of Radiance (Dissipation):** While Lux stabilizes Tenebris, its golden coherence requires
  steadfast emotional and ethical harmony. Any sudden surge of terror, wrath, or malice causes Lux to
  **dissipate instantly**, leaving Tenebris unbridled.

#### The Parley & The 7-Year Armistice

During peace negotiations, Prince Mi-ren lowered his defenses and offered total reconciliation to Usuru.
However, Usuru's deep bitterness erupted in a violent surge of hatred, causing Mi-ren's ambient Lux
aura to dissolve on the spot.

Recognizing that neither side could achieve unconditional victory without mutual annihilation, the six
powers ratified the **Accord of Kings**, bringing the Great War to a close after exactly **seven years**:

* **"Joint Realm, Separate Kingdoms":** Autonomous provinces and kingdoms formed a loose federated
  realm under the High Crown while preserving regional self-rule.
* **The Prophecy of Kings:** Sages enshrined the legend that true peace would only return when a bearer
  achieved the **Stance of Equilibrium**, harmonizing the Shield and Sword without allowing wrath to
  dissipate the light.
* **Usuru's Descent:** Refusing reconciliation, Usuru retreated into the deep subterranean abyss,
  where his resentment festered over three millennia until his name was forgotten, remembered only as
  **The Dark Lord**.

No single crown shall claim the sun, and no lone throne shall command the shadows.

* **Domain & Geography**: The subtropical southeastern archipelago and bamboo cloud forests.

## Chapter 7: Realm Chronicle: The Kingdom of Libira

> **Source**: `docs/lore/realms/kingdom_of_libira.adoc`

### Overview

The **Kingdom of Libira** is a remote, windswept, and arid frontier realm situated far to the
west across the Great Salt Basin, a full seven-day courier journey from the **Royal Capital of Sacyra**.
Lacking a fortified relay outpost system throughout the founding era, Libira became the birthplace
of the simmering socio-political grievance known historically as **"The Quiet Complaint"**.
It was here that a nameless boy was born to a humble shepherd family, traveled the continent as the
Three-Day Wanderer, and returned across the warped desert passes as **Usuru**, sovereign of the
broken crown and the future **Dark Lord**.

____
The sun burns the rocks by day, and the wind freezes the sands by night. Sacyra has water,
gold, and songs for its kings. Libira has only stone, salt, and the memory of every empty
wagon that never returned from the capital.
____

### Geography, Climate & The 7-Day Courier Chasm

* **Geographical Classification**: High-altitude arid plateau, red sandstone mesas, salt flats, and canyon wadis.
* **The Communication Gap**:
  - Distance: A full week (seven days) of hard riding by swift couriers across barren terrain.
  - Absence of Infrastructure: Because Libira was historically treated as an unestablished border territory,
    the Sacyran crown never invested in a chain of relay watchtowers or horse stations.
  - The Warped Rift: When the 11th Festival ritual collapsed, the spatial fabric between Libira and the
    capital expanded violently, turning a week-long journey into an impassable spatial wasteland.

| **Key Settlements** | **The Assembly of Libira** (Central adobe council citadel); **Wadi Usuru**. |
| **Strategic Resources** | Saltpeter, red iron ore, draught lizards, subterranean aquifer water. |


### Socio-Political Philosophy: The "Quiet Complaint"

Unlike open rebellions driven by greedy warlords, Libira's grievance was born of profound, generational neglect:

* **The Disconnect**: The capital celebrated annual harvests and imperial festivals, issuing royal
  edicts that arrived in Libira weeks after decrees were signed. Libiran citizens suffered under
  droughts and border raids without receiving imperial military reinforcement.
* **The "Quiet Complaint"**: A cultural attitude of stoic, bitter endurance. Libirans learned never to
  expect salvation from the Crown, fostering fierce communal solidarity and deep suspicion of
  ceremonial authority.
* **Modern Parallels in Dio Village**: The same dynamic repeats in the present era in **Dio Village**,
  where Grandma Magolia sighs over the Crown's neglect and the St. Baron taxes commoners without
  providing protection from monster raids.

> **[IMPORTANT]**

> Had the First King's court listened to the nameless boy's petition and built a simple relay road,
> the Seven-Year Great War would never have occurred.


### The Tragedy of Usuru's Assembly

When Usuru returned from the capital with his skin burned by radiant energy and his eyes alight
with abyssal fury, the Libiran Assembly Hall became the focal point of continental history:

1. **The Gathering of Elders**: Tribal elders, weary of decades of unanswered petitions, wept as the
   youth recounted how the Sacyran guards struck him down simply for asking for roads.
2. **The Acclamation of the Sovereign**: For the first time in their history, the disparate nomadic
   clans united, placing the iron circlet upon Usuru's brow and naming him their young King.
3. **The Call to the 6 Powers**: Leveraging trade routes across the salt flats, Usuru sent emissaries
   to Great Mau, Old Sacyra, and the Yun-yin Provinces, turning a localized frontier petition into
   a world-spanning coalition war.

## Chapter 11: Realm Chronicle: Dio Village

> **Source**: `docs/lore/realms/dio_village.adoc`

### Overview & Pastoral Heritage

**Dio Village** is a pastoral woodland settlement situated in the southwestern valley frontier of the
**Kingdom of Sacyra**. Functioning as the physical inception of the hero's journey upon awakening
from **The Dreamscape**, Dio Village serves as the terrestrial anchor of Chapter 1, embodying the modern
continuation of ancient Libira's systemic neglect under the 3,000-year dynastic order.

### Socio-Political Role: "The Quiet Complaint" of Modern Sacyra

* **Mirror of Ancient Libira:** Just as ancient Libira suffered a 7-day communication delay and lacked
  imperial relay outposts, modern Dio Village is neglected under the "Joint Realm, Separate Kingdoms"
  bureaucracy. As Grandma Magolia sighs in Map 021: _"This town used to be full of life. *sighs*
  The kingdom doesn't pay much attention to this place anymore..."_.
* **Economic Fragility & Class Exploitation:** While the Capital enjoys insulated peace, Dio Village
  faces monster outbreaks near the timber lines. **Eric the Lumberjack** faces ruin because monsters block
  the woods, while the predatory **St. Baron** exploits commoners through exorbitant rents and usury.
* **Gareth Ro-enheart's Shadow:** Dio Village is where Commander Gareth Ro-enheart placed his son Elijah
  under Grandma Magolia's care before his mysterious disappearance on Elijah's 10th birthday.

### Key Landmarks & Dual-Perspective Quest Hubs

* **Grandma Magolia's Cottage (Maps 020 & 021):** The warm stone hearth where the protagonist awakens.
  Contains the upstairs chest housing **Grandma's Watch** (activating the Layer 0 monotonic clock).
  - *Elijah's Approach:* Hesitates out of filial respect and family etiquette.
  - *Katrina's Approach:* Seeks the will immediately to escape the dead-end town.
* **Eric's Timber Yard & Test of Honesty (Map 028):** Filling Eric's rent pot shifts Moral Sway positively
  and rewards the **Ring of Fire**, enabling flame-weave relic embellishments.
* **Monica's Mystic Grove & Lyri's Offering (Maps 023, 027, 029):** Former Royal Battlemage Monica guards
  the memorial totem of fallen soldiers. Stopping the bandit kid and buying an offering potion for **Lyri,
  the Goddess of Peace**, attunes the relics to arcane resonance and awards the **Book of Monsters**.
* **The St. Baron's Estate & John the Thief (Maps 030 & 031):** Deciding whether to aid **John the Thief**
  in robbing the corrupt baron versus upholding feudal property law shapes the penetration or bastion
  embellishments of the relics.

### Mechanical Role in the C11 Simulation Engine

* **Engine Enumeration:** Registered in `include/sim/sim_types.h` as `LOC_DIO_VILLAGE`.
* **Sanctuary Province:** Zero hostile vigor during standard state; permits inventory management,
  dialogue interactions, moral compass calibration, and Layer 0 monotonic time passage.
* **Terrestrial Lockout:** Gated throughout Chapter 1, preventing return to The Dreamscape until
  the acquisition and awakening of **The Dream Compass**.

## Chapter 12: Realm Chronicle: The Dreamscape

> **Source**: `docs/lore/realms/the_dreamscape.adoc`

### Overview & Metaphysical Essence

**The Dreamscape** (also known as the Astral Substrate or Room of Sages) is an ethereal plane
of consciousness, ancestral memory, and timeless trials existing between waking reality and slumber.
Grounded in the recovered 2017–2018 demo script (Maps 006, 008, 011, 041, 042) and verified in the
ISO C11 engine architecture as `LOC_THE_DREAMSCAPE`, it serves as the cosmological origin point
where the protagonist awakens before entering the temporal Kingdom of Sacyra.

### Historical & Metaphysical Provenance

* **The Coma of the First King:** Following the inaugural Festival of Peoples 3,000 years ago,
  the First King collapsed into a coma. His spirit was found fully lucid and awake inside The Dreamscape.
  While Prince Mi-ren and Prince Casatti sustained projections for ten festivals, the 11th Festival
  tampering by Usuru snapped the ethereal tether, permanently sealing the First King's soul here.
* **The Annual Thinning of the Veil:** For one week each year—coinciding with the Festival of Heroes—the
  boundary ("the Veil") separating the temporal world from The Dreamscape thins to gossamer, allowing
  mortals to project desires across the threshold.
* **The Three Laws of Crossing the Veil:**
  1. *The Mirror of Intent:* The ether reflects mortal intent. Pure devotion projects harmony; terror,
     spite, or megalomania (such as King Jihad's treason) inverts the conduit into waking nightmares.
  2. *The Severed Tether:* Sustaining a long bridge across the veil carries fatal risk. Violent
     interruption snaps spiritual tethers, marooning consciousness permanently.
  3. *The Dream Eater Infiltration:* Tearing or weakening the veil attracts **Dream Eaters**—parasitic
     entities native to the deep astral voids that devour mortal memories, dreams, and ancestral records.

### Mechanical Integration in the C11 Engine

* **Engine Enumeration:** Registered in `include/sim/sim_types.h` as `LOC_THE_DREAMSCAPE`.
* **Opening Trial:** The protagonist awakens in the Hall of Sages with **Chrono the Sage**, attuning
  to class archetype stones and testing `Lux Barrio` and `Cuttatio Tenebris` against Dream Eaters before
  Chrono's command (*"AWAKEN!"*) transports consciousness to Grandma Magolia's hearth in Dio Village.
* **Terrestrial Gating:** Throughout Chapter 1 (the demo), return to The Dreamscape is barred, anchoring
  the player in terrestrial human stakes.
* **The Dream Compass & Nocturnal Phasing:** Upgraded from Grandma's Watch, the Dream Compass allows
  entry to The Dreamscape exclusively during nocturnal hours (`20:00 <= hour || hour <= 04:00`).
* **Tactical LeTBS Environments:** Comprises floating platform grids, shifting crystal leylines,
  and astral mechanisms that restore MP while exposing units to planar shear.

## Chapter 13: High School Inception & TRPG Genesis

> **Source**: `docs/dev_logs/2026/10/lore_and_design/devlog_002_the_sacyra_chronicles_and_trpg_vision.adoc`

### Abstract

This developer log chronicles the foundational provenance and overarching creative vision
of *Tenebris and Lux*, rooted in its 2014–2018 high school inception as the premier game project
of CreativeWorkzStudio. It documents the elimination of placeholder factions in favor of the canonical
**Kingdom of Sacyra**, the introduction of dual protagonists **Elijah Ro-enheart** and **Katrina Jaren**,
and the technical specification of the game's true genre: a **Tactical Strategy RPG (TRPG)** fusing
grid-based tactical maneuvering with cinematic traditional side-scroll / action-based combat resolution.

### Historical Provenance: The 2014–2018 High School Genesis

Before CreativeWorkzStudio evolved into a multifaceted publishing house, *Tenebris and Lux* was its
very first major game in active development.

* **The RPG Maker Era (2014–2018)**: Conceived while the author was in high school learning game design
  and programming. The initial iterations were built using the default offerings of RPG Maker, exploring
  community plugins, and crafting characters through its Character Generator.
* **The Lost Assets & The Blank Canvas**: With no legacy digital assets surviving from that era, the
  project stands not as a nostalgic port, but as a complete ground-up rebirth—realizing the original
  creative vision with the systems engineering maturity, mathematical rigor, and architectural discipline
  of modern custom engine design.

### Narrative Realignment: The Kingdom of Sacyra

During early prototype scaffolding, generic placeholder factions ("The Solar Order" and "The Umbral Covenant")
were inserted to test basic moral sway logic. On October 26, 2026, the creator firmly realigned the canon,
stripping away these synthetic factions to reveal the true world:

#### The Kingdom of Sacyra (`/sɔːˈkiːrə/`)

* The setting is the historic **Kingdom of Sacyra** (pronounced *"saw-kee-ruh"*).
* Sacyra is a structured, chivalric realm anchored by three distinct socio-political pillars:
  1. **The Crown & Royal Guard**: Guardians of legal order, national defense, and the ancestral throne.
  2. **The Noble Houses & Royal Court**: High aristocracy governing regional fiefs and vying for political dominance on the royal council.
  3. **The Adventurers' Guild**: A pragmatic, frontier-based brotherhood operating beyond courtly vanity, answering commoner monster distress calls and exploring untamed ruins.
* **The Existential Threat**: The ancient sovereign **The Dark Lord** breaking through the Abyssal Vaults, threatening to extinguish all mortal agency.

#### The Dual Protagonists

Rather than a generic lone wanderer, the narrative is anchored by two contrasting protagonists whose
fates, lineages, and martial philosophies intertwine:

| Character | Elijah Ro-enheart | Lady Katrina Jaren |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Social Origin** | Military Lineage (Common/Barracks Nobility) | High Aristocracy (House Jaren) |
| **Family Heritage** | Son of the former Head of the Royal Guard | Daughter of an influential noble house on the Royal Court |
| **Internal Desire** | Living up to his father's legendary military legacy while discovering what true honor demands | Escaping stifling aristocratic court expectations to join the local **Adventurers' Guild** |
| **Relic Synergy** | **The Shield of Lux** (Sanctuary, Warding, Formation Hold) | **The Sword of Tenebris** (Severance, Mist-Veil, Critical Strike) |
| **TRPG Doctrine** | Frontline heavy vanguard; zone-of-control denial; ally interception | Agile outrider skirmisher; bypassing enemy lines; flanking assassin |


### Ludological Evolution: The Tactical Strategy RPG (TRPG)

The game's true mechanical identity is a **Tactical Strategy RPG (TRPG)**. This fundamentally informs
how the simulation engine and presentation viewports are being constructed:

#### The Dual-Layer Combat Loop

The combat loop operates across two synchronized phases:

1. **Strategic Grid Phase**:
   - The battle takes place on a discrete 2D grid representing castles, ramparts, thickets, and void-blighted frontier passes.
   - Units position based on movement ranges, terrain defense bonuses, directional facing (Front, Flank, Rear), and Zone-of-Control (ZOC) restrictions.
2. **Cinematic Combat Resolution (Side-View Action)**:
   - When a unit initiates combat against an enemy target, the game transitions into a dynamic **2D traditional side-scroll / action-based clash**.
   - Attacks play out with timed player agency: precision blocks with the Shield of Lux, poise-breaking shield bashes, and devastating severance slashes with the Sword of Tenebris.
   - Upon duel conclusion, remaining unit health, stagger, and relic resonance seamlessly persist back to the tactical grid.

#### Architectural Implications for the C Engine

Knowing this was originally an RPG Maker project gives us clear architectural directives:
* **Decoupled Data Architecture**: Static definitions (`Units`, `Skills`, `Equipment`, `TerrainTypes`) must exist as clean data tables distinct from session state (`player_state_t`, `grid_t`).
* **Deterministic Event Pipeline**: Interactive map features (gates, switches, dialog triggers) will follow a clean, reentrant command interpreter model.
* **Zero Engine Bloat**: By building this custom in C11 rather than relying on bloated runtimes, our TRPG will maintain sub-millisecond turn updates, instantaneous scene transitions, and absolute softlock immunity.
