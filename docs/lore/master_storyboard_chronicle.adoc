== Overview & Cinematic Philosophy

This document contains the complete master storyboard sequence for the historical prologue
and ancestral memory sequences of *Tenebris and Lux*. Comprising twenty sequentially numbered
cinematic scenes (Scenes 01–20), this chronicle establishes the visual language, color temperature,
character staging, and dramatic pacing that bridge the founding of the Kingdom of Sacyra to the
catastrophe of the 11th Festival and the dawn of the Great War.

== 1. Cinematic Staging Matrix (Scenes 01–20)

[cols="1,2,2,3,4", options="header"]
|===
| Scene | Title | Setting & Tone | Visual Focus | Narrative & Thematic Action

| **01**
| **Symbols of Iron**
| Royal Forges of Sacyra (Warm hearth amber)
| Smiths quenching an unadorned steel kite-shield and broadsword.
| The Kingdom of Sacyra is founded. Two ordinary weapons are presented to the First King as civic symbols of **Peace** and **War**.

| **02**
| **The Crown and the Scroll**
| Sacyra Throne Room (Sunlit marble)
| The First King with his teenage sons, Mi-ren and Casatti.
| Mi-ren accepts his birthright as heir; Casatti declines the throne to establish **The Academy**.

| **03**
| **The Self-Running Realm**
| Capital Courtyards & Provincial Towns (Golden calm)
| Civic courts and markets running smoothly without royal interference.
| Establishes a reign of peace so well-engineered that war is merely an idea and royal audiences are needed only once in a blue moon.

| **04**
| **The First Festival & The Coma**
| Grand Festival Grounds / Royal Bedchamber
| The known world celebrating for a full week; the First King collapsing into a coma as his spirit awakens in the Dreamscape.
| The week-long Festival of Peoples accommodates distant travelers. Afterward, the King falls comatose; Casatti discovers the Dreamscape in full while tracking his father's lucid spirit.

| **05**
| **The Four-Year Rescue Effort**
| Academy Conduit Chamber (Festivals 1–4)
| Teenage Mi-ren and Casatti working across the thinned festival veil to reach their father.
| For four years, the festival week serves as the surest window to access the King while his sons attempt to pull him out of his coma.

| **06**
| **The Passing at the Fifth**
| Royal Sanctum (Festival 5, solemn candlelight)
| The First King's physical body expiring while his golden silhouette remains across the veil.
| The King dies during the 5th Festival. Because Mi-ren is not yet "of age" for the first succession, the Castle keeps the secret; the festival continues as the only way left to access the King in short bursts.

| **07**
| **The Quiet Complaint of Libira**
| Frontier Kingdom of Libira (Windy, sparse roads)
| A lone courier riding for seven days without a single relay outpost; a nameless boy born to a humble family.
| Highlights the week-long travel gap and lack of an outpost system in Libira, breeding a "quiet complaint" over the Capital's distance.

| **08**
| **The Three-Day Wanderer**
| Continental Roads & Capital Gates
| Montage of the boy wandering from town to town (never staying past 3 days), except for his annual week at the Festival of Peoples.
| For 10 years, the humble wanderer visits the Capital every festival week to celebrate and honor the First King.

| **09**
| **The Plague Year**
| Festival Concourse — Year 11 (Somber crowd)
| Heralds announcing a "plague" has struck the King; the 11-year-old boy looking at the empty royal balcony.
| Unseen for the entire 11th festival, the King's absence worries the boy, prompting him to sneak into the castle for an audience.

| **10**
| **Behind the Veil**
| Inner Castle Sanctum (Arcane glyphs, mist)
| The boy discovering Mi-ren using Academy knowledge to commune with the long-dead First King.
| From the boy's perspective, the Capital's golden peace is exposed as a years-long deception.

| **11**
| **The Shattered Ritual & Birth of Tenebris**
| Inner Sanctum (Violent violet and gold shockwave)
| Shocked, the boy accidentally tampers with the ritual; the tether snaps and a dark energy spike erupts.
| The First King is sealed forever in **The Dreamscape**. The devastating shift in the boy's heart spawns **Tenebris** and warps the physical distance between Libira and the Capital.

| **12**
| **The Rise of Sovereign Usuru**
| Libira Assembly Hall (Torchlight, raised spears)
| The boy takes the name **Usuru**, rallying Libira's quiet complaints into open fury.
| Seeing the warped distance as intentional malice, Usuru convinces Libira to crown him young ruler and declare a war of annexation against the Capital, inciting neighboring kingdoms to join.

| **13**
| **The Six-Power Conflagration**
| Continental War Map (Six burning fronts)
| Armies clashing across Libira, the Capital, Great Mau, Old Sacyra, the Academy, and the Yun-yin Provinces.
| **The Great War** engulfs the known world.

| **14**
| **The Infusion of Tenebris**
| Academy Arcane Forge (Deep twilight violet)
| Prince Casatti channeling the anomalous energy spike from the interrupted ritual into the mundane Sword of War.
| Synthesizing his study of the Dreamscape, world elements, and the rupture spike, Casatti creates the **Sword of Tenebris** to enact swift **Absolute Justice**.

| **15**
| **The General of Reconciliation**
| Capital Command Tent (Warm lantern light)
| Prince Mi-ren taking command of the army, vowing to seek Usuru's forgiveness.
| Mi-ren leads the defense not for conquest, but to restore peace and mend the broken trust with Usuru.

| **16**
| **The Discovery of Lux**
| Academy Spire & Battlefield (Golden radiance)
| Mi-ren adapting the temporal projection ritual into a golden defensive barrier (**Lux**), sparking the Magic vs. Science debate.
| Using what he learned from projecting the King into the temporal world, Mi-ren infuses the mundane Shield of Peace into the **Shield of Lux**.

| **17**
| **Tempering the Void**
| Frontlines of the Great War (Gold & violet clash)
| Mi-ren's Lux barrier containing and stabilizing Casatti's Tenebris strikes against coalition lines.
| Demonstrates that Lux is the only force able to temper Tenebris, bringing the continental war to a decisive end in just **7 years**.

| **18**
| **The Dissipation at Parley**
| Border Truce Grounds (Thick mist)
| Mi-ren offering reconciliation to Usuru; Usuru's violent surge of hatred causing the ambient Lux to dissolve.
| Proves the metaphysical law: any violent emotional shift causes Lux to dissipate instantly, just as it did on the day of the ritual rupture.

| **19**
| **The Joint Realm Accord**
| Grand Council Chamber (Six regional banners)
| Leaders ratifying the treaty establishing the "Joint Realm, Separate Kingdoms" political order.
| Solidifies the federated geopolitical structure that persists into the modern era of Sacyra.

| **20**
| **The Prophecy of Kings**
| Room of Sages / Royal Archives
| Chrono recording the legend of the twin artifacts awaiting a bearer of true Equilibrium.
| The First King's symbols of Peace and War are enshrined in the **Prophecy of Kings**, while Usuru retreats into the dark to become **The Dark Lord**.

== 2. Scene-by-Scene Narrative Walkthrough

=== Scene 01: Symbols of Iron
* **Atmosphere:** An ancient blacksmith workshop bathed in the deep amber glow of coal embers. Hammers strike rhythmic, resonant beats on an anvil.
* **Cinematics:** Steam hisses violently as a plain, unadorned kite shield and a straight broadsword are pulled from water troughs. There are no glowing runes, no magical stones, and no celestial sigils.
* **Thematic Grounding:** Sacyra's founding armaments were forged by mortal hands as mundane civic symbols of Peace and War. Power did not reside in the iron; it resided in the covenant of the sovereign who held them.

=== Scene 02: The Crown and the Scroll
* **Atmosphere:** High-vaulted marble halls of Sacyra Castle flooded with pure morning sunlight.
* **Cinematics:** The First King, majestic and warm, speaks with his two teenage sons. Prince Mi-ren kneels to receive the ceremonial signet of succession. Prince Casatti stands erect, clutching parchment scrolls and an astrolabe, smiling as he formally declines the throne to establish The Academy.
* **Thematic Grounding:** Establishes the harmonious brotherhood between governance (Mi-ren) and empirical inquiry (Casatti) before history fractured.

=== Scene 03: The Self-Running Realm
* **Atmosphere:** Vast panoramic shots of bustling provincial markets, lush terraced farms, and judicial courtyards under clear azure skies.
* **Cinematics:** Merchants trade freely; magistrates resolve disputes with open books; no armed patrols or royal inquisitors hover in the shadows. The throne room stands empty not from neglect, but because the machinery of state functions flawlessly.

=== Scene 04: The First Festival & The Coma
* **Atmosphere:** The vibrant, colorful sprawl of the inaugural Festival of Peoples.
* **Cinematics:** Seven days of joyous celebration give way to silence in the royal bedchamber. The First King collapses as the music fades. The camera slowly pushes past his dormant physical eyes into a starry, gossamer void: his consciousness stands upright, alive and lucid in The Dreamscape.

=== Scene 05: The Four-Year Rescue Effort
* **Atmosphere:** The dim, arcane candlelight of an Academy laboratory conduit chamber.
* **Cinematics:** For four consecutive years during festival week, teenage Mi-ren and Casatti channel glowing ether arrays toward their father’s spectral silhouette across the thinned veil, desperately trying to pull him back into physical reality.

=== Scene 06: The Passing at the Fifth
* **Atmosphere:** Cold, somber blue candlelight in the royal sanctum.
* **Cinematics:** The First King's mortal chest draws its final breath; his physical heart ceases. Yet across the gossamer veil, his golden silhouette remains awake. The Castle ministers and the teenage princes lower their heads in solemn consensus: the vigil must continue until Mi-ren is of age.

=== Scene 07: The Quiet Complaint of Libira
* **Atmosphere:** Bleak, windswept savanna and rugged canyons of the remote southern frontier.
* **Cinematics:** A lone royal courier gallops past solitary marker stones under scorching sun and freezing night, taking seven grueling days to reach an isolated frontier village without a single relay outpost. In a humble thatch hut, a nameless baby is born.

=== Scene 08: The Three-Day Wanderer
* **Atmosphere:** A dynamic, fast-paced montage across muddy trade roads, dusty inns, and river barges.
* **Cinematics:** The growing boy arrives in hamlets, helps villagers for three days, packs his light sack on dawn of the fourth day, and departs. He stays nowhere longer than three days—until the camera tracks him entering the golden gates of the Capital for the annual week-long Festival of Peoples.

=== Scene 09: The Plague Year
* **Atmosphere:** Chilly autumn mist hanging over a muted, uneasy festival concourse in the 11th year.
* **Cinematics:** Royal heralds announce from the battlement that the King has been stricken with illness and will not appear. The eleven-year-old boy stares up at the empty balcony with furrowed brows and rising dread.

=== Scene 10: Behind the Veil
* **Atmosphere:** Claustrophobic, echoing shadows of the forbidden inner keep.
* **Cinematics:** The boy slips through heavy oak doors into the projection chamber, watching from behind a stone pillar as Mi-ren chants over the long-dead, mummified body of the First King, projecting his spectral double onto the temporal dias. The boy's eyes widen in sheer horror at what he perceives as a monstrous fraud.

=== Scene 11: The Shattered Ritual & Birth of Tenebris
* **Atmosphere:** A blinding, violent detonation of midnight-violet and solar-gold.
* **Cinematics:** Tripping in his panic, the boy stumbles into the chalk glyphs and crystal anchors. The ethereal tether snaps with the sound of cracking glass; the First King is pulled backwards into the astral deep. From the boy's chest erupts an anomalous spike of pure cosmic void: **Tenebris** enters waking reality.

=== Scene 12: The Rise of Sovereign Usuru
* **Atmosphere:** The smoky, torchlit Great Hall of Libira.
* **Cinematics:** Standing before tribal chiefs and town elders, the youth raises a spear, his voice ringing with cold fury. He proclaims his name: **Usuru**. Spears clash against shields as Libira crowns their young sovereign and pledges to march on the Capital.

=== Scene 13: The Six-Power Conflagration
* **Atmosphere:** A vast tactical war table transitioning into panoramic battlefields lit by burning trebuchets.
* **Cinematics:** Six banners clash across smoking battlefields: Libira, The Capital, Great Mau, Old Sacyra, The Academy, and Yun-yin. Sacyra's golden peace dissolves into total war.

=== Scene 14: The Infusion of Tenebris
* **Atmosphere:** The subterranean resonance forge of The Academy.
* **Cinematics:** Prince Casatti holds the mundane iron broadsword over a crystal crucible containing the harvested violet rift energy. Chanting harmonic formulas, he quenches the steel in void essence. The blade turns star-black with rippling violet mist: the **Sword of Tenebris** is born for Absolute Justice.

=== Scene 15: The General of Reconciliation
* **Atmosphere:** The somber, parchment-strewn command tent of the Royal Army.
* **Cinematics:** Prince Mi-ren inspects defensive troop dispositions. He speaks to his captains not of conquest or retaliation, but of holding the line so that he may face Usuru and beg for his forgiveness.

=== Scene 16: The Discovery of Lux
* **Atmosphere:** An open battlefield at dawn, bathed in blinding celestial gold.
* **Cinematics:** Facing an enemy barrage, Mi-ren steps to the front ranks and raises the mundane iron shield. Channelling the memory of his father's temporal projection, radiant golden light solidifies into a geometric barrier: **Lux** manifests to shield the living.

=== Scene 17: Tempering the Void
* **Atmosphere:** A violent clash of solar-gold and abyssal-violet along the forward redoubts.
* **Cinematics:** Casatti's vanguard unleashes the severing edge of Tenebris, cutting through enemy siege engines, while Mi-ren's radiant Lux barrier contains the destructive blast wave. The two forces synchronize: Lux tempers Tenebris, bringing decisive victory in seven short years.

=== Scene 18: The Dissipation at Parley
* **Atmosphere:** A desolate borderland shrouded in thick morning mist.
* **Cinematics:** Mi-ren stands before Usuru with shield lowered, offering his hand and admitting the Crown's fault. Usuru's face twists with bitter, unyielding hatred. As Usuru roars in fury, Mi-ren's golden Lux barrier flickers and instantly dissolves into thin air, proving that violence in the heart shatters the light.

=== Scene 19: The Joint Realm Accord
* **Atmosphere:** The Grand Council Chamber in the reconstructed Capital.
* **Cinematics:** Envoys from all six sovereign factions sign their seals upon parchment treaties. The federated principle of "Joint Realm, Separate Kingdoms" is ratified, ensuring autonomous provincial governance under the High Crown.

=== Scene 20: The Prophecy of Kings
* **Atmosphere:** The timeless, floating crystalline library of the Room of Sages.
* **Cinematics:** Chrono the Sage dips a quill of starlight into an inkwell of void, recording the prophecy of the twin relics and the champion who must one day achieve Equilibrium. In the final shot, Usuru descends down stone stairs into the abyssal vaults, as shadow consumes his silhouette and history forgets his name, calling him only **The Dark Lord**.
