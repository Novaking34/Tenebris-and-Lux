== Overview & Core Thesis

Rather than mapping protagonists solely by the physical armaments they carry, the narrative architecture
of *Tenebris and Lux* links the ancient genesis era to the modern campaign through a psychological triad:
**Elijah is to Usuru**, **Katrina is to Prince Casatti**, and **The Custom Bearer (`<chosen_name>`) is to Prince Mi-ren**.
Governed by the foundational axiom **"Trauma is Inherited, Destiny is Chosen,"** this document establishes
how ancient grievances echo into the modern campaign and how player agency determines whether history repeats
its tragedies or achieves cosmic reconciliation.

== 2. Elijah Ro-enheart as Usuru (The Frontier Wound & Redemptive Counterpoint)

Although Elijah wields the **Shield of Lux** like Prince Mi-ren, his internal wound and psychological
arc directly mirror **Usuru**:

=== 2.1. The Child of the Forgotten Frontier
Usuru grew up on the neglected periphery of Libira, where royal couriers required seven days to arrive
and no imperial outposts existed. Elijah grows up in **Dio Village**, an isolated border settlement where
Grandma Magolia laments: _"This town used to be full of life... The kingdom doesn't pay much attention to
this place anymore"_. Both youths embody the quiet complaint of the forgotten border.

=== 2.2. The Tenth-Year Fracture
Usuru experienced ten years of innocent pilgrimage to the Festival of Peoples before his eleventh visit
shattered his faith in the Crown. Elijah’s childhood similarly froze on his **10th birthday**—the morning
his father, **Commander Gareth Ro-enheart**, vanished under King Jihad's sealed royal orders. When Grandma
Magolia asks Elijah to inspect the upstairs chest in Dio Village, Elijah immediately clings to that decade-old
wound: _"Maybe it is something about dad! I haven't seen him since my 10th birthday. Sure thing. This might
be my chance to get answers!"_.

=== 2.3. The Redemptive Choice
When Usuru discovered the First King's mummified body and the secret stasis projection, his innocent faith
collapsed into bitter wrath, birthing Tenebris and sparking a war of vengeance. Elijah begins with unquestioning
faith in the Royal Guard, but is forced to uncover King Jihad's treason in the throne room. Rather than
succumbing to destructive malice, Elijah chooses to raise the **Shield of Lux** to protect the vulnerable,
serving as Usuru granted a second chance at moral redemption.

== 3. Lady Katrina Jaren as Prince Casatti (The Renouncing Blade of Truth)

Lady Katrina Jaren carries the **Sword of Tenebris**, but her ideological kinship belongs to **Prince Casatti**:

=== 3.1. Renouncing Aristocratic Privilege
Just as Prince Casatti voluntarily renounced his royal birthright to the High Throne to pursue empirical truth,
Katrina actively flees her aristocratic heritage in **House Jaren**—one of the wealthiest noble families on the
Royal Court. She refuses to be bartered as a diplomatic pawn in court intrigues.

=== 3.2. Merit Over Pedigree
Casatti founded The Academy because he believed understanding the cosmos required objective observation rather
than imperial decree. Katrina seeks the **Adventurers' Guild**, a frontier brotherhood where practical grit,
swordsmanship, and survival skills outweigh bloodlines and heraldic crests.

=== 3.3. Surgical Severance for Justice
Casatti infused the Sword of Tenebris to execute **Absolute Justice**—slicing through entrenched siege engines
to end a seven-year stalemate. Katrina wields the Sword of Tenebris with cynical, irreverent pragmatism,
slicing through aristocratic hypocrisy, feudal exploitation (the St. Baron), and provincial inertia.

== 4. The Custom Bearer as Prince Mi-ren (The Steward of Balance)

When the player selects a custom name (`<chosen_name>`), the relics decouple from fixed protagonists,
placing the player into the ideological position of **Prince Mi-ren**:

=== 4.1. The Overarching Macro-Steward
While Elijah focuses on the military/institutional conspiracy of the Royal Guard, and Katrina navigates
the underground rebellion of the Guild, the Custom Bearer holds **The Dream Compass**, navigating the
astral tides of The Dreamscape exclusively at night.

=== 4.2. Bearing the Weight of Synthesis
To achieve completion, the Custom Bearer must perform the "double work" along the `[-100, +100]` **Moral Sway**
continuum, catching fragments of both Elijah's and Katrina's paths. They stand where Mi-ren stood during the
7-Year Great War: attempting to hold a fracturing world together through reconciliation.

=== 4.3. The Burden of Stasis vs. True Equilibrium
The Custom Bearer faces Mi-ren's original dilemma. They can succumb to the **Benevolent Stasis** ending—freezing
Sacyra in a protective golden barrier where no growth can occur—or they can transcend Mi-ren's mistake by
mending the planar tear in the Room of Sages, reconciling the ancient wound of Usuru, and unlocking the
**Secret Chronicler True Ending**.
