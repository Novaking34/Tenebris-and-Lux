== Overview

In the authentic 2017–2018 demo script and canonical narrative architecture, the ultimate
antagonist is designated strictly as **The Dark Lord**. He is not a generic cardboard demon,
but the tragic culmination of three millennia of festering bitterness born from the catastrophe
of the 11th Festival: the ancient Libiran youth **Usuru**.

== 1. Ground-Truth Canonical Identity & Origin

* **The Nameless Wanderer of Libira:** Born on the remote southern frontier of Libira during
  the First King's reign, he grew up as a restless three-day wanderer whose sole enduring devotion
  was his annual week-long pilgrimage to the Capital for the Festival of Peoples.
* **The 11th Year Rupture:** Seeking an audience with the sovereign during the rumored "plague,"
  the eleven-year-old boy discovered the First King's mummified corpse and Prince Mi-ren's
  protective projection ritual. Viewing the vigil as a monstrous deception, his terror triggered
  an accidental collision with the ritual array.
* **The Birth of Tenebris:** The catastrophic collapse of the boy's innocent faith produced an
  anomalous metaphysical surge that birthed raw **Tenebris** into reality. Taking the name
  **Usuru**, he rallied Libira and border kingdoms to wage the 7-Year Great War of annexation.
* **The 3,000-Year Descent:** Following his armistice with Prince Mi-ren under the Accord of Kings,
  Usuru refused spiritual reconciliation. He retreated into the subterranean abyssal vaults, where
  his unresolved grief and fury curdled over three thousand years until his mortal name was lost
  to history, remembered only with terror as **The Dark Lord**.

== 2. Narrative Function: The Overarching "A Plot"

* **Existential Backdrop:** The Dark Lord is not an early-game hallway encounter. He represents
  the overarching existential shadow ("The A Plot") looming over Sacyra.
* **Foreground vs. Background:** While provincial survival (Dio Village), noble exploitation
  (St. Baron), and King Jihad's treason form the active foreground ("B Plot" and "C Plot"),
  the awakening of The Dark Lord informs the dread of the entire continent.
* **The Metaphysical Siege Window:** The Dark Lord strategically aligns his vanguard strikes
  with the annual **Festival of Heroes**, knowing that the boundary to the Dreamscape thins to
  gossamer during that week, providing a planar bridge to reclaim the surface world.

== 3. "The King's Curse" & Possession of King Jihad

* **The Planar Rift:** When Usuru shattered the First King's stasis array 3,000 years ago, he left
  a jagged planar wound directly beneath the dias of the High Throne.
* **Psychic Conduit:** For three millennia, sovereigns were exposed to ethereal backwash. When
  King Jihad attempted to harness void power to usurp the Dark Lord, Jihad's megalomania inverted
  the conduit, allowing a dormant remnant of Usuru's consciousness to possess the reigning monarch.
* **Two-Half Campaign Resolution:** In Act I, heroes confront the possessed King Jihad; in Act II,
  they must travel into the deep abyssal threshold to face The Dark Lord himself.

== 4. Metaphysical Neutrality & True Resolution

* **Not Elemental Evil:** Darkness (Tenebris) is a cosmic apex force of rest, mystery, and severance.
  The Dark Lord represents Tenebris corrupted by an unhealed heart of vengeance.
* **The Ugly Truth:** Defeating The Dark Lord through violence alone (Neutral Ending) leaves the First
  King marooned and the cycle dormant. Only by confronting "The Ugly Truth"—acknowledging Usuru's
  valid childhood pain and achieving the **Stance of Equilibrium**—can heroes permanently release
  Usuru's tortured soul and heal the realm.
