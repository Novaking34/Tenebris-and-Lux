== 1. Astral Parasites of the Slumber Realm

**Dream Eaters** are shifting metaphysical entities native to the borders of The Dreamscape.

* **Nature**: Astral parasites that feed on unresolved regrets, fears, and psychic vulnerabilities.
* **Combat Role**:
  - Manifesting during Chrono's trial, they serve as the opening combat challenge in the game.
  - **Base Vigor**: $70$ Threat HP.
  - **Defensive Attributes**: $11$ Physical Defense (DEF), $8$ Magic Defense (MDF).
  - **Flank Vigor**: $70$ HP representing flanking swarms susceptible to *Cuttatio Tenebris* splash damage.
* **Significance**: Defeating the Dream Eaters demonstrates the bearer's ability to maintain mental fortitude and twin-relic resonance, breaking free of the dream's illusions.
