== Overview

This directory contains the canonical world bible, cosmology, character dossiers,
and socio-political history of the **Kingdom of Sacyra** in *Tenebris and Lux*.

== Canonical Manifest

1. **[world_and_lore.adoc](file:///home/professorseanex/Tenebris%20and%20Lux/docs/lore/world_and_lore.adoc)**  
   *The Chronicles of Sacyra*: The foundational world bible detailing:
   - The primordial neutrality of Lux (Radiance) and Tenebris (Darkness).
   - The genesis and mechanics of the Shield of Lux and Sword of Tenebris.
   - The Kingdom of Sacyra (Crown, Royal Guard, Noble Houses, Adventurers' Guild).
   - The existential invasion of Dark Lord Malakor from the Abyssal Threshold.
   - The moral sway continuum of mortal wielders.

2. **[characters.adoc](file:///home/professorseanex/Tenebris%20and%20Lux/docs/lore/characters.adoc)**  
   *Dramatis Personae*: Complete character dossiers, lineage, and TRPG doctrines:
   - **Elijah Ro-enheart**: Son of the former Royal Guard Head; stalwart defender of Sacyra; bearer of the Shield of Lux.
   - **Katrina Jaren**: Noble-born daughter of House Jaren; Adventurers' Guild aspirant; swift wielder of the Sword of Tenebris.
   - Dual-protagonist synergy, social conflicts, and tactical battlefield dynamics.
   - **Dark Lord Malakor** and the void-blighted vanguard.
