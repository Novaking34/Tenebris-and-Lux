== Overview

This directory contains the canonical world bible, macro-chronology, storyboard sequences,
character dossiers, and socio-political history of the **Kingdom of Sacyra** in *Tenebris and Lux*.

== Canonical Manifest

1. **[world_and_lore.adoc](file:///home/professorseanex/Tenebris%20and%20Lux/docs/lore/world_and_lore.adoc)**  
   *The Chronicles of Sacyra*: The foundational world bible detailing:
   - Primordial neutrality of Lux (Radiance) and Tenebris (Darkness).
   - Law of Tempering and Dissipation.
   - Mundane inception of the twin symbols under the First King and the two sons (Mi-ren and Casatti).
   - The 11th Festival rupture, Usuru, the 7-Year Great War, and the 3,000-year dynastic wave.
   - Tri-perspective protagonist architecture and the TRPG combat paradigm.

2. **[genesis_and_historical_canon.adoc](file:///home/professorseanex/Tenebris%20and%20Lux/docs/lore/genesis_and_historical_canon.adoc)**  
   *Chronicles of Antiquity & Macro-History*:
   - Eras I–VI: Founding, Coma, Silent Vigil, Meaning of "Of Age", Libira's quiet complaint.
   - The 11th Festival catastrophe, Usuru's naming, the 7-Year Great War across 6 powers.
   - Casatti's infusion of Tenebris (Absolute Justice) and Mi-ren's discovery of Lux (Reconciliation).
   - The 3,000-year dynastic wave (30–50 kings) and the five axioms of established dynastic order.

3. **[master_storyboard_chronicle.adoc](file:///home/professorseanex/Tenebris%20and%20Lux/docs/lore/master_storyboard_chronicle.adoc)**  
   *Master Storyboard Sequence (Scenes 01–20)*:
   - 20-scene cinematic walkthrough from the founding forges to the Prophecy of Kings.
   - Setting & tone, visual focus, and narrative/thematic beats.

4. **[thematic_triad.adoc](file:///home/professorseanex/Tenebris%20and%20Lux/docs/lore/thematic_triad.adoc)**  
   *Thematic Architecture: Past, Present & Future Triad*:
   - "Trauma is Inherited, Destiny is Chosen".
   - Usuru / Elijah Ro-enheart (The Frontier Wound / Redemptive Grace).
   - Prince Casatti / Lady Katrina Jaren (The Renouncing Blade / Truth & Merit).
   - Prince Mi-ren / The Custom Bearer (The Steward of Balance / Synthesis).

5. **[characters.adoc](file:///home/professorseanex/Tenebris%20and%20Lux/docs/lore/characters.adoc)**  
   *Dramatis Personae*: Complete character dossiers, lineages, and TRPG doctrines:
   - **Elijah Ro-enheart**: Heavy vanguard anchor; bearer of the Shield of Lux.
   - **Lady Katrina Jaren**: Agile skirmisher; wielder of the Sword of Tenebris.
   - **The Custom Bearer**: The Secret Chronicler holding The Dream Compass.
   - **King Jihad**: Monarch of ambition; grotesque inverse of the founding dynasty.
   - **The Dark Lord**: Usuru's 3,000-year descent in the abyssal vaults.
