== Overview

The **Whispering Woods** (`LOC_WHISPERING_WOODS`) form an ancient, dense primeval canopy
encircling **Dio Village**, separating the peaceful hamlet from the windswept frontier of
the **Sacyran Marches**. Named for the haunting acoustic phenomenon produced by autumn winds
filtering through hollow ironwood boughs, the forest serves as the player's first physical
wilderness trial after awakening from **The Dreamscape**.

[quote, Eric the Lumberjack, Dio Village Timber Yard]
____
Those woods were quiet when my grandfather worked the timber. Now, you can't walk twenty
paces off the road without hearing shadows moving in the thicket. Something deep in the
capital is leaking into the roots.
____

== 1. Geography & Environmental Atmosphere

* **Location Identifier**: `LOC_WHISPERING_WOODS` (Integer index `2` in `location_id_t`).
* **Territorial Domain**: Southwestern province of the Kingdom of Sacyra.
* **Topographical Neighbors**:
  - **South**: Borders the agricultural plots and timber yards of **Dio Village**.
  - **North & East**: Ascends into the crags and signal hills of the **Sacyran Marches**.
* **Flora & Terrain**: Gnarled ironwood trunks, glowing amber fungi, thick moss carpets,
  and narrow cobblestone paths choked by invasive briers and fallen timber.

== 2. Lore & Ancient Secrets

Local Sacyran folklore claims the woods were planted by Prince Casatti during his frontier
expeditions to serve as a natural barrier shielding the commoners from imperial court politics.
Beneath the dense root network lie forgotten stone markers from Era I, indicating that the forest
grew atop an ancient tectonic fault line directly connected to the abyssal fissure beneath the
Royal Capital.

****
**Archival Marginalia: The Soughing Boughs**

When night falls across the canopy, travelers report hearing murmurs that sound like voices
debating in the archaic dialect of Era IV. Scholars from The Academy believe these are acoustic
reflections of the First King's sealed court, vibrating along ley lines from the Dreamscape.
****

being isolated.

=== Environmental Grid Invariants
1. **Tree & Granite Rock Obstacles**: Completely block line-of-sight and unit transit.
2. **Dense Brier Patches**: Inflict $2.0\times$ movement cost and $-10$ HP per step for non-flying units.
3. **Forester's Shrine (`SHRN`)**: Standing adjacent to the shrine grants $+15\%$ Lux warding efficiency.
