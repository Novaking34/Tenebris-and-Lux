== Overview

The **Royal Capital of Sacyra** is the administrative, spiritual, and military apex of the
kingdom. Crowned by the gleaming white-stone bastions of the **High Throne Citadel**, the
city embodies the architectural zenith of Era I's founding dynasties. Yet beneath its
ceremonial grandeur, opulent marble halls, and courtly pageantry lies a festering metaphysical
wound: **The King's Curse**, an unhealed planar rupture beneath the throne room floor that
has leaked abyssal corruption for three millennia, steadily eroding the sanity of **King Jihad**.

[quote, High Priest Roté, Royal Sanctum Secret Journal]
____
We wash the marble with lavender and rosewater, but the stones remember the blood.
When the sun reaches its zenith, the shadow of the throne does not fall toward the door—it
falls inward, pooling like dark oil around the King's feet. He smiles at the envoys,
but his teeth are gray. Sacyra is dancing upon the lid of a tomb.
____

== 1. Geography, Architecture & Urban Districts

* **Topographical Location**: Built upon the central continental plateau overlooking the Sacyran river basin.
* **Access Routes**: Reached via the Grand Imperial Causeway traversing the **Sacyran Marches**.
* **Architectural Style**: Monolithic white limestone, soaring arched flying buttresses, gilded
  radiant domes, and deep subterranean catacombs dating back to the Accord of Kings.

[cols="1,2", options="header"]
|===
| District | Description & Socio-Political Function
| **The Citadel Ward** | The innermost fortress housing the High Throne, the Royal Council Chambers,
  and the private quarters of King Jihad.
| **The Aristocratic Quarter** | Palatial townhouse estates of the Great Noble Houses (including House Jaren);
  seat of courtly intrigue and political scheming.
| **The Grand Cathedral of Lux** | Spiritual center governed by High Priest Roté, where the sacred
  relics are celebrated and celestial portents are interpreted.
| **The Lower River Ward** | Densely packed commoner quarter, guildhalls, iron forges, and the local
  headquarters of the **Adventurers' Guild**.
| **The Subterranean Vaults** | Forbidden pre-dynastic catacombs directly beneath the throne, site of the 11th Festival Rupture.
|===

== 2. Lore & The King's Curse Planar Wound

During the 11th Festival of Peoples, the young Libiran wanderer Usuru accidentally struck the
ritual focus stone during the First King's communion ritual. The resulting explosion severed the
First King's soul and cracked the bedrock beneath the dais:

1. **The Hidden Rupture**: The First 30 Kings sealed the physical chamber beneath massive basalt
   slabs inscribed with warding runes, building the High Throne directly over the scar to mask its presence.
2. **The 3,000-Year Dynastic Wave**: Every monarch who sat upon the High Throne was bathed in the
   slow, imperceptible radiation of the planar breach. While strong-willed sovereigns resisted its
   whispers, weak or ambitious rulers inevitably succumbed to paranoia and tyrannical megalomania.
3. **The Possession of King Jihad**: In the present era, King Jihad's obsession with matching the
   legendary military feats of his ancestors drove him to open the basalt seal. Tenebrous vapor
   now directly commands the monarch's nervous system, transforming him into an unwitting puppet
   of **The Dark Lord**.

[WARNING]
====
The planar wound beneath the High Throne is not merely a narrative backdrop—it serves as the
**Midpoint Campaign Fork** in the ludological engine. Entering the throne room without the
*Dream Compass* triggers the tragic *Neutral Path*, whereas possessing the calibrated compass
allows the party to expose the curse and enter the *True Ending Path*.
====

== 3. Factions & Urban Intrigue

The Capital is torn between four competing power structures:

* **The Royal Guard**: Commanded by hardline royalists loyal to the throne, increasingly bewildered
  by King Jihad's erratic and tyrannical decrees.
* **House Jaren & The High Nobles**: Aristocratic court faction maneuvering to consolidate tax
  monopolies and replace the Ro-enhearts' military influence.
* **The High Priesthood**: Church hierarchy caught between religious dogma and High Priest Roté's
  terrifying revelations regarding the cosmic corruption of the Crown.
* **The Adventurers' Guild**: Underground network of commoner veterans and sympathizers (including
  Katrina Jaren) operating outside royal law to protect the citizenry from unexplained void manifestations.

=== Battlefield Invariants
1. **The Planar Wound (`VOIDC`)**: Generates an aura of $-15$ HP decay per turn to all units
   standing within a 2-cell radius unless protected by *Lux Barrio*.
2. **Marble Pillars (`PILLR`)**: Provide heavy cover ($+30\%$ evasion against ranged magic).
3. **Royal Dais Steps (`STEPS`)**: Units attacking upward suffer a $-15\%$ accuracy penalty.
