== Overview

The **Sacyran Marches** (`LOC_SACYRA_MARCHES`) encompass the windswept limestone hills,
fortified military outposts, and ancient battlefields stretching between the **Whispering Woods**
and the white-stone bastions of the **Royal Capital**. Established during the Seven-Year Great
War as the kingdom's primary defensive perimeter, the Marches serve as the tactical proving ground
where the player transitions from local woodland skirmishes to large-scale military engagements.

[quote, Commander Gareth Ro-enheart, Field Dispatch - Frontier Fortress Redoubt]
____
The Marches are where boyish dreams of glory die and real soldiers are forged. The wind here
carries no warmth—only dust, iron, and the perpetual vigilance of men who know that if these
ridges fall, nothing stands between the abyss and our homes.
____

== 1. Geography & Strategic Topology

* **Location Identifier**: `LOC_SACYRA_MARCHES` (Integer index `3` in `location_id_t`).
* **Territorial Domain**: Central border province linking southern farmlands with the high plateaus.
* **Topographical Neighbors**:
  - **West / South**: Slopes gently downward into the canopy of the **Whispering Woods**.
  - **North / East**: Connects directly to the grand paved highway leading to the **Royal Capital**.
* **Terrain Features**: Terraced limestone plateaus, fortified palisades, stone watchtowers,
  and deep defensive trenches left behind by centuries of border warfare.

== 2. Lore & The Legacy of the 7-Year Great War

During the Seven-Year Great War (approximately 3,000 years ago), Usuru's coalition forces launched their primary
armored spearhead across these very hills. The earth of the Marches remains soaked in residual
abyssal and radiant energies:

* **The Trenches of the First 30 Kings**: Deep geometric trenches dug into the chalk hills to funnel
  void automata into kill zones.
* **The Sunken Citadel**: A subterranean fortress carved into the limestone bedrock where Commander
  Gareth Ro-enheart made his legendary stand before his mysterious disappearance following Elijah's
  10th birthday.
* **The Broken Standards**: Decaying heraldic banners of ancient kingdoms (Great Mau, Libira, Old Sacyra)
  still flutter from iron spires atop the limestone ridges.

[NOTE]
====
The Marches are the first location where the player encounters organized military opposition rather
than mindless wild beasts. Enemies employ tactical formations, archery volleys, and coordinated
flanking maneuvers.
====

=== Battlefield Mechanics
1. **Elevated Ramparts (`RAMP`)**: Grants ranged units $+1$ Attack Range and $+20\%$ Physical Defense.
2. **Muddy Trench (`TRENCH`)**: Costs $2.5\times$ movement points to traverse and prevents defensive parries.
3. **Iron Gate (`GATE`)**: Destructible obstacle ($150$ HP) that must be breached or bypassed via flanking ladders.
