== Overview & Pastoral Heritage

**Dio Village** is a pastoral woodland settlement situated in the southwestern valley frontier of the
**Kingdom of Sacyra**. Functioning as the physical inception of the hero's journey upon awakening
from **The Dreamscape**, Dio Village serves as the terrestrial anchor of Chapter 1, embodying the modern
continuation of ancient Libira's systemic neglect under the 3,000-year dynastic order.

== 1. Socio-Political Role: "The Quiet Complaint" of Modern Sacyra

* **Mirror of Ancient Libira:** Just as ancient Libira suffered a 7-day communication delay and lacked
  imperial relay outposts, modern Dio Village is neglected under the "Joint Realm, Separate Kingdoms"
  bureaucracy. As Grandma Magolia sighs in Map 021: _"This town used to be full of life. *sighs*
  The kingdom doesn't pay much attention to this place anymore..."_.
* **Economic Fragility & Class Exploitation:** While the Capital enjoys insulated peace, Dio Village
  faces monster outbreaks near the timber lines. **Eric the Lumberjack** faces ruin because monsters block
  the woods, while the predatory **St. Baron** exploits commoners through exorbitant rents and usury.
* **Gareth Ro-enheart's Shadow:** Dio Village is where Commander Gareth Ro-enheart placed his son Elijah
  under Grandma Magolia's care before his mysterious disappearance on Elijah's 10th birthday.

== 2. Key Landmarks & Dual-Perspective Quest Hubs

* **Grandma Magolia's Cottage (Maps 020 & 021):** The warm stone hearth where the protagonist awakens.
  Contains the upstairs chest housing **Grandma's Watch** (activating the Layer 0 monotonic clock).
  - *Elijah's Approach:* Hesitates out of filial respect and family etiquette.
  - *Katrina's Approach:* Seeks the will immediately to escape the dead-end town.
* **Eric's Timber Yard & Test of Honesty (Map 028):** Filling Eric's rent pot shifts Moral Sway positively
  and rewards the **Ring of Fire**, enabling flame-weave relic embellishments.
* **Monica's Mystic Grove & Lyri's Offering (Maps 023, 027, 029):** Former Royal Battlemage Monica guards
  the memorial totem of fallen soldiers. Stopping the bandit kid and buying an offering potion for **Lyri,
  the Goddess of Peace**, attunes the relics to arcane resonance and awards the **Book of Monsters**.
* **The St. Baron's Estate & John the Thief (Maps 030 & 031):** Deciding whether to aid **John the Thief**
  in robbing the corrupt baron versus upholding feudal property law shapes the penetration or bastion
  embellishments of the relics.

== 3. Mechanical Role in the C11 Simulation Engine

* **Engine Enumeration:** Registered in `include/sim/sim_types.h` as `LOC_DIO_VILLAGE`.
* **Sanctuary Province:** Zero hostile vigor during standard state; permits inventory management,
  dialogue interactions, moral compass calibration, and Layer 0 monotonic time passage.
* **Terrestrial Lockout:** Gated throughout Chapter 1, preventing return to The Dreamscape until
  the acquisition and awakening of **The Dream Compass**.
