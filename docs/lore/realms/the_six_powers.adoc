== Overview

The historical landscape of *Tenebris and Lux* was shaped by the clash and eventual reconciliation
of **The Six Powers** during Eras IV through VI. When the planar rupture during the 11th Festival of
Peoples shattered the First King's Pax Sacyrana, Usuru rallied these disparate sovereign nations
into the catastrophic **Seven-Year Great War**. Understanding the cultural, architectural, and
military profile of each power is essential to comprehending the ancestral factions, regional
alliances, and diplomatic endings available within the TRPG campaign.

[quote, The Accord of Kings, Preamble - Inscribed upon the Six Obelisks of Unity]
____
No single crown shall claim the sun, and no lone throne shall command the shadows.
Six thrones bled upon the salt and marble; six thrones shall swear that the realm shall
be joint in peace, yet separate in kingdom, so long as the stars circle the spindle.
____

== 2. Comprehensive Profiles of the Six Powers

=== 1. The Royal Capital of Sacyra (The Radiant Center)
* **Domain & Geography**: The fertile central plains and plateaus surrounding the White Rim Citadel.
* **Cultural Philosophy**: Monarchy, institutional duty, legalism, and devotion to the light of the First King.
* **Military Doctrine**: Heavy vanguard infantry, phalanx formations, and divine warding priests wielding
  the techniques of the *Shield of Lux*.
* **Key Figure**: King Jihad (Present Era); Prince Mi-ren (Founding Era).

=== 2. The Kingdom of Libira (The Arid Frontier)
* **Domain & Geography**: The harsh western desert canyons, sandstone mesas, and salt flats.
* **Cultural Philosophy**: Radical communal self-reliance, stoic endurance, and fierce resentment of
  centralized royal arrogance ("The Quiet Complaint").
* **Military Doctrine**: Agile camel skirmishers, desert outriders, and shadow assassins who pioneered
  the early stances of the *Sword of Tenebris*.
* **Key Figure**: Usuru the Wanderer (The Dark Lord).

=== 3. The Great Mau Kingdom (The Merchant Empire of the East)
* **Domain & Geography**: The sprawling eastern river deltas, rich alluvial floodplains, and bustling port cities.
* **Cultural Philosophy**: Mercantilism, contract law, civic guilds, and wealth accumulation.
  To the Mau, war is an economic calculation rather than an ideological crusade.
* **Military Doctrine**: Mercenary phalanxes, river ironclad barges, and repeating crossbowmen funded
  by the grand banking cartels.
* **Role in the Great War**: Financed Usuru's coalition armies in exchange for mining rights in Libira,
  switching sides at the midpoint when Sacyra offered trade tariff exemptions.

=== 4. Old Sacyra (The Highland Ancestral Cradle)
* **Domain & Geography**: The sheer mountain peaks and pine forests north of the Sacyran river valley.
* **Cultural Philosophy**: Strict aristocratic traditionalism, ancestral veneration, and genealogical pride.
  View the modern Royal Capital as degenerate upstarts who diluted pure Sacyran blood.
* **Military Doctrine**: Mountain rangers, greatsword shock troops, and heavy cavalry capable of fighting
  on steep inclines.
* **Role in the Great War**: Joined Usuru early to punish the Capital, but broke away when Usuru
  unleashed uncontrollable void constructs into mortal villages.

=== 5. The Academy (The Spire of Pure Reason)
* **Domain & Geography**: The frozen northern tundras and geothermal volcanic springs.
* **Cultural Philosophy**: Scientism, arcanotech experimentation, and the empirical dissection of cosmic forces.
  Reject both religious worship of Lux and superstitious fear of Tenebris.
* **Military Doctrine**: Alchemical siege engines, elemental clockwork automata, and crystal-resonance artillery.
* **Role in the Great War**: Remained neutral for five years before joining the coalition to study the
  abyssal tear firsthand, later contributing the mathematical formulas used to draft the *Accord of Kings*.

=== 6. The Yun-yin Provinces (The Valleys of Mist & Wind)
* **Domain & Geography**: The subtropical southeastern archipelago and bamboo cloud forests.
* **Cultural Philosophy**: Spiritual harmony, ki manipulation, cyclical flow, and the balance of dualities.
  Understood the metaphysical neutrality of darkness long before Sacyran theologians.
* **Military Doctrine**: Martial artists, wind-runners, and agile spear masters using evasion and
  counter-poise redirection.
* **Role in the Great War**: Sided with Usuru to restore balance against Sacyra's overbearing expansionism,
  ultimately brokering the peace conference that created the *Joint Realm, Separate Kingdoms* doctrine.

== 3. The Accord of Kings: "Joint Realm, Separate Kingdoms"

Following seven years of devastating continental slaughter, the exhausted sovereigns met upon the
neutral plains of the Central Marches to sign the historic **Accord of Kings**:

[cols="1,3", options="header"]
|===
| Article | Diplomatic Covenant & Systemic Law
| **Article I: Mutual Sovereignty** | Each of the Six Powers maintains complete legislative, military,
  and economic autonomy within their borders.
| **Article II: The Joint Ward** | Sacyra's Royal Guard is legally entrusted with guarding the
  central rift beneath the High Throne, but may never station troops within foreign realms without consent.
| **Article III: The Free Caravan** | Courier and trade routes between all six nations are declared
  sacrosanct, guaranteeing that no province may be starved through communication blockade.
|===

[NOTE]
====
In the modern era, King Jihad's clandestine efforts to annex the frontier and resurrect the
centralized empire violate the fundamental tenets of the Accord of Kings, providing the political
justification for the party's multi-ending divergence.
====
