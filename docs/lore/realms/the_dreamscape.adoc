== Overview & Metaphysical Essence

**The Dreamscape** (also known as the Astral Substrate or Room of Sages) is an ethereal plane
of consciousness, ancestral memory, and timeless trials existing between waking reality and slumber.
Grounded in the recovered 2017–2018 demo script (Maps 006, 008, 011, 041, 042) and verified in the
ISO C11 engine architecture as `LOC_THE_DREAMSCAPE`, it serves as the cosmological origin point
where the protagonist awakens before entering the temporal Kingdom of Sacyra.

== 1. Historical & Metaphysical Provenance

* **The Coma of the First King:** Following the inaugural Festival of Peoples 3,000 years ago,
  the First King collapsed into a coma. His spirit was found fully lucid and awake inside The Dreamscape.
  While Prince Mi-ren and Prince Casatti sustained projections for ten festivals, the 11th Festival
  tampering by Usuru snapped the ethereal tether, permanently sealing the First King's soul here.
* **The Annual Thinning of the Veil:** For one week each year—coinciding with the Festival of Heroes—the
  boundary ("the Veil") separating the temporal world from The Dreamscape thins to gossamer, allowing
  mortals to project desires across the threshold.
* **The Three Laws of Crossing the Veil:**
  1. *The Mirror of Intent:* The ether reflects mortal intent. Pure devotion projects harmony; terror,
     spite, or megalomania (such as King Jihad's treason) inverts the conduit into waking nightmares.
  2. *The Severed Tether:* Sustaining a long bridge across the veil carries fatal risk. Violent
     interruption snaps spiritual tethers, marooning consciousness permanently.
  3. *The Dream Eater Infiltration:* Tearing or weakening the veil attracts **Dream Eaters**—parasitic
     entities native to the deep astral voids that devour mortal memories, dreams, and ancestral records.

== 2. Mechanical Integration in the C11 Engine

* **Engine Enumeration:** Registered in `include/sim/sim_types.h` as `LOC_THE_DREAMSCAPE`.
* **Opening Trial:** The protagonist awakens in the Hall of Sages with **Chrono the Sage**, attuning
  to class archetype stones and testing `Lux Barrio` and `Cuttatio Tenebris` against Dream Eaters before
  Chrono's command (*"AWAKEN!"*) transports consciousness to Grandma Magolia's hearth in Dio Village.
* **Terrestrial Gating:** Throughout Chapter 1 (the demo), return to The Dreamscape is barred, anchoring
  the player in terrestrial human stakes.
* **The Dream Compass & Nocturnal Phasing:** Upgraded from Grandma's Watch, the Dream Compass allows
  entry to The Dreamscape exclusively during nocturnal hours (`20:00 <= hour || hour <= 04:00`).
* **Tactical LeTBS Environments:** Comprises floating platform grids, shifting crystal leylines,
  and astral mechanisms that restore MP while exposing units to planar shear.
