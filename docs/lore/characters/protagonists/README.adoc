== Overview

The narrative of *Tenebris and Lux* is propelled by two contrasting protagonists:
**Elijah Ro-enheart**, the disciplined frontline vanguard bearing the **Shield of Lux**,
and **Katrina Jaren**, the sharp-witted noble outrider wielding the **Sword of Tenebris**.
Neither hero embodies pure virtue or pure vice; their choices determine the moral
equilibrium of the Kingdom of Sacyra.
