== Overview

The narrative core and mechanical heartbeat of *Tenebris and Lux* is the
interplay between **Elijah Ro-enheart** and **Katrina Jaren**. Contrasting in
social caste, philosophical outlook, and combat rhythm, their evolving
partnership mirrors the cosmic relationship between Light and Darkness.

== 2. Interpersonal Chemistry & Banter

Elijah and Katrina embody the classic dramatic friction between an earnest,
rule-following guardian and an irreverent, sharp-witted rogue. Where Elijah
pauses to consult protocol or verify authority, Katrina acts impulsively,
cutting through bureaucratic red tape with sarcastic practicality.

=== Narrative Evolution Across the Campaign
1. **Act I (The Provincial Divide)**: Initial friction in Dio Village. Katrina
   mocks Elijah's military formality; Elijah cautions against Katrina's
   cynical disregard for property and propriety.
2. **Act II (The Capital Conspiracy)**: Arrival at Sacyra Castle. Katrina's
   insight into aristocratic deception exposes King Jihad's corruption,
   forcing Elijah to choose between his oaths to the throne and his duty to
   the kingdom.
3. **Act III (The Sovereign Eclipse)**: Total metaphysical synchronization.
   Facing Dark Lord Malakor, the two heroes overcome their individual biases,
   passing relics fluidly between each other to unlock the Stance of Equilibrium.

== 3. Tactical Synergy on the TRPG Battlefield

On the tactical grid, Elijah and Katrina form a mutually dependent combat pair:

1. **The Phalanx Pivot**: Elijah pins enemy vanguards with his Shield of Lux
   ZOC aura. Katrina uses high mobility to circle the battle line and strike
   flanked enemies.
2. **Warded Severance**: Katrina lures high-threat enemies into charging toward
   her, stepping back onto Elijah's tile perimeter as Elijah executes a
   staggering shield deflection.
3. **The Stance of Equilibrium**: In moments of ultimate synchronization,
   both relics resonance counters combine, unlocking unified abilities where
   radiant starlight and abyssal void strike in a single coordinated turn.
