== Overview

**Dio Village** is the historic starting settlement in the southwestern province
of the **Kingdom of Sacyra**. A provincial community once teeming with trade and
prosperity, the village has fallen into economic stagnation as the central Crown
focuses resources on the capital. Dio Village is sharply divided into the
pastoral **Upper Ward** and the impoverished, exploited **Lower Ward**.

== Topographical & Social Strata

* **Upper Ward (Dio Upper)**:
  - *Main Square & Plaza*: Community gathering place, bordered by ancestral homes.
  - *Grandma's House*: Home of Grandma Magolia, keeper of *Grandma's Watch*.
  - *Monica's Grove*: Sanctuary of Monica the Mystic and her elemental familiar Lyri.
  - *Eric's Hearth*: Modest dwelling of Eric, former lumberjack.
* **Lower Ward (Dio Lower)**:
  - *Shopping District*: Local shops struggling under crushing taxes.
  - *The St. Baron's Estate*: Lavish residence of the exploitative baron.
  - *The Thief's Den*: Shadows where John the Thief operates.
