== Overview

Beyond mortal kings and knights exist the **Sages and Ancients**—immortal
scholars and archivists who maintain the cosmic chronicles of Sacyra. They
understand the true origin of the **Shield of Lux** and **Sword of Tenebris**,
watching the cycles of light and darkness across the centuries.
