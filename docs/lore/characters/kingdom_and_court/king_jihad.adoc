== Overview

**King Jihad** (referenced in ancient annals as *D. Jihad*) is the reigning
monarch of the **Kingdom of Sacyra**. Outwardly a dignified sovereign presiding
over the chivalric traditions and the **Festival of Heroes**, King Jihad secretly
harbors a perilous ambition: to seize and harness the forbidden abyssal power of
the returning Dark Lord Malakor to establish absolute dominion.

== 1. Identity & Sovereign Office

* **Full Name**: King Jihad of Sacyra (Monarchical Title: *D. Jihad*)
* **Office**: Crown Sovereign, King of Sacyra, High Commander of the Realm
* **Seat of Power**: The High Citadel of Sacyra Castle
* **Political Standing**: Balances the aristocratic Noble Houses against the
  meritocratic Adventurers' Guild while commanding the elite Royal Guard.

== 2. Psychological Profile & Secret Ambition

King Jihad is calculating, authoritative, and outwardly composed. While his
subjects view him as a protector against chaos, he views the resurgence of the
ancient Darkness not as a tragedy to be repelled, but as an apex weapon to be
claimed. Believing himself superior to both holy creeds and dark lords, he
seeks to subvert the cosmic void to overthrow all limits on his crown.

== 3. Socio-Political Role in the Sacyra Campaign

1. **State Censorship & Denial**: When the mysterious storm and rising island
   manifest along the frontier, King Jihad silences border outposts and
   forbids the public from learning the truth before the Festival of Heroes.
2. **The Disappearance of Commander Gareth**: Historical records suggest
   Gareth Ro-enheart's sudden departure under sealed royal orders was
   connected to King Jihad's clandestine investigations into the Abyssal Vaults.
3. **The Catalyst for Rebellion**: When Elijah and Katrina uncover that the
   Crown is actively pursuing void corruption rather than safeguarding the
   realm, Elijah is forced into open defiance against the very throne his family
   swore to protect.
