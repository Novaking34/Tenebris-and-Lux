== Overview

The **Kingdom of Sacyra** is anchored by the Crown, protected by the **Royal
Guard**, and influenced by competing **Noble Houses** on the Royal Court. While
the public honors the Crown during the **Festival of Heroes**, shadow politics
and dangerous covetousness fester beneath the royal halls.
