== Overview

Three millennia prior to the awakening of the Relic Bearer in Dio Village, the Kingdom of
Sacyra and its neighboring realms were shaped by three pivotal historical figures whose
choices laid the foundations of the current world: **Prince Mi-ren**, **Prince Casatti**,
and **Usuru the Wanderer**. Their deeds, philosophies, and tragic conflicts establish the
thematic spine of the *Tenebris and Lux* overarching canon.

== Historical Dossier Index

1. **xref:prince_mi_ren.adoc[prince_mi_ren.adoc]**
   *The Sovereign of the Vigil & Architect of Sacyra*: The elder founding prince who walked
   the Path of Light, founded the Royal Guard and civil administration, and held the legendary
   Four-Year Vigil over his brother. Progenitor of the Custom Bearer's *Steward of Balance* axis.

2. **xref:prince_casatti.adoc[prince_casatti.adoc]**
   *The Outrider Prince & Blade of Truth*: The younger founding prince who walked the Path of
   Darkness, awakened from a four-year coma with transfigured vision, and forged the Sword of
   Tenebris to sever corruption. Spiritual ancestor to Lady Katrina Jaren.

3. **xref:usuru_the_wanderer.adoc[usuru_the_wanderer.adoc]**
   *The Sovereign of Grief & The First Broken Crown*: Born nameless in remote Libira, he walked
   the world as the Three-Day Wanderer seeking royal aid for his people. Corrupted by the 11th
   Festival planar rupture, he crowned himself King of Libira and ignited the Seven-Year Great
   War, ultimately falling to darkness to become The Dark Lord.
