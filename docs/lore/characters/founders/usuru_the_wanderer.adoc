== Overview

Before he was feared across the ages as **The Dark Lord**, he was a nameless boy born to an
impoverished family on the arid borders of the **Kingdom of Libira** during Era IV. Known
as the **Three-Day Wanderer** because he could never bear to settle in any settlement for
more than three sunsets, he held an unyielding, reverent devotion to the First King and the
splendor of the Capital. Driven by the "Quiet Complaint" of his neglected homeland and
catastrophically altered by the snapping of the planar tether during the 11th Festival of Peoples,
Usuru chose his name in defiance, crowned himself sovereign of Libira, and declared the
devastating **Seven-Year Great War**.

[quote, Sovereign Usuru, Speech to the Assembly of Libira - Dawn of the Great War]
____
We waited seven days for their riders to tell us if our children were allowed to starve.
We bowed when their golden heralds told us that peace demanded our silence. But when I
reached out to touch the King's light, it burned my skin and sealed our skies. They did
not leave us behind by mistake—they left us behind because we were quiet. Libira will be
quiet no longer.
____

== 1. Identity & Origin

* **Birth Identity**: Nameless Boy of Libira (Born to a frontier shepherd family).
* **Chosen Name**: **Usuru** (Ancient Libiran root meaning *"He Who Remembers What Was Buried"*).
* **Historical Era**: Era IV–V (approximately 3,000 Years Ago, Pre-Dynastic Wave).
* **Titles**: The Three-Day Wanderer; Sovereign of the Red Desert; Vanguard of the Broken Crown;
  later corrupted across the millennia into **The Dark Lord**.
* **Affiliation**: Kingdom of Libira; Supreme Commander of the 6-Power Coalition against Sacyra.

== 2. The Wanderer's Pilgrimage & The "Quiet Complaint"

Due to the lack of an outpost relay system, a message sent from the Royal Capital took seven
grueling days of desert riding to reach Libira. While the capital feasted, Libira struggled
against drought, bandit raids, and forgotten infrastructure.

1. **The Three-Day Law**: Growing up, the boy walked the breadth of the continent, staying no more
   than three days in any village. He listened to commoners, blacksmiths, and outriders, cataloging
   the quiet complaints of the forgotten provinces.
2. **The Annual Pilgrimage**: The sole exception to his three-day rule was the annual **Festival of
   Peoples** in the capital, where he stayed an entire week, gazing with awe at the High Throne and
   the First King.
3. **The 11th Year Catastrophe**: During the 11th festival, determined to deliver a petition for a
   Libiran relay road, the boy slipped into the Inner Sanctum beneath the High Throne. Startled by
   guards, he accidentally struck the ritual focus stone. The planar tether snapped, venting primordial
   abyssal energy that sealed the First King in the Dreamscape and violently warped the distance
   between Libira and Sacyra into an impenetrable spatial rift.

[WARNING]
====
Believing the sudden spatial expansion was a deliberate imperial curse cast by the Sacyran court
to permanently exile Libira, the boy's devotion inverted into pure, consuming hatred.
====

== 3. Ascension, War & Metaphysical Corruption

Returning across the warped desert passes, the youth took the name **Usuru**, mounted the dais
of the Libiran assembly hall, and proclaimed the end of subservience:

* **The Crown of Libira**: Libirans, enraged by generations of neglect and royal silence, crowned
  Usuru by unanimous acclamation.
* **The 6-Power Conflagration**: Forging military alliances with the Great Mau Kingdom, Old Sacyra,
  and the Yun-yin Provinces, Usuru ignited the **Seven-Year Great War**.
* **Descent into Ruin**: To match the invulnerable barrier of the *Shield of Lux*, Usuru dipped his
  soul repeatedly into the abyssal breach beneath the battlefield, forging void constructs and
  commanding *Dream Eaters*. By the war's end, his humanity had burned away, leaving behind the
  eternal, brooding sovereign now remembered only as **The Dark Lord**.

== 4. Thematic Mirror: Elijah Ro-enheart & Usuru

Usuru serves as the tragic historical negative to **Elijah Ro-enheart**:

* Both originate from the marginalized periphery (frontier Libira vs. neglected Dio Village).
* Both suffer from the abrupt disappearance of a revered paternal figure (The First King vs. Commander Gareth).
* Where Usuru allowed his frontier grievance to consume him into becoming a monster of vengeance,
  Elijah must learn to acknowledge his family's pain without repeating Usuru's destructive crusade.
