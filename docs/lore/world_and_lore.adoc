== Canonical Introduction

This document establishes the living world-building canon, cosmological ontology,
and historical epic of *Tenebris and Lux* ("Darkness and Light"). 
At its core, this is the story of two primordial relics—**The Shield of Lux** and 
**The Sword of Tenebris**—and a noble Kingdom fractured by civil strife, creeping 
corruption, and the catastrophic re-awakening of the **Dark Lord**.

== 1. The Primordial Cosmology: Neutrality of the Apex Forces

[quote, The Ancient Covenant of Sol-Tenebrae]
"Light without shadow is a blinding desert that calcifies the soul.
Shadow without light is an endless void where memory sleeps.
The Shield protects and confines; the Sword severs and defends.
The metal knows neither virtue nor malice—only the hand that grips the hilt."

In the cosmology of *Tenebris and Lux*, the cosmos is governed by two apex forces that
transcend mortal morality. They are neither benevolent nor malevolent:

=== Lux (The Apex of Radiance)

* **Ontological Essence**: Manifestation, illumination, cohesion, preservation, and structure.
* **The Dual Nature of Light**:
  - **Righteous Manifestation**: Offers sanctuary to the vulnerable, dispels terrifying deceptions, preserves cultural wisdom, and reveals truth.
  - **Tyrannical Manifestation**: Scorches the land, blinds those who seek nuance, enforces merciless stasis, and calcifies living beings into unyielding stone.

=== Tenebris (The Apex of Darkness)

* **Ontological Essence**: Mystery, dissolution, rest, severance, and the fertile void.
* **The Dual Nature of Darkness**:
  - **Virtuous Manifestation**: Veils the weak from tyrannical gaze, severs unholy chains, allows the weary to sleep and heal, and pierces through arrogant illusions.
  - **Malevolent Manifestation**: Sows suffocating paranoia, consumes compassion, unleashes ravenous entropy, and aids the destructive ambitions of the Dark Lord.

=== The Moral Sway of the Wielder

Neither force can be judged in the abstract. Good and evil belong entirely to the
**intent, conscience, and actions of the wielder**. A knight wielding the blinding
radiance of Lux can become a cruel oppressor, while a shadow-knight wielding the
abyssal edge of Tenebris can act as the ultimate savior of an enslaved realm.

== 2. The Twin Relics: Shield and Sword

Before the founding of the Kingdom, the Primordial Architects forged two armaments
to maintain cosmic equilibrium across the land:

[cols="1,2,2", options="header"]
|===
| Relic | The Shield of Lux | The Sword of Tenebris
| **Aspect** | The Sovereign Aegis of Radiance | The Sovereign Blade of the Abyss
| **Physical Form** | A towering kite-shield of burnished solar silver, centered with a living celestial orb that pulses with warm golden resonance. | A two-handed greatsword forged from star-quenched obsidian, its edge rippling with deep twilight violet and mist.
| **Primal Function** | Warding, barrier generation, radiant illumination, kinetic deflection, and stasis containment. | Severance, reality piercing, armor penetration, mist-veiling, and entropy unleashing.
| **Virtuous Wielding** | Wards innocents against onslaught; creates safe havens; unmasks deceptions. | Slices through tyrants' armor; cuts unholy binds; cloaks the fleeing in merciful shadow.
| **Corrupted Wielding** | Blinds subjects into obedience; traps souls in eternal petrified stasis. | Slaughter; terrorizing the helpless; tearing down the pillars of civilization.
|===

=== Relic Resonance & Synergy

The Shield and the Sword are not antagonistic enemies; they are metaphysical twins.
- When raised together in harmony (**The Stance of Equilibrium**), the Shield anchors the defender's footing while the Sword carves a path through encroaching evil.
- If one relic is lost or corrupted without the counterweight of the other, the wielder risks being consumed by either calcifying light or hungry shadow.

== 3. The Kingdom Divided: Sol-Tenebrae in Crisis

The Kingdom of **Sol-Tenebrae** once stood as a testament to the balance between
day and night. Its cities were designed with sunlit spires for study and moonlit
groves for rest and meditation.

=== The Schism of the High Courts

Generations of peace bred dogma. The Kingdom fractured into two warring ideologies:

1. **The Order of the Radiant Solar**:
   - Zealous purists who claim Lux is the only divine power.
   - Seek to banish all shadows, burning night markets and outlawing sleep during daylight.
   - Their inquisitors wield solar scepters to forcibly "purge" citizens of shadow.

2. **The Umbral Covenant**:
   - Outcasts, subterranean guilds, and shadow-scholars who retreat into deep catacombs.
   - Believe the Solar Order has blinded the Kingdom to truth.
   - Some maintain peaceful stewardship of the dark, but radical sects embrace nihilistic destruction.

=== The Re-Awakening of the Dark Lord

Exploiting the Kingdom's internal fracture, the ancient harbinger known in whispers as
the **Dark Lord Malakor** has broken the primordial seals of the Abyssal Vault.

* The Dark Lord is **not** Tenebris itself; he is a mortal who achieved immortality by
  twisting the void into a weapon of domination.
* Malakor seeks to claim both the **Sword of Tenebris** to sever the Kingdom's defenses
  and the **Shield of Lux** to petrify the surviving populace into eternal, unblinking submission.
* His vanguard—the **Umbral Legions** and **Blighted Automata**—now march across the
  provinces, laying siege to the border marches.

== 4. Topography of the Fractured Realm

The current journey begins across the contested threshold of the Kingdom:

=== 1. The Garrison Altar (`LOC_GARRISON_ALTAR`)
* **Setting**: The ancestral redoubt of the Wardens. Ancient banners hang above an
  altar of twin pedestals where the Shield and Sword once rested in peace.
* **Role**: The nexus of awakening and strategic council. Here the Champion chooses
  how to attune the relics before marching to the frontlines.

=== 2. The Sunken Citadel Gates (`LOC_CITADEL_GATES`)
* **Setting**: Massive battlements of white granite scorched by void-fire. The gates
  stand half-shattered, besieged by the Dark Lord's vanguard.
* **Role**: Tactical chokepoint. Raising the Shield of Lux repels the siege engines;
  drawing the Sword of Tenebris allows the Champion to strike down the siege captains.

=== 3. The Fractured Marches (`LOC_DIVIDED_MARCHES`)
* **Setting**: A windswept highland where Solar Order knights and Umbral Covenant partisans
  clash in blind, tragic civil war while the real evil approaches.
* **Role**: Moral crucible. The Champion must choose whether to force peace through
  overwhelming light, strike from the shadows, or demonstrate the harmony of both relics.

=== 4. The Abyssal Threshold (`LOC_DARK_LORD_THRESHOLD`)
* **Setting**: The shattered gates to the Dark Lord's dread monolith, where the fabric
  of reality bends and shadows take physical, razor-sharp forms.
* **Role**: The vanguard of the final confrontation. Demands mastery of both the
  Shield's warding brilliance and the Sword's devastating severance.

== 5. The Champion's Stances & Gameplay Paradigm

Rather than a survival clock measuring dying embers, the Champion actively shifts
between four fundamental combat and narrative stances:

1. **Stance of the Aegis (Shield Dominant)**:
   - *Mechanic*: Maximizes Lux Ward power; creates impenetrable defensive barriers;
     deflects projectile curses; reveals cloaked enemies.
   - *Ethical Risk*: Excessive reliance on the Shield induces defensive passivity and
     risks calcifying the wielder's heart against empathy.

2. **Stance of the Edge (Sword Dominant)**:
   - *Mechanic*: Unleashes Tenebris strike resonance; pierces enchanted armor; severs
     demonic bindings; slips through enemy lines inside a cloak of mist.
   - *Ethical Risk*: Excessive bloodlust risks feeding the blade's hunger, eroding
     the wielder's restraint into ruthless brutality.

3. **Stance of Equilibrium (Twin Mastery)**:
   - *Mechanic*: Channels the Shield and Sword simultaneously. The Shield parries
     attacks while the Sword counter-strikes.
   - *Requirement*: Demands deep internal poise and balanced moral alignment.

4. **The Moral Sway**:
   - Every major deed shifts the Champion's resonance toward **Virtue (Stewardship & Mercy)**
     or **Malice (Domination & Ruin)**, altering how factions react and determining the
     fate of the Kingdom.
