== Canonical Introduction

This document establishes the living world-building canon, cosmological ontology,
and historical epic of *Tenebris and Lux* ("Darkness and Light"). 
Conceived in 2014–2018 as the foundational game project of **CreativeWorkzStudio**, 
this is the epic of two primordial relics—**The Shield of Lux** and 
**The Sword of Tenebris**—set within the storied **Kingdom of Sacyra** (pronounced *"saw-kee-ruh"*).
The story is driven by two central protagonists: **Elijah Ro-enheart**, son of a former head
of the Royal Guard, and **Katrina Jaren**, a noble-born daughter navigating royal court expectations
while yearning for the Adventurers' Guild, as the re-awakening **Dark Lord Malakor** rises from the abyss.

== 1. The Primordial Cosmology: Neutrality of the Apex Forces

[quote, The Sacyran Chronicle of Antiquity]
"Light without shadow is a blinding desert that calcifies the spirit.
Shadow without light is a suffocating void where purpose drowns.
The Shield preserves and confines; the Sword severs and liberates.
The relics know neither virtue nor malice—only the soul that wields them."

In the cosmology of *Tenebris and Lux*, the cosmos is governed by two apex forces that
transcend human morality. Neither is inherently good nor evil:

=== Lux (The Apex of Radiance)

* **Ontological Essence**: Manifestation, illumination, cohesion, preservation, and structure.
* **The Dual Nature of Light**:
  - **Virtuous Manifestation**: Wards the vulnerable with impenetrable golden barriers, unmasks dark deceits, preserves cultural knowledge, and inspires steadfast courage.
  - **Tyrannical Manifestation**: Scorches the earth in unyielding glare, blinds those seeking mercy, enforces inflexible order, and petrifies living will into stone.

=== Tenebris (The Apex of Darkness)

* **Ontological Essence**: Mystery, dissolution, rest, severance, and the fertile void.
* **The Dual Nature of Darkness**:
  - **Virtuous Manifestation**: Veils the oppressed from predators, cuts through unholy bonds and tyrant armor, grants restful oblivion to the weary, and pierces through arrogant illusions.
  - **Malevolent Manifestation**: Spreads creeping rot, feeds paranoia, dissolves conscience into nihilism, and serves the destructive malice of the Dark Lord.

=== The Moral Sway of the Wielders

Neither Lux nor Tenebris dictates a character's morality. Good and evil belong entirely to the
**intent, conscience, and actions of the wielder**. In the Kingdom of Sacyra, a knight channeling
the blinding radiance of Lux can act as a cold oppressor, while a warrior wielding the void-quenched
blade of Tenebris can stand as the people's ultimate protector.

== 2. The Twin Relics: Shield of Lux & Sword of Tenebris

Before the crowning of Sacyra's first monarch, primordial celestial smiths forged two sovereign
armaments to anchor the realm against cosmic oblivion:

[cols="1,2,2", options="header"]
|===
| Relic | The Shield of Lux | The Sword of Tenebris
| **Aspect** | The Sovereign Aegis of Radiance | The Sovereign Blade of the Abyss
| **Physical Form** | A towering kite-shield of burnished solar silver, centered with a living celestial sunstone that pulses with warm golden resonance. | A two-handed greatsword forged from star-quenched obsidian, its edge rippling with deep twilight violet mist and void-tempered steel.
| **Primal Function** | Warding, kinetic deflection, barrier projection, illumination, and stasis shielding. | Severance, armor piercing, shadow manipulation, reality-edge slicing, and mist cloaking.
| **Virtuous Wielding** | Wards allies, shields innocents from siege engines, and unmasks lurking infiltrators. | Slices through corrupted war machines, cuts captive chains, and strikes from the dark to liberate.
| **Corrupted Wielding** | Blinds subjects into terrified compliance; traps souls in eternal petrified stasis. | Indiscriminate butchery; feeding the blade's hunger on mortal dread; total subjugation.
|===

=== Harmonized Equilibrium

The Shield and Sword were never intended to war against one another; they are complementary halves
of a cosmic balance:
- When wielded in tandem or in synchronized coordination by trusted companions (**The Stance of Equilibrium**), the Shield deflects kinetic onslaught while the Sword opens devastating counter-attack avenues.
- If one relic is wielded without the tempering influence of the other, its bearer risks succumbing to either calcifying stasis or devouring void-sickness.

== 3. The Kingdom of Sacyra: Setting & Society

The **Kingdom of Sacyra** (pronounced *"saw-kee-ruh"*, `/sɔːˈkiːrə/`) is a grand, historic realm
defined by stone bastions, sweeping borderlands, and deeply rooted chivalric traditions.

=== Governance and Courtly Politics

* **The Crown & The Royal Guard**: Sacyra's royal palace commands the central citadel. The **Royal Guard** represents the pinnacle of martial honor and personal devotion to the throne, sworn to protect the realm against external ruin and internal treachery.
* **The Noble Houses**: Hereditary aristocratic families hold vast regional fiefs, supply knights to the royal army, and compete for influence and appointed seats on the prestigious **Royal Court**.
* **The Adventurers' Guild**: An independent, meritocratic brotherhood operating throughout Sacyra's frontier outposts and merchant crossroads. Unlike the rigid court or the ceremonial nobility, the Guild takes on perilous monster contracts, frontier patrols, and ancient ruin expeditions, valuing practical skill and personal freedom above noble pedigree.

=== The Crisis: The Re-Awakening of Dark Lord Malakor

After centuries of dormancy sealed beneath the Abyssal Vaults along Sacyra's furthest borders,
the ancient terror **Dark Lord Malakor** has broken free.

* Malakor is not a personification of Tenebris, but a corrupt mortal arch-sorcerer who twisted dark magic into a tool of catastrophic conquest.
* Seeking to conquer Sacyra, Malakor commands legions of void-blighted fiends, corrupted constructs, and dread vanguard captains.
* His vanguard has already shattered the outer border redoubts, threatening to overrun Sacyra unless heroes arise who can awaken and master the legendary Shield and Sword.

== 4. The Dual Protagonists

The heart of *Tenebris and Lux* is the intertwined journey of two young figures from opposite ends
of Sacyra's social fabric:

=== 1. Elijah Ro-enheart (The Vanguard of Duty)

* **Lineage**: Born into a proud line of soldiers who have served in Sacyra's royal army for generations. His father was a legendary former head of the **Royal Guard**.
* **Role & Archetype**: Frontline combatant, tactician, and stalwart protector. Raised under a strict code of chivalric duty, martial conditioning, and sacrificial defense.
* **Character Dilemma**: Strives to live up to the immense shadow of his father's legacy while questioning whether blind obedience to royal orders truly protects the people of Sacyra when court politics paralyze the realm.
* **Relic Attunement**: Naturally drawn to the **Shield of Lux**, anchoring defensive formations, absorbing enemy assaults, and safeguarding his squad.

=== 2. Katrina Jaren (The Free-Spirited Blade)

* **Lineage**: A daughter of **House Jaren**, one of the most prominent noble families in the Kingdom of Sacyra.
* **Role & Archetype**: Agile skirmisher, perceptive scout, and tactical striker. Possesses natural martial brilliance and a fiercely independent spirit.
* **Character Dilemma**: Raised and groomed since childhood to take a ceremonial seat upon the Royal Court, Katrina suffocates under high-society diplomacy. She secretly longs to break free of courtly expectations and join the **local Adventurers' Guild**, seeking genuine adventure and frontline camaraderie.
* **Relic Attunement**: Naturally resonates with the **Sword of Tenebris**, channeling its fluid, decisive severance to bypass armored juggernauts and cut through enemy flanks.

=== Dynamic Interplay

Together, Elijah and Katrina represent the living synthesis of Sacyra: military discipline paired with adventurer initiative; steadfast defense matched with decisive offense; the golden starlight of Lux intertwined with the twilight edge of Tenebris.

== 5. The Tactical Strategy RPG (TRPG) Paradigm

Unlike standard linear dungeon crawlers, *Tenebris and Lux* is built as a **Tactical Strategy RPG**:

1. **Strategic Grid Battlefield**:
   - Battles take place across multi-elevation grid maps (provinces, castle courtyards, bridges, and mountain passes).
   - Units position according to terrain bonuses, line-of-sight, directional flanking, and movement budgets.
2. **Cinematic Combat Resolution**:
   - When a unit initiates combat against an enemy on the grid, the game transitions into a dynamic **traditional side-scroll / action-based cinematic duel**.
   - Attacks, parries, spell wards, and severance slashes play out with visual weight and player agency before returning to the grid.
3. **Moral Sway Influence**:
   - Dialogue choices, tactical decisions (mercy vs. ruthless execution), and relic usage alter the party's **Moral Sway** (`[-100, +100]`), influencing recruitable companions, kingdom politics, and the story's ultimate conclusion.
