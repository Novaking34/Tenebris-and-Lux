== Overview

This document provides complete biographical dossiers, martial doctrines, and thematic arcs
for the central protagonists and key figures of *Tenebris and Lux* in the **Kingdom of Sacyra**
and **The Dreamscape**. Grounded in the recovered 2017–2018 demo baseline and the Past/Present/Future
thematic triad ("Trauma is Inherited, Destiny is Chosen"), it establishes the tri-perspective narrative
architecture across Elijah Ro-enheart, Lady Katrina Jaren, and the Custom Bearer (`<chosen_name>`).

== 1. Elijah Ro-enheart (The Aegis of Lineage)

[quote, Elijah Ro-enheart]
"My father taught me that a shield is not a wall to hide behind, but a promise made to the people standing behind you."

=== 1.1. Biography & Heritage
* **Full Name:** Elijah Ro-enheart
* **Affiliation:** Kingdom of Sacyra Royal Army (Cadet Vanguard); House Ro-enheart
* **Family Lineage:** The Ro-enhearts have served the Sacyran crown for five unbroken generations.
  Elijah's father, **Commander Gareth Ro-enheart**, was the esteemed former Head of the Royal Guard,
  famed for holding the Sunken Citadel. Gareth vanished under mysterious royal orders on Elijah's
  **10th birthday**, leaving behind unhealed questions and immense expectations.
* **Upbringing:** Raised in the barracks and training quadrangles of the capital citadel, but spends
  his youth in **Dio Village** under the watchful care of **Grandma Magolia**.
* **Visual Profile:** Broad-shouldered, athletic build, steel-blue eyes, and cropped dark hair. Wears
  fitted silver-steel infantry plate over deep navy quilted gambeson, marked with the defensive crest of the Ro-enhearts.

=== 1.2. Thematic Parallel: Mirror of Ancient Usuru
Although Elijah wields the Shield of Lux, his personal arc directly mirrors **Usuru** (The Frontier Wound):
* **The Boy from the Forgotten Border:** Usuru grew up in neglected Libira; Elijah grows up in neglected Dio Village.
* **The Tenth-Year Fracture:** Usuru had ten years of innocent devotion before his world shattered in year 11;
  Elijah's childhood froze on his 10th birthday when his father vanished.
* **Redemption Over Vengeance:** Confronting King Jihad's betrayal, Elijah refuses to burn the kingdom down.
  He raises the Shield of Lux to protect the living, representing Usuru granted a second chance at moral redemption.

=== 1.3. Combat & Tactical Doctrine (TRPG)
* **Battlefield Role (Grid):** Frontline Heavy Vanguard / Fortress Anchor.
* **Grid Attributes:** Heavy physical mitigation, high poise, Zone of Control (ZOC) bottleneck denial, ally interception.
* **Tactical Signature:** `Lux Barrio` — Manifests a radiant geometric barrier dome ($2\dots5$ turn duration,
  $25\%$ damage mitigation).

== 2. Katrina Jaren (The Blade of Freedom)

[quote, Katrina Jaren]
"Silk dresses and court etiquette won't turn back a vanguard blade. Let my cousins argue over tax registries; my place is on the frontier."

=== 2.1. Biography & Heritage
* **Full Name:** Lady Katrina Jaren
* **Affiliation:** House Jaren (Aristocracy of Sacyra); Aspirant of the Adventurers' Guild
* **Family Lineage:** **House Jaren** is an ancient, wealthy noble family commanding lucrative vineyards
  and silver mines in Sacyra's eastern provinces, holding significant patronage leverage on the Royal Court.
* **Upbringing:** Groomed by diplomats and heralds to occupy a ceremonial court seat, Katrina despised courtly
  hypocrisy, frequently escaping to Dio Village and training with Adventurers' Guild rangers.
* **Visual Profile:** Agile, athletic build, sharp amber eyes, and unbound chestnut hair tied in a practical combat braid.
  Clad in tailored lightweight leather arming doublets over noble-weave tunic, with rapier and long-dagger scabbards.

=== 2.2. Thematic Parallel: Mirror of Prince Casatti
Katrina carries the Sword of Tenebris and embodies the philosophy of **Prince Casatti** (The Renouncing Blade):
* **Renouncing Aristocratic Birthright:** Just as Casatti renounced the High Throne, Katrina flees House Jaren.
* **Merit Over Pedigree:** Casatti founded The Academy for empirical truth; Katrina seeks the Adventurers' Guild
  where practical survival and skill outweigh heraldic crests.
* **Surgical Severance:** Wields the Sword of Tenebris with sharp, cynical candor to slice through court corruption,
  feudal extortion (the St. Baron), and provincial stagnation.

=== 2.3. Combat & Tactical Doctrine (TRPG)
* **Battlefield Role (Grid):** High-Mobility Skirmisher / Flanking Striker.
* **Grid Attributes:** 4–5 cell movement, ignores basic ZOC delays, backstab criticals, hit-and-run disengagement.
* **Tactical Signature:** `Cuttatio Tenebris` — Striking primary target while inflicting $20\%$ splash cleave
  to adjacent enemy units.

== 3. The Custom Bearer (`<chosen_name>` — The Secret Chronicler)

=== 3.1. The Third Perspective: Mirror of Prince Mi-ren
When the player customizes their protagonist name via `name <str>`, the relics decouple from fixed identities,
placing the player into the ideological position of **Prince Mi-ren** (The Steward of Balance):
* **Macro-Steward of the Veil:** Holds **The Dream Compass**, entering The Dreamscape exclusively during
  nocturnal windows (`20:00 <= hour || hour <= 04:00`).
* **The "Double Work" of Synthesis:** Navigates the `[-100, +100]` Moral Sway continuum to weave fragments
  of both Elijah's military investigation and Katrina's underground rebellion.
* **Resolving the Stasis Fallacy:** Must avoid repeating Mi-ren's ancient error (Benevolent Stasis) to unlock
  the **Secret Chronicler True Ending**, reconciling the ancient wound of Usuru and permanently mending the planar tear.

== 4. Antagonists & The Two-Half Campaign

=== 4.1. King Jihad (The Sovereign of Ambition — Act I Antagonist)
* **Office:** Reigning Monarch of Sacyra, commanding the Royal Guard.
* **Role:** The grotesque inverse of the Founding Dynasty. Imposes state censorship on border warnings
  and seeks to usurp void power for total dominion.
* **The Possession:** Sitting on the High Throne above the ancient planar wound, Jihad's megalomania inverts
  the conduit, allowing a dormant remnant of Usuru (**The Dark Lord**) to possess him at the Act I climax.

=== 4.2. The Dark Lord (Usuru's Descent — Act II Antagonist)
* **Canonical Identity:** The ancient Libiran youth **Usuru**, whose faith in the Crown was shattered during
  the 11th Festival.
* **Role:** The overarching existential shadow ("The A Plot") menacing Sacyra from the subterranean Abyssal Vaults.
* **The Final Confrontation:** In Act II, heroes must penetrate the cosmic threshold. Slaying him blindly
  produces the Neutral Ending; achieving the **Stance of Equilibrium** (*Duo-Loca Bashin n Slash*) severs
  his abyssal tether while absorbing planar fallout without dissipating, granting Usuru eternal rest.
