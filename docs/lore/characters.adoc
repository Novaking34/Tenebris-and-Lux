== Overview

This document provides complete biographical dossiers, martial doctrines, and thematic arcs
for the central protagonists and key figures of *Tenebris and Lux* in the **Kingdom of Sacyra**.

== 1. Elijah Ro-enheart (The Aegis of Lineage)

[quote, Elijah Ro-enheart]
"My father taught me that a shield is not a wall to hide behind, but a promise made to the people standing behind you."

=== Biography & Heritage

* **Full Name**: Elijah Ro-enheart
* **Affiliation**: Kingdom of Sacyra Royal Army (formerly Royal Cadet Corps)
* **Family Lineage**: The Ro-enhearts have served the Sacyran crown for five unbroken generations. Elijah's father, **Commander Gareth Ro-enheart**, was the esteemed former Head of the Royal Guard, famed for holding the Sunken Citadel against an allied barbarian siege decades prior.
* **Upbringing**: Raised in the barracks and training quadrangles of the capital citadel. Trained rigorously in shield craft, formation tactics, and military law from childhood.
* **Visual & Demeanor**: Broad-shouldered, armored in fitted silver-steel infantry plate over deep navy gambeson. Earnest, disciplined, observant, and burdened by the immense expectations of his family name.

=== Combat & Tactical Doctrine (TRPG)

* **Battlefield Role (Grid)**: Heavy Frontline Anchor / Guardian Vanguard.
* **Grid Attributes**: High defense, heavy physical mitigation, zone-of-control denial, ally interception.
* **Cinematic Action Style (Side-View)**:
  - Solid, grounded sword-and-shield techniques.
  - Heavy shield-bashes that stagger incoming charges and break enemy poise.
  - Barrier projection: Raises the **Shield of Lux** to cast wide golden auric domes that deflect projectile swarms.

=== Narrative Arc & Internal Conflict

Elijah enters the war believing unquestioningly in military discipline and hierarchy. However, as the
Dark Lord Malakor's invasion accelerates and noble houses in the royal court squabble over political
favor rather than sending reinforcements to border villages, Elijah is forced to confront what true
honor demands: does a soldier serve the written decree of a hesitant court, or the living people of Sacyra?

== 2. Katrina Jaren (The Blade of Freedom)

[quote, Katrina Jaren]
"Silk dresses and court etiquette won't turn back a vanguard blade. Let my cousins argue over tax registries; my place is on the frontier."

=== Biography & Heritage

* **Full Name**: Lady Katrina Jaren
* **Affiliation**: House Jaren (Aristocracy of Sacyra); Aspirant of the Adventurers' Guild
* **Family Lineage**: **House Jaren** is an old, wealthy noble house holding lucrative trade vineyards and silver mines in Sacyra's eastern province. They command significant political clout on the Royal Court.
* **Upbringing**: Groomed by private tutors, diplomats, and court heralds to occupy a powerful hereditary seat on the Royal Court to advance family interests. Katrina despised the stifling, hypocritical court etiquette, sneaking away to train with Guild sellswords and ranger outriders.
* **Visual & Demeanor**: Quick-footed, clad in tailored lightweight leather armor over noble-weave tunic, with rapier and long-dagger sheaths. Quick-witted, fiercely autonomous, skeptical of bureaucratic protocol, and fiercely loyal to companions who earn her respect.

=== Combat & Tactical Doctrine (TRPG)

* **Battlefield Role (Grid)**: High-Mobility Skirmisher / Flanking Striker.
* **Grid Attributes**: High movement tiles, evasive evasion against counter-attacks, armor-piercing critical strikes, hit-and-run tactics.
* **Cinematic Action Style (Side-View)**:
  - Fluid, dynamic acrobatics, quick dashes, and precise target severance.
  - Channels the **Sword of Tenebris** to cloak in mist, closing distances instantly.
  - Unleashes crescent twilight blades that slice clean through heavy automaton plating and void-cursed armor.

=== Narrative Arc & Internal Conflict

Katrina longs for the unvarnished honesty of the **Adventurers' Guild**, where skill and character
matter more than noble pedigree. Yet her noble education gave her a sharp analytical mind and an
understanding of political subterfuge that simple soldiers lack. As she takes up the Sword of Tenebris
alongside Elijah, she must reconcile her thirst for personal freedom with her inescapable responsibility
to the survival of Sacyra.

== 3. Dual-Protagonist Dynamics: The Wielders of Harmony

The heart of the gameplay and story is the contrast and evolving partnership between Elijah and Katrina:

[cols="1,2,2", options="header"]
|===
| Dimension | Elijah Ro-enheart | Katrina Jaren
| **Social Caste** | Military Soldier / Son of the Royal Guard Head | High Aristocracy / House Jaren
| **Institution** | Sacyran Royal Army (Structure, Law, Duty) | Adventurers' Guild Aspirant (Freedom, Merit, Exploration)
| **Relic Affinity** | **The Shield of Lux** (Defense, Preservation, Barrier) | **The Sword of Tenebris** (Offense, Severance, Velocity)
| **Combat Rhythm** | Anchor, Stagger, Absorb, Hold the Line | Flank, Pierce, Evade, Sever Key Threats
| **Moral Perspective** | Starts with Lawful Duty; learns the necessity of independent conscience | Starts with Rebellious Independence; learns the weight of sacrifice and leadership
|===

=== Tactical Synergy on the Grid

* **The Warding Wall**: Elijah positions on a bottleneck grid tile, activating Shield of Lux aura to increase Katrina's evasion and protect her from ranged artillery.
* **The Twilight Riposte**: When an enemy assaults Elijah's shield, Katrina triggers an interception attack from an adjacent grid square, executing a devastating tandem strike.
* **Relic Sharing**: While each hero naturally attunes to one relic, critical narrative crises and high-level TRPG skills allow them to pass or dual-wield the twin armaments, unlocking the supreme **Stance of Equilibrium**.

== 4. Antagonist: Dark Lord Malakor (The Void Sovereign)

* **Identity**: The ancient necromantic lord of the deep Abyssal Vaults bordering Sacyra.
* **Nature**: An immortal warlord who long ago submerged his mortal body in forbidden void mists, Malakor embodies tyrannical ambition.
* **Objective**: Overrun the Kingdom of Sacyra, capture both the Shield of Lux and the Sword of Tenebris, and use their primordial power to permanently extinguish free will across the continent.
* **The Vanguard**:
  - **Umbral Centurions**: Corrupted soldiers encased in blackened, spiked plate armor.
  - **Blighted Automata**: Ancient war-golems reanimated by dark soul-engines.
  - **Void Stalkers**: Ethereal shadow beasts that bypass heavy armor to strike at unit rear lines.
