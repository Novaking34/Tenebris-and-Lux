== Overview & Historiographical Scope

This volume establishes the definitive historical canon, cosmological chronology, and dynastic
chronicle of the **Kingdom of Sacyra** (pronounced *"saw-kee-ruh"*, `/sɔːˈkiːrə/`). Grounded in
the authentic 2017–2018 demo archives and formal narrative architecture, it traces the 3,000-year
continuum from the founding by the First King through the 7-Year Great War, the tragedy of Usuru,
the awakening of the twin relics, the 3,000-year dynastic wave, and the modern crisis under King Jihad.

== 2. Era I: The Founding of Sacyra & The Two Princes

=== 2.1. The Mundane Armaments of Inception

At the founding of the Kingdom of Sacyra, the **Shield of Lux** and the **Sword of Tenebris** were
not magical or celestial artifacts. They were forged as ordinary, non-magical armaments of steel and
silver by royal smiths, presented to the First King as civic symbols of statecraft:

* **The Mundane Shield:** Represented **Peace**, communal defense, and agricultural stability.
* **The Mundane Sword:** Represented **War**, territorial vigilance, and border law enforcement.

=== 2.2. The Self-Running Systems of the First King

The First King was deeply beloved by his subjects across all provinces. Under his leadership, the
kingdom's municipal, legal, and civic institutions were so well-engineered that the realm practically
governed itself. Citizens did not lack access to the King out of tyranny or aloofness, but because
the systems functioned with such frictionless harmony that a personal royal audience was required
only "once in a blue moon".

=== 2.3. The Ideological Fork: Mi-ren and Casatti

The First King had two sons who chose distinct paths while still young teenagers:

* **Prince Mi-ren (The Elder Son):** The rightful heir to the High Throne, raised in administrative
  discipline to inherit his father's covenant of golden peace.
* **Prince Casatti (The Younger Son):** Renounced his royal birthright to the throne to establish
  **The Academy**, dedicating his life to empirical inquiry, elemental sciences, and mapping the
  astral geometries of the ethereal plane.

== 3. Era II & III: The Festival of Peoples, The Coma & The Silent Vigil

=== 3.1. Why the Festival Lasted a Full Week

Every year, the Capital hosted the **Festival of Peoples**—an international gathering where border
demarcations were ratified, continental infrastructure treaties negotiated, and subjects celebrated.
Rather than holding a single-day event, the First King mandated a full seven-day festival out of
deliberate consideration for distant subjects: those with harvest or trade obligations could depart
early, while delegations traveling from the furthest frontiers of the known world were guaranteed
meaningful participation.

=== 3.2. The First Festival Coma & Discovery of The Dreamscape

Immediately following the first grand Festival of Peoples—where the collective emotional consciousness
of an entire continent converged—the First King collapsed into a deep coma. His body remained dormant,
yet his spirit was fully lucid and awake on the other side of the ethereal boundary in **The Dreamscape**.
By tracing his father's displaced consciousness, Prince Casatti and The Academy mapped the Dreamscape
in full.

=== 3.3. Festivals 1 Through 4: The Four-Year Rescue Effort

During the first four years of the coma, the annual festival week—when the boundary ("the Veil")
thinned to gossamer—served as the **surest, most stable window** to access the King. Throughout these
four years, Mi-ren, Casatti, and the court worked tirelessly across the thinned veil attempting to
pull the First King back into his mortal body.

=== 3.4. Era III: The 5th Festival Death & The Meaning of "Of Age"

During the **5th Festival of Peoples**, the First King's physical body died. Pulling him out of the
coma was no longer possible. However, the Castle court continued the festival because that thinned-veil
week was now the **sole remaining lifeline** allowing Mi-ren to commune with his father's lucid spirit
and project his consciousness into the temporal hall in short bursts when rare royal audiences were needed.

This secret was maintained across Festivals 5 through 10 not out of malice, but as a protective vigil:

* **No Precedent for Succession:** Sacyra had never experienced the death of a monarch before; international
  peace treaties were bound to the First King's personal covenant, and no constitutional law existed for a regency.
* **The Chivalric Definition of "Of Age":** Mi-ren and Casatti were teenagers. In Sacyran chivalric law,
  attaining majority required martial maturity to personally command the Royal Guard alongside mastery
  of the Academy's statecraft systems so the self-running realm would not shatter under foreign pressure.

== 4. Era IV: Usuru the Wanderer & The 11th Year Tragedy

=== 4.1. The "Quiet Complaint" of Remote Libira

Far on the southern frontier lay the **Kingdom of Libira**, a sparse, underdeveloped territory separated
from the Capital by a grueling seven-day journey. Because Libira was not yet established enough to maintain
an imperial relay outpost network, communications suffered a chronic seven-day lag. This systemic gap
fostered a simmering **"quiet complaint"** among Libirans regarding the disparity between Capital prosperity
and border isolation.

=== 4.2. The Three-Day Wanderer

During the First King's peaceful reign, a boy was born in Libira to a humble family without being given
a name. For ten years, he grew up traveling the continent as a restless wanderer who lived by a strict
personal rule: **never stay more than three days anywhere**—except for the annual week-long Festival of
Peoples in the Capital, which he attended with pure devotion to honor the King he revered.

=== 4.3. The 11th Year Rupture

During the boy's eleventh annual pilgrimage to the festival, heralds announced that a "royal plague"
kept the First King unseen on his balcony. Anxious for the monarch he adored, the eleven-year-old boy
slipped past castle guards into the royal sanctum to seek an audience.

Inside, the boy discovered the long-dead body and Prince Mi-ren utilizing Academy projection glyphs.
Lacking the statecraft context to understand the court's desperate vigil, the boy viewed the revelation
as a monstrous, hypocritical deception. In his terror and shock, he accidentally collided with the
ritual array, triggering four catastrophic consequences:

1. **The Eternal Astral Seal:** The First King's spiritual tether snapped; his consciousness was permanently
   marooned in **The Dreamscape**.
2. **The Birth of Tenebris:** The violent collapse of the boy's innocent faith into profound betrayal produced
   an anomalous metaphysical shockwave, releasing the cosmic apex force of **Tenebris** (severance and dissolution)
   into the waking temporal world that very day.
3. **The Warped Distance:** The planar backwash physically warped continental leylines, stretching the geographical
   distance between Libira and the Capital into an unnatural chasm that the boy interpreted as an act of deliberate
   hostile separation by the Crown.
4. **The Rise of Sovereign Usuru:** Taking the name **Usuru**, the youth fled back to Libira, revealed the Capital's
   deception, was crowned young ruler of Libira, and rallied neighboring border kingdoms into an alliance to declare
   a war of total annexation against the Capital.

== 5. Era V: The 7-Year Great War & Awakening of the Relics

=== 5.2. Casatti's Infusion of Tenebris (The Sword of War)

Determined to end Usuru's siege quickly and preserve the realm, Prince Casatti analyzed the anomalous
energy spike recorded during the ritual rupture. Synthesizing his knowledge of the Dreamscape and elemental
properties, Casatti isolated **Tenebris** and infused it into the mundane Sword of War. He forged the
**Sword of Tenebris** not out of bloodlust, but to execute **Absolute Justice**—creating a surgical blade
capable of severing siege lines and unyielding armor to halt the war decisively.

=== 5.3. Mi-ren's Discovery of Lux (The Shield of Peace)

Taking command of the Capital's forces, Prince Mi-ren sought not conquest or vengeance, but **atonement
and forgiveness from Usuru**. By reverse-engineering the projection ritual used to anchor ethereal concepts
into physical reality, Mi-ren unlocked **Lux** (cohesion, preservation, and radiance) and infused it into
the mundane Shield of Peace, creating the **Shield of Lux**.

=== 5.4. The Law of Tempering and Dissipation

Battlefield confrontations between the two armaments revealed the foundational cosmic dynamic:

* **Lux as the Singular Tempering Agent:** Raw Tenebris is purely severing and entropic; left unchecked,
  it erodes both wielder and target. Lux is the only force capable of containing and stabilizing Tenebris,
  granting structural cohesion so void strikes can be directed with precision.
* **The Fragility of Radiance (Dissipation):** While Lux stabilizes Tenebris, its golden coherence requires
  steadfast emotional and ethical harmony. Any sudden surge of terror, wrath, or malice causes Lux to
  **dissipate instantly**, leaving Tenebris unbridled.

=== 5.5. The Parley & The 7-Year Armistice

During peace negotiations, Prince Mi-ren lowered his defenses and offered total reconciliation to Usuru.
However, Usuru's deep bitterness erupted in a violent surge of hatred, causing Mi-ren's ambient Lux
aura to dissolve on the spot.

Recognizing that neither side could achieve unconditional victory without mutual annihilation, the six
powers ratified the **Accord of Kings**, bringing the Great War to a close after exactly **seven years**:

* **"Joint Realm, Separate Kingdoms":** Autonomous provinces and kingdoms formed a loose federated
  realm under the High Crown while preserving regional self-rule.
* **The Prophecy of Kings:** Sages enshrined the legend that true peace would only return when a bearer
  achieved the **Stance of Equilibrium**, harmonizing the Shield and Sword without allowing wrath to
  dissipate the light.
* **Usuru's Descent:** Refusing reconciliation, Usuru retreated into the deep subterranean abyss,
  where his resentment festered over three millennia until his name was forgotten, remembered only as
  **The Dark Lord**.

== 6. The 3,000-Year Chasm & The Dynastic Wave

Between the Great War and the modern campaign lies a three-thousand-year chasm ($3,000\text{ years}$),
spanning between **30 and 50 monarchs** (averaging $60\text{ to }100\text{ years}$ per reign).

The dynastic progression oscillates across three cyclical phases:

1. **Long Stasis Crests (60–90+ Years):** In eras of high stability, the First King's self-running
   institutions, Academy restorative arts, and the preserving aura of the Shield of Lux allowed monarchs
   to reign for nearly a century in peaceful continuity.
2. **Tether-Sickness Troughs (5–20 Years):** Succession clusters triggered when the dormant planar wound
   beneath the High Throne flared up, afflicting sovereigns with premature wasting, paranoia, and sudden abdications.
3. **Bureaucratic Consolidations (35–50 Years):** Standard generational transitions managed smoothly by the
   Royal Court and hereditary Noble Houses.

=== 6.2. Five Axioms of Dynastic Continuity

Why does the narrative require an established 3,000-year dynastic order rather than constant civil anarchy?

1. **Embodying the Cosmological Shadow of Lux:** Unbroken continuity proves Sacyra succumbed to the negative
   extreme of Lux—calcifying stasis—transforming a golden peace into a rigid, bureaucratic museum.
2. **Exposing Frontier Neglect as Systemic:** Because the Capital enjoyed millennia of comfort, the decay of
   border settlements like **Dio Village** is exposed not as temporary wartime fallout, but as cold,
   institutional apathy mirroring ancient Libira.
3. **Isolating "The King’s Curse":** In a stable, legally sacrosanct monarchy free of routine coups, the
   periodic madness afflicting sovereigns stands out as a haunting metaphysical anomaly.
4. **Validating the Ro-enheart Lineage:** Five unbroken generations of Ro-enheart leadership at the head of
   the Royal Guard holds sacred weight only if the crown they defended felt ancient, honorable, and permanent.
5. **Magnifying King Jihad’s Rupture:** An established 3,000-year legacy transforms **King Jihad** (`D. Jihad`)
   from a common bad ruler into an existential traitor gambling away three millennia of peace for void supremacy.
